### Added

- **Event crew** (`docs/CREW.md`), ported from Yuyu and extended for Space Works. Organisers keep an event's organising team in **Guests → Crew** (`MANAGE_REGISTRATIONS`): name, email, food preference with a catering summary, role, bio, photo, LinkedIn; add, edit, reorder, show or hide, remove. Each member gets a free confirmed crew ticket (never an order) by a new transactional `crew-ticket` email with the designed ticket; crew never count against capacity or seat holds, are refused by checkout, transfers, self-cancel, the Guests · People actions, teams and projects, and a bought ticket is never converted. A crew sign-up link (`/crew/:token`) adds members hidden until reviewed, without revealing who is registered. An optional **Crew** section on the event page. Crew badges, check-in details, the attendee CSV, `crew` in webhook registrations, a chat notice for link sign-ups, and crew expense claims (`CREW`, behind *Let crew claim expenses*).

Tickets show the design above the live check-in stub, download as PNG or PDF (`/api/ticket/:token/design`), and the PDF is attached to confirmation emails. - **Event finance** (`docs/FINANCE.md`): a new **Finance** tab on the event dashboard. Members and co-organisers file **expense claims** (category, details, amount, up to three receipt photos stored privately and served only to owners, admins and the claimant at `/api/claims/receipts/:id`), for themselves or on behalf of a speaker or volunteer; **participants** claim from their ticket page when the event allows it, up to an optional cap each. Owners and admins approve in full or in part, reject with a reason, record payment in the append-only `ClaimPayout` ledger and reverse mistakes. **Refund requests** from the order page are refunded in full or in part (optionally cancelling tickets) or declined with a reason. The tab sums income, refunds, expenses and prize money paid against an optional budget, with a CSV of every money movement (`/api/exports/events/:id/finance`) and a Spending part in the PDF report. New chat event *Expense claims*; emails to claimants, buyers and owners. Cores in `lib/claims/`, `lib/refundRequests.ts`, `lib/finance.ts`.

- **Paid team registration.** Team events can sell tickets: **Tickets → Who pays for teams** picks every member (default) or the leader once per team. Team places are paid up front: a leader's new team is held as `PENDING_PAYMENT` (and takes a unit) while they pay, a member paying to join waits outside the team until the money lands, and the place is then confirmed or, on approval events, awaits approval. Paid team places never wait on the waitlist; a late payment whose place is gone is refunded. Rejecting a team refunds its members' paid orders in full; cancelling a per-team leader's order cancels the whole team. Leaders can't remove paid members, disband or be deleted with paid members: paid tickets are cancelled as orders. The join link sells the ticket on per-member events. Core in `lib/payments/teamOrders.ts`; the teams-and-tickets guard is gone (group tickets stay off team events).

### Security

- Submission links are random 256-bit tokens stored only as SHA-256 hashes, emailed only to the registration's own address (at most once a minute), and retired when replaced; the outbox deletes the message after delivery. The ticket QR alone never opens a submission.
- `JUDGE` never satisfies any other event permission check. Judges see project content only, never entrant emails, answers, or tickets, and never an entry their own account is registered on. Disqualification reasons and judge comments stay organiser-only; scores and comments are never public.
- Entrant, judge, and organiser ids are re-read with their event and organisation inside each write transaction, and the submission window is compared with server time there.
- A registration without a team on an event that registers teams only is refused with the code `TEAM_REQUIRED`.
- Team changes send organisation webhooks like individual RSVPs: one `registration.status_changed` per member for whole-team approve, reject and promote, automatic team promotion, moves into a team, leader removals and disbanding (`CANCELLED`), organiser team deletion (`REMOVED`) and its undo, and `registration.created` when a leader adds a member. Undoing a team deletion (or a last-member deletion) restores the members' custom check-in list records, like the single-RSVP undo.

### Fixed

- **Large events stay fast** ([Large-event performance](docs/PERFORMANCE.md)): migration `20261108000000_large_event_indexes` adds six composite indexes for the keyset-paged People, Teams, Submissions, judge workspace, Recent check-ins and Invites lists (`CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS` only); judging Results, Progress and auto-assignment read one aggregated row per judge and project instead of every score; the offline check-in roster is read set-based; gallery search no longer scans the whole instance; and every CSV export (People, emails, Teams, Judging, Feedback, orders, Finance) streams one batch at a time as the download asks for it.
- **Dashboard pages return real 404s and redirects.** The dashboard-wide, organisation-wide and event-wide `loading.tsx` screens wrapped every page below them, so a refusal streamed a 200 with the not-found page or a client-side redirect. Loading screens are now scoped to one page each and sit below a guard layout that runs the page's access check first (`lib/views/dashboardGuards.ts`), covering the organisation and event overviews, check-in and badges, schedule, members, series, every settings page, webhooks, member IDs and ticket designs.
- Guests · People chip counts always cover the whole event, whatever the search or dropdowns; the "Showing x of y" label and the export buttons follow a separate count of the matching registrations.
- **Event dashboard tabs fail on their own.** A tab that fails to load (for example a dropped connection while it streams in) shows an error with **Try again** inside that tab instead of taking down the dashboard, and is reported as "Dashboard section crashed in the browser". **Try again** on the page and app error screens now re-fetches instead of re-rendering the failed payload, and the Teams and Invites lists show an error instead of loading forever when a request throws.
- **Speaker and sponsor photos.** A failed upload no longer freezes the form: files over 5 MB are refused in the browser before the upload, the form unlocks whatever happens, and the error shows as a toast next to the form. Event page images (cover, page logo, speaker photos, sponsor logos) have their own rate-limit bucket, `eventPageUpload` (60 an hour), so the shared `upload` bucket for certificates, member IDs, ticket designs, organisation logos and profile photos stays at 12 an hour.
- `db:restore` accepts `--via-local-tunnel` for a loopback tunnel to the production server and prints a clear reason when it refuses a target.
- **Raw SQL timestamps** are written and compared as UTC wall-clock `timestamp(3)` values (`lib/sqlTimestamp.ts`): judge scores and reviews, the webhook delivery claim, series-date payment locks, processed payment webhooks, and every keyset cursor, so a database whose `TimeZone` is not UTC no longer shifts them.
- Ops alerts no longer report ordinary sign-in outcomes (wrong password, the authenticator or new-device code step, unverified email, too many attempts, missing CSRF token, declined or expired sign-in) as "Sign-in failed" (`lib/authLogger.ts`).
- Publishing an event locks its row, so concurrent publishes emit `event.published` and audit `EVENT_PUBLISHED` once.
- An auto-disabled webhook endpoint keeps each queued delivery's own last error (`Endpoint disabled after: HTTP 503`).
- Tables position their scroll box, so screen-reader-only text cannot widen the page; Undo check-in and Invite buttons name the person; the registration dialog says "Join the waitlist" when the event is full; the feedback form explains an email without a confirmed registration; the venue station has a page gutter.
- Helm: `updateStrategy` makes the web Deployment's upgrade strategy configurable (Recreate for a release that must not overlap the previous one).
- **Dashboard memory.** Date, time-zone and money formatting reuse cached `Intl` formatters (`lib/format/intlCache.ts`) instead of building one per registration or order, so a large event no longer allocates native memory per row on every dashboard, analytics, revenue or report render.
- **New event dialog on the event's clock.** The suggested start (a whole hour at least 30 minutes away), the earliest allowed start, the end that follows the start, the Next check and Create all use the selected event timezone, never the browser's; the timezone picker is controlled, focus moves into each step and returns to the button on close.
- `/discover` no longer scrolls sideways on a phone when an event has a long unbroken title, tag or place.
- Saving an event without touching its times keeps the stored start and end (the form sends whole minutes), so guests no longer get a spurious "the organiser changed the time" email.
- **Private pages.** Signed-in areas, `/login`, invitations, `/reset-password`, `/verify-email`, `/verify-mfa`, order links and member ID verification, and any request with the session cookie are sent `private, no-store`; order, verification, invitation, reset and email-verification links send no referrer.
- **Redis outages** no longer stall requests: request-path Redis commands have a 500 ms deadline, and the rate limiter answers at once while the client reconnects (still failing closed in production).
- **Event times on the event's clock.** Creating or editing an event, creating a series, and adding a series schedule item read wall-clock times in the event's or series' timezone on the server, never the server's clock, and refuse a time that a daylight-saving change skips.
- Handled unique-constraint errors (a duplicate registration) are no longer written to the server log; other Prisma errors are logged on one line.
- The venue station's "sign in again" message has a **Sign in again** button.
- Schema drift: migration `20261107020000_drop_orphan_event_session_type` drops the unused `EventSessionType` enum; `schema.prisma` now matches the descending RSVP attendance index and no longer declares a duplicate feedback-form index. CI fails when the migrated database differs from the schema.
- **Outbox draining.** A delivery pass no longer stops early when a batch contained messages dropped on purpose (a recipient who no longer matches, a link that no longer works); it drains until nothing is due or 40 seconds have passed, and backs off only when a batch failed without a single send. Transactional email is claimed before broadcasts, invitations and results emails.
- The event dashboard no longer renders a streamed panel twice. next-auth's `<SessionProvider>` fetched the session after the first client render and changed its context value while the page's `loading.tsx` Suspense boundary was still waiting for React's batched reveal; React then gave up hydrating that boundary and rendered the panel again on the client, leaving the server copy in the hidden `div#S:1` streaming container (duplicate ids such as `#guest-search`, and in a background tab until it was shown). Client components now read the session from a small store (`components/auth/useAuthSession.ts`) whose updates re-render only its readers, and the provider is gone; `tests/e2e/filtered-exports.spec.ts` asserts a single `#guest-search` from the first byte.

### Added

- **Custom check-in lists.** Organisers add lists beyond venue entry ("Swag collection", "Lunch – Day 1", "Workshop B") from **Manage lists** on the Check-in tab or page (owners/admins and `EDIT_DETAILS` co-organisers), and anyone who can check people in (`CHECK_IN`) switches the desk from **Entry** to a list, then scans, types a code or searches to mark guests, with undo, "already checked in to <list> at <time>" feedback, collected / eligible counts and a collected / not collected guest view. Same eligibility and Override as entry, an optional "only after entry check-in" rule, read-only archived lists, a `Check-in: <list>` column per list in the attendee CSV, and list chips in the Guests list. Entry check-in, the live ticket and the entry history are unchanged. New Server Actions in `app/actions/checkin-lists.ts` over `lib/core/checkinLists.ts`; migration `20261103000000_check_in_lists` is additive. Standalone events only for now.
- **Organisation webhooks.** Owners and admins can send their organisation's events to their own systems from **Settings → Webhooks**: up to 10 endpoints, each with subscribed event types (`registration.created`, `registration.status_changed`, `checkin.created`, `checkin.undone`, `event.published`, `event.cancelled`, `feedback.submitted`, plus a `ping` test), a `whsec_` signing secret shown once and stored encrypted, Rotate secret, Send test, Enable/Disable, Delete, and a delivery log with Redeliver. Requests carry `x-spaceworks-signature` (HMAC-SHA256 over `<t>.<body>`), minimal DTOs without emails, answers or tokens, retry with jittered backoff (8 attempts over about a day) from the existing outbox worker, and an endpoint is switched off after 20 consecutive failures. Targets are SSRF-restricted (public https on 443/8443, every resolved address checked at save and delivery time, pinned connection, no redirects).
