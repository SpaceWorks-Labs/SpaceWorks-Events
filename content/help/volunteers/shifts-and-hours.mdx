Volunteer shifts help you plan who covers which job and when, and keep a fair record of the time your crew gives. Your crew sign up from their crew ticket, you approve them, the desk checks them in and out of each shift, and an owner or admin verifies the hours afterwards.

Shifts are for people on the event crew. Add the people first in **Guests** → **Crew**. Anyone on the crew can sign up, whatever kind you gave them: the kind (Organiser, Volunteer, Mentor or Judge) is only a label for badges and filters, and never lets anyone do more.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with **Manage registrations**, run shifts and the attendance desk. Only owners and admins verify or adjust hours and issue certificates.

</Callout>

## Turn on shifts

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose **Guests** → **Crew**.
2. Choose **Shifts** above the crew list.
3. Turn on **Volunteer shifts**.

</Steps>

Until shifts are on, crew tickets don't show any shifts. You can add shifts before you turn them on.

## Add a shift

<Steps>

1. In **Shifts**, choose **Add shift**.
2. Give it a **Title**, and optionally a **Zone** (where) and a **Task** (what).
3. Set when it **Starts** and **Ends**, in the event's timezone. A shift can be up to 12 hours; split longer duties into several shifts.
4. Set when **Sign-ups close**. It must be at or before the start; leave it empty to close at the start.
5. Set the **Places**. 0 means no limit.
6. Set the **Check-in grace**: how many minutes before the start volunteers can check in, and after the end they can still check out. Up to 60.
7. Choose whether **Organisers approve each sign-up**, and whether to **Keep a waitlist when full**.
8. Choose **Add shift**.

</Steps>

You can't lower the places below the number of people who already have one. Once anyone has checked in, the shift's times can't change.

## Sign-ups and the waitlist

Each sign-up shows its state:

- **Waiting for approval**: the shift needs approval. Choose **Approve** or **Decline**.
- **Has a place**: approved. The volunteer gets an email.
- **Waitlisted**: the shift was full. When a place opens up, the first person on the waitlist who is still free gets it and an email.
- **Withdrew** or **Declined**: no longer on the shift.

A volunteer can't hold places on two shifts at the same time. If approving would overlap another shift they already have, the app refuses, and the waitlist skips people who are now busy elsewhere. Back-to-back shifts are fine.

Choose **Cancel place** to take someone off a shift before it starts. They get an email, and the place goes to the waitlist. **Close** a shift (the lock button) to stop new sign-ups; reopening it offers free places to the waitlist again. **Delete** works only while nobody has checked in; volunteers with a place or on the waitlist are emailed that it is cancelled.

## The attendance desk

Shift attendance is separate from the event check-in. Checking someone into a shift doesn't check them into the event, and the other way round.

Volunteers can check themselves in and out on their crew ticket. At the desk, use the buttons on each volunteer:

- **Check in**: from the grace period before the start until the end.
- **Check out**: until the grace period after the end. Enter any **Break minutes**; they are taken off the time.
- **No-show**: after the shift ends, for someone who never checked in.

If someone forgot, change the **Time** to when it really happened and give a reason. The reason is kept with the attendance record and can't be edited. A person can only be on one shift at a time, and two shifts' recorded time can never overlap.

## Verify hours

When a volunteer checks out, their time appears under **Hours** as **To verify**. The minutes are the time between check-in and check-out, less breaks.

<Steps>

1. Under **Hours**, find the volunteer.
2. Choose **Verify**.

</Steps>

Verified hours can be corrected with **Adjust**: enter whole minutes (up to 1,440) and a reason. Every verification and adjustment is kept and can't be changed or deleted.

<Callout kind="note">

You can never verify or adjust your own hours, even as an owner. Another owner or admin has to. This keeps the record fair when organisers volunteer too.

</Callout>

Changing someone's hours revokes their certificate if they have one, because it would no longer match. Issue a new one afterwards: see [Contributor certificates](/help/volunteers/contributor-certificates).

## Hours, claims and the CSV

Hours are a record of time given, never money. **Contributors and certificates** lists each person's verified hours beside the number of expense claims they made from their crew ticket; you decide and pay claims in **Finance**.

Choose **Download hours CSV** for one row per sign-up with the shift, attendance times, breaks, minutes and whether they are verified. It has names but no email addresses.

## Good to know

- Volunteers can withdraw from a shift until they check in. After that, ask them to check out instead.
- Emails go out when a place is confirmed, when someone comes off the waitlist, when you cancel a place or delete a shift, and when a certificate is issued.
- See [Signing up for shifts](/help/volunteers/my-shifts) for what your crew see.
