A contributor certificate thanks someone on your crew for their work. It prints their name, their role and the hours they gave, and they download it from their crew ticket. It is separate from the attendee certificate people get after the feedback form.

<Callout kind="permission">

Only organisation owners and admins can design, issue and revoke contributor certificates. Nobody can issue a certificate to themselves.

</Callout>

## Who can get one

A volunteer needs **verified hours** above zero: pending hours don't count, and neither does a role on its own. Mentors on the crew can be issued a certificate without shift hours. See [Volunteer shifts and hours](/help/volunteers/shifts-and-hours#verify-hours) for verifying hours.

## Issue a certificate

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose **Guests** → **Crew** → **Shifts**.
2. Under **Contributors and certificates**, find the person.
3. Choose **Issue certificate**.

</Steps>

They get an email, and **Download your certificate** appears on their crew ticket. The certificate keeps the role, hours and design it was issued with. Each person has at most one certificate at a time.

## When hours change

If you verify more hours or adjust someone's hours after their certificate was issued, the certificate is revoked automatically, so a download never shows hours that are out of date. Choose **Issue certificate** again to give them a new one with the current hours.

To take a certificate back for another reason, choose **Revoke**. The download link stops working.

## Design the certificate

Without a design, certificates use a standard Space Works Events layout: "Certificate of contribution" with the person's name, role, hours and the event.

<Steps>

1. Under **Contributors and certificates**, choose **Design certificate**.
2. Upload a background, then add text and image layers, just like the attendee certificate.
3. In a text layer, insert **Contributor role** and **Hours contributed** where the role ("Volunteer · Registration desk") and the hours ("6.5 hours") should go.
4. Choose **Save design**.

</Steps>

Certificates you issue after saving use the new design. Certificates already issued keep the design they had.

## Good to know

- The role printed is the person's crew kind and their role, for example "Mentor · Workshops", or just the kind when there's no role.
- Removing someone from the crew removes their shifts, hours and certificate.
- See [Signing up for shifts](/help/volunteers/my-shifts) for what volunteers see.
