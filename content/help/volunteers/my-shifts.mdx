If you're on an event's crew and the organisers run volunteer shifts, your crew ticket has a **My shifts** section. Use it to sign up for shifts, check in and out when you're on duty, and see the time you've given.

## Find My shifts

Open the crew ticket the organisers emailed you ("You're on the crew for …"). If your ticket belongs to an account, sign in to that account first. **My shifts** appears below your ticket once the organisers turn shifts on. If you don't see it, ask the organisers.

## Sign up for a shift

<Steps>

1. Under **Open shifts**, find a shift. Each shows its time, where it is, the task and how many places are left.
2. Choose **Sign up**.

</Steps>

What happens next depends on the shift:

- **You have a place**: you're on it. You also get an email.
- **Waiting for approval**: the organisers confirm each sign-up. You get an email when they approve you.
- **On the waitlist**: the shift is full. If a place opens up before the shift starts, the first person on the waitlist gets it, and you get an email if that's you.

You can't hold places on two shifts that overlap. Sign-ups close at the time the organisers set, at the latest when the shift starts.

## Check in and out

<Steps>

1. When you arrive for your shift, open your crew ticket and choose **Check in**. It opens a little before the shift starts.
2. When you finish, choose **Check out**.

</Steps>

You can only be checked in to one shift at a time. If you forget to check out before the check-out window closes, ask the desk to record your time. Checking in to a shift is separate from the event check-in with your QR code.

## Your hours

After you check out, your time shows as waiting for verification. An organiser checks it, and it then shows as verified. The total at the top of **My shifts** is your verified time. Your hours are a thank-you record, not pay.

If you also spend money for the event, use **Claim expenses** on the same ticket, when the organisers allow it. See [Claiming expenses](/help/attending/claiming-expenses).

## Withdraw from a shift

Choose **Withdraw** next to a shift you no longer want, any time before you check in. Your place goes to the next person on the waitlist. You can sign up again later if there's room, unless the organisers declined you for that shift.

## Your certificate

When the organisers issue you a contributor certificate, you get an email and **Download your certificate** appears in **My shifts**. It shows your role and your verified hours. If your hours change later, the old certificate stops working until the organisers issue a new one.
