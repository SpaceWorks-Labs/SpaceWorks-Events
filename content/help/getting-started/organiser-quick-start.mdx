This walkthrough takes you from a brand-new account to a published event with guests checked in at the door. Each step links to a fuller article when you want more detail.

## 1. Create your account

<Steps>

1. Choose **Sign in** in the site header and open the **Create account** tab.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email** and a **Password** of at least 12 characters, then choose **Create account**.
3. Open the verification email, follow its link and choose **Confirm email**.
4. Sign in with your email and password.

</Steps>

You must confirm your email before you can create events. See [Creating an account and signing in](/help/account/sign-up-and-sign-in).

## 2. Create your organisation

Everything you run belongs to an organisation. Your dashboard shows a short **Get set up** checklist that starts here.

<Steps>

1. On your dashboard, choose **Create organisation**.
2. Enter a **Name** and a **Web address**. The web address becomes your organisation's public page, so keep it short: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes.
3. Optionally add a **Description** and a **Logo**, then choose **Create organisation**.

</Steps>

You become the organisation's owner. See [Creating an organisation](/help/organisations/create).

## 3. Set up your authenticator app

Everyone who helps run an organisation must use an authenticator app, so the next time you open your dashboard you're sent to **Account settings** → **Security** first.

<Steps>

1. In **Two-step verification**, choose **Set up authenticator** and scan the QR code with an app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
2. Enter the six-digit code from the app and choose **Verify and enable**.
3. Save the recovery codes somewhere safe, then choose **I saved them — sign in again**.
4. Sign in with your password and a code from the app.

</Steps>

From now on, each sign-in asks for a code before your dashboards open. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).

## 4. Create the event

<Steps>

1. In your organisation's dashboard, choose **New event**.
2. **Basics**: give the event a **Title** and fill in **About the event**. Tags are optional.
3. **Schedule**: set the **Start**, **End** and **Timezone**. The start can't be in the past.
4. **Location & sign-ups**: enter the venue in **Location**, or turn on **Online event** and paste the meeting link.
5. Choose **Create**.

</Steps>

The event starts as a **Draft** and its event dashboard opens. Nobody else can see it yet, so take your time. See [Creating an event](/help/events/create).

## 5. Make it look right

Open the **Event page** tab:

- **Details** holds the cover image, schedule, location and the **Publishing** settings.
- **Page & programme** builds the public page: highlights, sessions, speakers, sponsors and FAQs.
- **Registration form** adds questions beyond name and email.

In the **Settings** tab, **Ticket design** lets you give tickets your own look; see [The ticket designer](/help/check-in/ticket-designer). The **Overview** tab shows a readiness checklist, such as "No cover image" or "No programme yet", with a shortcut to fix each item.

## 6. Decide whether it's free or paid

An event without tickets is free: anyone can register. To charge, your server's super admin must have turned paid events on, and your organisation's owner connects Razorpay or Stripe under **Settings** → **Payments**. Then add tickets in the event's **Settings** tab under **Tickets**. See [Turning on paid events](/help/payments/enable-paid-events) and [Ticket types, group tickets and add-ons](/help/payments/ticket-types).

## 7. Publish and share

New events are open to everyone with unlimited places. To change that, open **Event page** → **Details** → **Publishing**. You can make the event findable only by link or by invitation, turn on **Approve each sign-up**, and **Limit the number of spots**. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).

<Steps>

1. On the **Overview** tab, find "Not published yet" in the checklist and choose **Publish event**. You can also choose **Live** under **Who can see this event?** in the Publishing section and save.
2. Copy the event's address and share it. You can also create a short link from the **Overview** tab.

</Steps>

<Callout kind="permission">

Only organisation owners and admins can create and publish events.

</Callout>

## 8. Look after your guests

As people register, they appear in the **Guests** tab. From there you can approve or decline sign-ups, give waitlisted people a place, add guests by hand, and export the list. The **Emails** tab sends reminders and updates. Every guest with a confirmed place gets an email with a link to their ticket.

- [The guest list](/help/guests/guest-list)
- [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests)
- [The Finance tab and budget](/help/finance/overview-and-budget), for expenses, refunds and the budget

## 9. Check people in

<Steps>

1. On the day, open the **Check-in** tab and choose **Open check-in** on the phone, tablet or laptop you'll use at the door.
2. Scan each guest's QR code, type their ticket code, or search by name.
3. If the venue Wi-Fi is unreliable, choose **Download guest list for offline** beforehand so the desk keeps working without a connection.

</Steps>

Volunteers without an account can use the PIN-locked [door station](/help/check-in/door-station). See [The check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk).

## 10. Follow up

After the event, open a [feedback form](/help/after/feedback), optionally with [certificates](/help/after/certificates), and see how it went in [Analytics](/help/after/analytics). Owners and admins can download a [post-event report](/help/after/event-report) once the event has ended. Running a club with regular members? You can also issue them [member IDs](/help/organisations/member-ids).
