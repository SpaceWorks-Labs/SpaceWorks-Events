Space Works Events uses the same words across the app, its emails and this guide. If a screen uses a word you don't recognise, look for it here.

## Organisations and people

- **Organisation**: the group that runs events, such as your club or meetup. Every event and series belongs to one. See [Creating an organisation](/help/organisations/create).
- **Member**: someone who belongs to an organisation. Each member has one role:

| Role | In short |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can do, plus decisions kept for the owner: changing roles, connecting payments, the organisation's email settings, API access, and deleting the organisation. |
| **Admin** | Creates, edits, publishes and runs the organisation's events. |
| **Member** | Can look around the organisation's event dashboards, and changes an event only with co-organiser permissions on it. |

- **Co-organiser**: someone given exact permissions on one event or series, such as checking people in or judging, without joining the organisation. See [Event co-organisers](/help/organisations/co-organisers).
- **Staff**: anyone with an organisation role or co-organiser permission. Staff must use an [authenticator app](/help/account/two-step-verification).
- **Guest** or **attendee**: anyone who registers for an event. Guests don't need an account.

[Roles and permissions](/help/organisations/roles) has the full detail.

## Events

- **Event**: a single gathering with a date, a place or online link, and its own guest list.
- **Recurring series**: a repeating event, such as a weekly meetup. Each date is an **occurrence** with its own guest list. See [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
- **Event page**: the public page for an event, with its details, programme and the registration button. The **event dashboard** is where organisers run it.
- **Draft**, **Live** and **Offline**: an event's publishing state. Only Live events are public and open for sign-ups. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).
- **Short link**: an optional short address for an event that only works while it's Live.

## Registration

- **Registration** or **sign-up**: one person's place at an event or occurrence. Each registration has a status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** (organisers see **Going**) | The person has a place and a ticket they can use at the door. |
| **Waitlisted** (organisers see **Waitlist**) | The event was full. They can move up if a place frees up. |
| **Waiting for approval** (organisers see **To approve**) | The organisers review each sign-up before confirming it. |
| **Payment pending** (organisers see **Unpaid**) | A paid place is held while the person pays. |
| **Declined** | The organisers turned the registration down. |
| **Checked in** | The guest arrived and was checked in at the door. |

- **Capacity** or **spots**: the most places an event allows. When it's full, new sign-ups join the waitlist. See [Capacity, waitlists and approvals](/help/registration/capacity-and-waitlist).
- **Registration form**: the questions people answer when they register. Name and email are always asked. See [The registration form](/help/registration/form).
- **Invite-only**: an event only the people on its invite list can open. See [Invite-only events](/help/registration/invitations).

## Paid events

- **Ticket type**: a ticket people can buy or claim, with a price, a number available and a sales window. An event without ticket types is free. See [Ticket types, group tickets and add-ons](/help/payments/ticket-types).
- **Extra**: something bought with a ticket, such as lunch or a T-shirt.
- **Promo code** and **access code**: a discount, and a code that unlocks a hidden ticket. See [Promo and access codes](/help/payments/promo-and-access-codes).
- **Pass**: one purchase that covers every remaining date of a series. See [Passes](/help/payments/passes).
- **Order**: one purchase, with a private order page, a receipt and a ticket for each person. See [Your order and receipt](/help/attending/orders-and-receipts).
- **Hold**: places kept for a buyer while they pay: 30 minutes at checkout, or 24 hours with a payment link sent after an approval or from the waitlist.
- **Refund policy**: what a buyer can get back if they cancel, shown before they pay. See [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).

## On the day

- **Ticket**: a guest's personal page with a QR code, linked from their confirmation email. Organisers can design its look in the [ticket designer](/help/check-in/ticket-designer). See [Your ticket](/help/attending/your-ticket).
- **Check-in**: marking a guest as arrived, by scanning their QR code, typing their ticket code or searching their name. See [The check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk).
- **Door station**: a PIN-locked check-in page for volunteers without an account. See [The door station](/help/check-in/door-station).
- **Check-in list**: an extra list alongside entry, for things like swag or meals. See [Custom check-in lists](/help/check-in/check-in-lists).

## Teams and hackathons

- **Team event**: an event where people register in teams. Each team takes **one place**, however many members it has.
- **Team leader**: the person who created the team. They manage it from a private **team page** and share a **join link** with teammates. See [Registering as a team](/help/attending/teams).
- **Entrant** and **project**: in a hackathon, an entrant is a team or one person taking part alone. Each entrant can submit one project.
- **Judge**, **rubric** and **award**: judges score projects against the rubric, and organisers give awards from the results. See [Running a hackathon](/help/hackathons/overview).

## Money and members

- **Finance tab**: an event's money in, money out and budget. See [The Finance tab and budget](/help/finance/overview-and-budget).
- **Expense claim**: a request to be paid back for money spent on an event. See [Expense claims and reimbursements](/help/finance/expense-claims).
- **Refund request**: a buyer asking for money back beyond the refund policy. See [Refund requests](/help/finance/refund-requests).
- **Member ID**: an ID card an organisation issues to its members, with a QR code that shows whether it's genuine. See [Member IDs](/help/organisations/member-ids).

## After the event

- **Feedback form**: a short survey after the event, anonymous unless the event offers certificates.
- **Certificate**: a certificate of participation guests download after giving feedback. See [Feedback and certificates](/help/attending/feedback-and-certificates).
