Member IDs give the people in your community a digital ID card with their photo, an ID number and a QR code anyone can scan to check it's genuine. You run them as **programmes**: one per kind of card, such as members, staff or volunteers. Each programme has its own join form, card design, approval rules and expiry.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins create programmes, approve members and issue cards. Members of your organisation can view programmes and their member lists (without email addresses). Co-organisers can't see Member IDs.

</Callout>

## Create a programme

<Steps>

1. Choose **Member IDs** in the sidebar.
2. Under **New programme**, enter a **Programme name**, such as "Community members 2026". It's shown on the join form and the back of the card.
3. Pick a **Starting design**: **Community**, **Corporate**, **Event pass** or **Student**. You can change everything later.
4. Choose **Create programme**. The programme opens on its **Card design** tab.
5. Design the card, then open **Settings**, set the **Status** to **Active**, turn on **Join form open**, and choose **Save settings**.

</Steps>

A programme has four tabs: **Members**, **Import**, **Card design** and **Settings**.

## Design the card

The **Card design** tab shows a live preview with sample details and saves as you go. Click an item to edit it; drag it or use the arrow keys to move it. Switch sides with **Card side** (turn off **Print a back** for a single-sided card), choose **Portrait** or **Landscape**, the colours, the photo shape and a watermark, and show or hide items such as the **ID number**, **Valid until** and the **Check QR code**. Cards are standard ID card size (85.6 × 54 mm).

## Settings and join form questions

On the **Settings** tab:

- **Status**: **Draft** (only your team can see it), **Active** (can take requests) or **Closed** (no new requests; issued cards keep working until they expire).
- **Join form open** and **Approve each request**. With approval off, cards are issued as soon as someone joins.
- **ID number prefix**: 2 to 6 capital letters or digits, giving numbers like `ID-2026-0042`.
- **Cards are valid**: **For a time** (1 to 3,650 days from approval), **Until a date**, or **No expiry**, for cards approved or renewed from then on.

The join form always asks for the name on the card and email, with an optional photo. Under **Join form questions**, choose **Add a question** for up to 12 more, and where each answer **Prints on**: front, back, or not printed.

## Add members

- **The join form.** On the **Members** tab, choose **Copy link** in the **Join link** card and share it. People sign in, fill in the form and choose **Request my card**. Your organisation's public ID page also lists every Active programme with its join form open.
- **By hand.** Choose **Add member**, and tick **Approve and issue the card now** if you like.
- **Import.** On the **Import** tab, choose a CSV file (up to 2 MB and 2,000 rows) or paste a Google Sheets link shared as "Anyone with the link can view". Match columns to Name and Email, check the preview, and import. Emails already in the programme aren't added twice.

## Approve, issue and revoke

Filter the **Members** list by **All**, **Waiting**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Revoked**, and search by name, email or ID number. Choose **Approve** on a waiting request, or select several people and use the bar that appears.

Approving issues the card with the next ID number and a valid-until date from the programme's rule. A member's actions menu then offers **Download PDF**, **Download PNG (front)**, **Email card**, **Renew**, **Edit details and photo**, **Revoke card** and **Delete member**.

- **Reject** and **Revoke** take an optional **Reason**, seen only by your team. A rejected request can still be approved later, and a revoked card can be brought back with **Reinstate**.
- **Renew** restarts validity from today; **Renew all** does it for every approved card.
- **Delete member** removes their card, ID number and photo for good. To stop a card but keep the record, revoke it instead.

## Send and download cards

Approving doesn't email anyone. To send cards, use **Email all cards** at the top of the programme, or **Email cards** for selected people. Each email has the card as a PDF and a link to the holder's ID wallet. A bulk send reaches only members with a valid card who joined themselves or haven't been emailed yet, and nobody is emailed twice in 24 hours. Your organisation can send up to 200 card emails a day (50 a day in its first week).

**All cards (PDF)** downloads every approved card in one file, and **All cards (ZIP)** gives one PDF per member.

## How checking works

Scanning a card's QR code opens a public check page: **Valid card**, **Expired card**, **Revoked card** or **Card not found**. Valid and expired cards show the holder's name, photo, programme, organisation, ID number and valid-until date; a revoked card shows only its status. Waiting and rejected requests, deleted members and unknown codes all show **Card not found**.

## Good to know

- Holders find their cards in **Account** → **Your IDs**. See [Your member ID](/help/attending/member-id).
- **Delete programme** on the **Settings** tab removes every member and card in it; their check pages then say **Card not found**. It can't be undone.
- For name badges at an event rather than membership cards, see [ID cards and badges](/help/check-in/badges).
