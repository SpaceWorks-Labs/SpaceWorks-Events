A co-organiser is someone who helps with one event without joining your organisation. You choose exactly what they can do: a door volunteer might only get **Check-in**, while a partner running sign-ups gets **Registrations**. They see only the events they're invited to, never your other events, members or settings.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation invite, change and remove co-organisers.

</Callout>

## Invite a co-organiser

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Settings** tab.
2. In the **Co-organisers** card, under **Invite someone**, enter their email address.
3. Choose what they can do by turning permissions on or off. **Edit details** is on to start with; pick at least one.
4. Choose **Send invite**.

</Steps>

The app emails them a link to accept. The invite shows in the list as **Invited**, with how long it has left. Invites expire after 7 days.

To accept, the person opens the link, signs in (or creates an account) **with the same email address you invited**, and chooses **Accept invite**. If they sign in with a different address, the invite shows as unavailable.

Co-organisers are organisation staff, so they must set up an authenticator app and enter a code from it before they can open the event's dashboard. The app walks them through it the first time. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).

## The permissions

| Permission | What it lets them do |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit details** | Edit the event page content in **Event page** → **Page & programme**: page settings, highlights, speakers, sponsors, resources, FAQs and venue. Manage ticket types, prices, add-ons, promo codes and the ticket design in **Settings**. Create, rename, reorder and archive custom check-in lists. |
| **Registrations** | Work the guest list in **Guests**: approve, decline, give waitlist places, add, edit and remove guests, and export CSVs. Handle payments on orders: refunds, offline payments, waivers and changes. See the registration numbers, revenue and **Analytics**. |
| **Attendee invites** | Manage the invite list in **Guests** → **Invites**. |
| **Check-in** | Open the check-in desk: scan tickets, search for guests, check people in and out of lists, work offline and print ID cards. |
| **Publish and schedule** | Build the programme: sessions, tracks and live delays. |
| **Judge** | Score hackathon projects in the judging workspace. See [Judges, rubric and scoring](/help/hackathons/judging). |

Permissions are exact: one never includes another. Someone with only **Check-in** can't see the guest list in **Guests**, and someone with only **Registrations** can't open the check-in desk.

<Callout kind="note">

Whatever permissions they have, co-organisers can't edit the **Details** form (title, dates, location, capacity, cover and publishing), make the event live, change the registration form, send emails, manage feedback and certificates, run judging or award prizes, decide expense claims, or download the event report. Despite its name, **Publish and schedule** covers the programme, not making the event live.

</Callout>

A co-organiser whose only permission is **Judge** sees just the judging workspace for that event, and nothing else on the dashboard. Any other co-organiser can also file their own expense claims on the event's **Finance** tab. See [Expense claims and reimbursements](/help/finance/expense-claims).

## Change or remove access

In the **Co-organisers** card, each person who has accepted shows as **Active** with their permissions.

- To change what they can do, choose **Edit**, turn permissions on or off, and choose **Save permissions**.
- To take away their access, choose **Remove**, then **Remove access**. They lose access to the event straight away.
- To withdraw an invite that hasn't been accepted, choose **Cancel invite**, then confirm. The link in their email stops working.

## What co-organisers see

When a co-organiser opens your organisation's dashboard, they see only a list of the events they help with. Each event's dashboard shows the tabs and buttons their permissions allow, with a short note where they don't have permission.

Their co-organiser roles are listed in **Account** → **Organisations** under **Event co-organiser roles**, and invites waiting for them under **Pending invitations**.

## Good to know

- To re-invite someone whose invite expired, send a new invite from the same card.
- If an organisation member also co-organises an event, they get the co-organiser permissions on that event on top of their member role.
- Removing someone from your organisation doesn't remove their co-organiser access. Remove it here as well if needed.
- See [Roles and permissions](/help/organisations/roles) for how co-organisers compare with members and admins.
