Everyone who helps run events on Space Works Events has a role. Organisation members are an **Owner**, an **Admin** or a **Member**, and that role applies to every event in the organisation. **Co-organisers** are different: they help with one event only, with exactly the permissions you give them.

Use this page to decide who should get which role. To invite people or change a role, see [Inviting and managing members](/help/organisations/members).

## Everyone on the team needs an authenticator

Owners, admins, members and co-organisers are all organisation staff, and staff must use an authenticator app. Until someone has set one up and entered a code from it, they can't open the organisation's dashboard or do anything with its events, whatever their role. Guests who only register for events don't need one. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).

## The three organisation roles

### Owner

The person who created the organisation. The owner can do everything an admin can, plus:

- Change other members' roles between Admin and Member.
- Remove admins as well as members.
- Connect the organisation's Razorpay or Stripe account in **Settings** → **Payments**.
- Choose the sender name and reply-to address for guest email, or send through the organisation's own mail server.
- Manage API access for the organisation.
- Delete the organisation.

There is one owner per organisation, and the owner role can't be changed or handed over in the app.

### Admin

Admins run events day to day. They can:

- Create, edit, publish, clone and delete events and recurring series.
- Use every tab of every event dashboard: guests and approvals, the event page, emails, check-in, judging, feedback, analytics, finance and settings.
- Handle money on events: refund orders, decide expense claims and refund requests, set the budget, and award prizes and record prize payouts.
- Invite and manage co-organisers on events.
- Create and revoke member invite links, and remove people with the Member role.
- Edit the organisation's name, description and logo.
- Set up chat notifications, webhooks and the organisation's ticket designs.
- Create member ID programmes, approve members and issue cards.

### Member

Members can see the organisation's dashboard, its events and the team list, but can't change anything. They can open any event's dashboard, where most tabs show a note that they don't have permission. They don't see other people's email addresses. They can view member ID programmes and their member lists, without email addresses, and file their own expense claims on an event.

Give someone the Member role when they should be part of the team but don't need to manage events. If a member needs to help with a particular event, you can also make them a co-organiser of that event.

## Co-organisers

A co-organiser helps with a single event. They don't join your organisation and don't see your other events. You choose exactly what they can do from six permissions, such as **Check-in** for door volunteers or **Registrations** for someone approving sign-ups. See [Event co-organisers](/help/organisations/co-organisers).

## Who can do what

| Task | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the dashboard, events and team | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create, edit, publish and delete events and series | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage guests, emails, check-in and feedback | Yes | Yes | No |
| Refunds, expense claims, budget and prize payouts | Yes | Yes | Own claims only |
| Invite and manage co-organisers | Yes | Yes | No |
| Create member invite links | Yes | Yes | No |
| Remove members | Yes | Members only | No |
| Change roles | Yes | No | No |
| Edit the organisation profile | Yes | Yes | No |
| Member IDs | Yes | Yes | View only |
| Chat notifications and webhooks | Yes | Yes | No |
| Ticket designs | Yes | Yes | No |
| Payment account (Razorpay or Stripe) | Yes | No | No |
| Email sender and mail server | Yes | No | No |
| API access | Yes | No | No |
| Delete the organisation | Yes | No | No |

Co-organisers never reach organisation-level screens such as **Members**, **Member IDs** or **Settings**, whatever permissions they hold.

## Choosing the right role

- **A fellow organiser who runs events with you**: Admin.
- **Someone who wants to follow along**: Member.
- **A volunteer, speaker wrangler or partner helping with one event**: co-organiser on that event, with only the permissions they need.

<Callout kind="tip">

Give people the least access that lets them do their job. You can always add more later, and a co-organiser's permissions can be changed at any time.

</Callout>

## Good to know

- If a button described in this guide is missing for you, your role probably doesn't allow it. Ask an owner or admin.
- Some sensitive changes, such as changing roles, deleting the organisation, changing email settings or connecting a chat, need a sign-in within the last 10 minutes. If the app asks you to sign in again, sign out, sign back in and try again.
- Your roles in every organisation are listed in **Account** → **Organisations**, along with the events you co-organise. You can leave an organisation from there, unless you're its only owner. See [Your data and memberships](/help/account/your-data).
