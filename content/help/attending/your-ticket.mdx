Every registration has its own ticket page. It shows your status, the event details and, once your place is confirmed, the QR code you show at the door. The ticket link is private to you: anyone with it can see your ticket, so don't post it publicly.

## Open your ticket

You can reach your ticket in any of these ways:

- the **View Your Ticket & QR Code** button in your confirmation email
- **View ticket** straight after registering
- the event page in the same browser you registered in, which shows **View your ticket**
- **Account settings** → **Registrations** → **View ticket**, if you registered while signed in
- **View ticket** on your order page, if you bought tickets

## What the ticket shows

The chip at the top shows your status:

| Status | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | "You're in", the event's date, time, venue and your name, and a QR code. |
| **Payment pending** | Your seat is held until the time shown while the order is paid. Choose **Pay now** to finish paying. |
| **Waitlisted** or **Pending approval** | The event details and "A scannable ticket will be available after this registration is confirmed." |
| **Rejected** | The organisers declined this registration. There's no QR code. |

For a paid ticket, the page also shows the ticket type and its price, or **Complimentary** if the organisers gave it to you free. For an in-person event with a map link, choose the venue name to open the map in a new tab. **Event page** at the top takes you back to the event.

If the organisers designed their own tickets, your designed ticket appears above the QR code, with the same QR code on it.

## At the door

Show the QR code on your phone, or a saved or printed copy, to the person checking guests in. They can also paste your ticket link or find you by name.

If your ticket page is open when you're checked in, it updates by itself: the QR code tears away and the page shows **You're checked in** and the time. After that the QR code and designed ticket aren't shown again, because they've been used.

<Callout kind="tip">

Venue Wi-Fi can be slow. Save your ticket before you set off so you can show it without a connection.

</Callout>

## Save it and add it to your calendar

Once your place is confirmed:

- **Save ticket** downloads your ticket, with its QR code, as an image you can keep in your photos or print. Choose **PDF** underneath it for a PDF instead. Both use the organisers' ticket design when they have one.
- **Add to calendar** opens Google Calendar with the event filled in. Your confirmation email also includes a calendar file for Apple Calendar, Outlook and other apps.

When the organisers have designed their tickets, your confirmation email also has the designed ticket attached as a PDF.

A pass for a series has a ticket for each date. The ticket page lists them under **Your other dates**, each with its own QR code.

## Cancel your registration

If you can't make it, cancel so your place can go to someone else.

<Steps>

1. Open your ticket and choose **Can't make it? Cancel registration**.
2. Confirm with **Yes, cancel**.

</Steps>

If you registered while signed in, you can also choose **Cancel registration** next to the event in **Account settings** → **Registrations**.

Cancelling can't be undone. To go after all, register again, if there's still room. Cancelling a request or a waitlist spot withdraws it, and cancelling a ticket that's waiting for payment releases the hold. Nothing is charged either way.

You can't cancel from the ticket page:

- a ticket that's been paid for: cancel from your order page instead, under the event's refund policy (see [Asking for a refund](/help/attending/asking-for-a-refund))
- after you've been checked in
- a registration the organisers declined
- from someone else's account: a registration made while signed in can only be cancelled by that account, so sign in first
- as a team member: leave the team from your ticket instead, which cancels your registration (see [Registering as a team](/help/attending/teams#leave-a-team))

## If you've lost your ticket

- Search your inbox (and spam folder) for the confirmation email from the event.
- Open the event page in the browser you registered with: it may still show **View your ticket**.
- If you registered while signed in, find it in **Account settings** → **Registrations**.
- If you bought tickets, open your order page from the receipt email.
- Otherwise, ask the organisers. They can see your registration and help you at the door.

## Team events, hackathons and expenses

On a team event, your ticket also shows your team, its leader and its members, and lets you leave the team or join one. See [Registering as a team](/help/attending/teams). If the event takes project submissions, your ticket has a **Your project** or **Team project** card. See [Submitting a hackathon project](/help/attending/submitting-a-project). If the organisers pay back costs, your ticket has a **Claim expenses** section. See [Claiming expenses](/help/attending/claiming-expenses).
