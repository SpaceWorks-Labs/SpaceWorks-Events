Hackathons, game jams and competitions often register people in teams. One person creates the team and becomes its **leader**, and teammates join with the team's link. Each team takes one place, however many members it has.

## How team registration works

When you register for a team event, the form asks **How are you taking part?**

- **Create a team**: you lead the team and get a link to invite teammates.
- **Join with a link**: your leader shares a link that registers you straight into their team.
- **Register solo**: take part on your own, when the organisers allow it.

A team below the minimum size is marked **Incomplete** and can't be approved until it grows.

Everyone in a team shares its status: the whole team waits for approval or a place together, and every member is confirmed at once, each with their own ticket by email.

## Create a team

<Steps>

1. On the event page, choose **Register your team** (or **Register a team or yourself**).
2. Choose **Create a team** and enter a **Team name** (up to 60 characters, different from the other teams' names at this event).
3. Choose a ticket if the event sells them, fill in your details and any questions, then register or continue to payment.
4. Check your email. Alongside your ticket, you get a link to your private **team page**, with the join link to share.

</Steps>

Signed in, you can also reach the team page from **Manage team** on your ticket or in **Account settings** → **Registrations**.

## Paid team events

When a team event sells tickets, the form says who pays:

| The form says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Everyone in a team buys their own ticket." | Each person pays when they create or join a team. |
| "One ticket covers your whole team." | The leader pays once; members then join free. |

Team places are paid up front, never requested first. Nobody can join a new team until its leader has paid, and the team page link is emailed then. A full event takes no new paid places.

If the organisers decline your team, every paid order in it is refunded in full.

## Join a team

Open the join link from your team leader. It shows **You're joining** with the team's name and size, and the usual registration form. Fill it in and register.

**Already registered on your own?** Don't register again. Either:

- open the join link while signed in to the account you registered with, or in the browser you registered in, and choose **Join** followed by the team's name, or
- open your ticket, find **Join a team**, paste the link into **Team link** and choose **Join this team**.

You keep your ticket, and your place follows the team's. You can be in one team per event, and can't join a full or declined team.

## Find a team

On some events, people who register solo can look for a team. When registering, choose **Register solo**, tick **List me as looking for a team**, and add a **Headline** (up to 120 characters) and up to 8 **Skills**.

Your ticket then has a **Looking for a team** card listing **Teams looking for members** (choose **Ask to join**) and **Others looking for a team**. Leaders' invitations appear there with **Join team** and **Decline**. Others see only your first name, last initial, headline and skills. Use **Edit details** to change your profile, or turn off **Show me on the list** to hide it. Nobody is emailed about requests and invitations, so check your ticket now and then.

## Manage your team

Your team page is for leaders. Anyone with its link can manage the team, so keep it private.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Rename the team | **Rename**, enter the new name, then **Save**. |
| Share the join link | **Copy** in **Join link**. **Create a new link…** stops the old one working. |
| Add someone yourself | Under **Add a member**, enter their **Name** and **Email** and choose **Add member**. They're emailed their own ticket. |
| Remove a member | Their **⋯** menu → **Remove from team…**. Their registration is cancelled. |
| Hand over leadership | Their **⋯** menu → **Make leader…**. They're emailed a new team page link and yours stops working. You stay in the team. |
| Find teammates | Turn on **Looking for members**, then **Accept** or **Decline** requests. |
| Secure the page | **Reset management link…**. The new link is emailed to you. |
| Disband the team | **Disband team…**. Everyone's registration is cancelled and the team is deleted. This can't be undone. |

You can't add members yourself on invite-only events (each person signs in with their invited account and uses the join link), once registration has closed, when the team is full, or when everyone buys their own ticket. You can't remove a member who paid, or disband a team with paid members.

A declined team can still be viewed, trimmed or disbanded, but not changed.

## Leave a team

<Steps>

1. Open your ticket and choose **Leave team** in your team's card.
2. Confirm with **Leave team**.

</Steps>

Leaving cancels your registration; you can then register on your own or join another team. If the leader leaves, the member who joined first becomes leader, and a team with nobody left is deleted. You can't leave once checked in. If you paid for your ticket, cancel from your order page instead.

## Good to know

- Recurring series don't use teams.
- Teammates see each other's names on their tickets, never their emails.
- To cancel a paid place, see [Asking for a refund](/help/attending/asking-for-a-refund).
- To submit your team's project, see [Submitting a hackathon project](/help/attending/submitting-a-project).
