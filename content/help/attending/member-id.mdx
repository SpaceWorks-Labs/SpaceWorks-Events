Some organisations issue ID cards to their members, volunteers or students. Your card has your name, an ID number, how long it's valid and a QR code that anyone can scan to check it's genuine. You keep it in your account's ID wallet and can download it to print.

## Request a card

Organisations share a link to their ID programme, or list their open programmes on their ID cards page. You need a Space Works Events account, because your card is kept in your account.

<Steps>

1. Open the link the organisation gave you, and choose **Request your card** if you see a list.
2. If you aren't signed in, choose **Sign in to continue**, then sign in or create an account.
3. Check **Name on the card**. Your **Email** comes from your account and the card is linked to it.
4. Add a **Photo** if you like: a clear, front-facing photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB).
5. Answer any questions the organisation added.
6. Choose **Request my card**.

</Steps>

The form tells you before you send it whether the organisation approves each card first or issues it straight away. You can ask once per programme.

If the page says **Requests are closed**, the organisation isn't taking new requests right now. Organisations can also add you themselves, by hand or from a list, without you asking.

## Open your ID wallet

Your cards live in **Account settings** → **Your IDs**.

<Screenshot name="account-ids-wallet" alt="The Your IDs page with an issued ID card, its Valid status, the Flip to the back button and the PDF, PNG and Check page buttons" />

The wallet shows the cards you requested, and any card an organisation issued to your email address, as long as that address is verified. Cards that aren't issued yet show their status and a link to the programme. If a card you expect is missing, check the email address on your account is verified, or ask the organisation which address they used.

On each card you can:

- tilt it to catch the light (on a phone, choose **Tilt with your phone** if asked)
- choose **Flip to the back** to see the back, when the card has one
- choose **PDF** to download it for printing (front and back, when there is a back)
- choose **PNG** to download it as an image
- choose **Check page** to open its public check page

## Card statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Valid** | Issued and in date. |
| **Waiting for approval** | The organisation hasn't approved your request yet. |
| **Expired** | Genuine, but past its valid-until date. Ask the organisation to renew it. |
| **Not approved** | The organisation didn't approve your request. |
| **Revoked** | The organisation has withdrawn the card. Don't use it. |

## Your card by email

When your card is issued, you may get an email titled "Your ID card from" the organisation's name. It has your card attached as a PDF, your ID number and valid-until date, and a button to **Open your ID wallet**. Sign in with that email address to see the card in your wallet.

## Show and check a card

Show the card on your phone or printed. Anyone can check it by scanning its QR code, which opens a check page showing one of:

- **Valid card**: genuine and in date, with your name, photo, the organisation, the programme, your **ID number** and **Valid until**
- **Expired card**: genuine, but past its valid-until date
- **Revoked card**: withdrawn by the organisation, so not proof of membership
- **Card not found**: the code didn't match a card

The check page shows only what's needed to confirm the card. It never shows your email address or your answers.

<Callout kind="note">

The check link works for anyone who has it, so a printed card or a photo of it can be checked. If you lose a card, tell the organisation: they can revoke it.

</Callout>

## Good to know

- Your name, photo and answers go only to the organisation that issues the card.
- To change your name or photo on a card, ask the organisation.
- Organisers: see [Member IDs](/help/organisations/member-ids).
