Shortly before an event, the organisers may email you to ask whether you're still coming. It helps them plan food, seats and staff, and lets them offer your place to someone else if you can't make it. Answering takes a moment and you don't need an account.

The email is only sent to people with a confirmed place, and only when the organisers choose to ask. Its subject is "Are you still coming to" followed by the event name; a reminder starts with "Reminder:". Organisers can also send you the same link by text or chat.

## Answer the question

<Steps>

1. Open the email and choose **Yes, I'll be there** or **No, I can't make it**.
2. Your answer page opens with that choice picked. It shows the event's date, time and place, and your name and email.
3. If you like, add a **Note for the organisers**, such as "Arriving late". Notes can be up to 500 characters.
4. Choose **Send my answer**.

</Steps>

You'll see "Thanks, see you there!" or "Thanks for letting us know", with your answer and when you sent it. The organisers see your answer and note straight away.

<Callout kind="note">

The link is private to you: anyone with it can answer for you. If the organisers send a reminder or a new link, the earlier one stops working, so use the newest.

</Callout>

## Saying No

What happens when you say No depends on how the organisers set up the event, and on your ticket:

- **Your answer is just recorded.** You keep your place and your ticket. The organisers may follow up with you.
- **Your place is released.** The page warns you first with "Answering No releases your place", and the button changes to **Release my place**. When you send it, your registration is cancelled and your place can go to someone else. You'll see "Your place has been released".
- **A paid ticket you bought on its own order** is cancelled under the event's refund policy, the same as cancelling from your order page. The page tells you how much comes back. A ticket you reserved to pay at the desk is simply cancelled. See [Asking for a refund](/help/attending/asking-for-a-refund).
- **A paid ticket after the refund window has closed** keeps its place. Your No is sent to the organisers with a refund request (including your note), and they decide. If you change your answer to Yes while they haven't decided yet, the request is withdrawn.
- **A paid ticket on an order with several tickets, or one someone else paid for**, is never cancelled from this page. Your answer goes to the organisers, who will sort out the order with whoever paid.

The page only says your place will be released when that is really what will happen.

<Callout kind="warning">

A released place can't be taken back from the answer page. If your plans change, register again from the event page while places are left.

</Callout>

If you've already been checked in at the event, you keep your place whatever you answer.

On a team event, a released place cancels only your own registration, as if you'd left the team. See [Registering as a team](/help/attending/teams).

## Changing your answer

You can change your answer until the event starts. Open the link again, choose **Change my answer**, pick the other option and send it. If you answered No and your place was released, you can't change it: register again instead.

Once the event has started, the page says "Answers are closed" and shows the answer you gave.

## Good to know

- If the event has been cancelled, or your registration is no longer confirmed, the page explains that there's nothing to answer.
- Answering Yes doesn't replace your ticket. Bring your QR code as usual. See [Your ticket](/help/attending/your-ticket).
- You can also cancel from your ticket at any time before you're checked in.
- If you have an account, your answers are included when you [download your data](/help/account/your-data).
