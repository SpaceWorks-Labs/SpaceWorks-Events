Some events choose who comes: when you register, you're applying for a place. This page explains what happens next and how to answer an offer.

## Following your application

After you register you get an email and a ticket page link, just like any registration. Open it any time to see **Your application**:

- **In review**: the organisers are looking at it. It may move through a few stages, such as a shortlist.
- **Offered a place**: you've been offered a place. Answer before the time shown.
- **Waitlisted**: all places are offered for now. If one opens up, places go in the order people joined the waitlist, and you'll get an email.
- **Accepted**: your place is confirmed. Your ticket is ready.
- **Not successful**, **Declined**, **Offer expired** or **Withdrawn**: the application is closed.

Until the first decision about you, you can change your answers from the same page with **Edit answers**.

## Answering an offer

<Steps>

1. Open the link in your offer email, or your ticket page.
2. Choose **Accept** to take the place, or **Decline** to let someone else have it.

</Steps>

For a free place, choosing **Accept** confirms it straight away and your ticket arrives by email.

For a paid place, choose **Accept and pay**. The place is held for you for a day while you pay with the payment link (see [Paying for a ticket](/help/attending/paying-for-tickets)). It's confirmed only when the payment goes through. If you don't finish paying, you can try again until the offer's answer-by time.

On a team application, the team leader answers for everyone and, for a paid place, pays once for the whole team. Team members see the same status on their own ticket pages.

## Withdrawing

Changed your mind before you have a place? Choose **Withdraw** on your ticket page. Once your place is confirmed, cancel your ticket instead.

## Good to know

- An offer you don't answer in time expires and goes to the next person on the waitlist.
- Nothing is charged unless you accept a paid place and pay.
- Reviews and scores are never shown to applicants.
