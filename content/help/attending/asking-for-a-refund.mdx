If you've paid for tickets and can't go, there are two ways to get money back, both from your order page: cancel under the event's refund policy, or ask the organisers for a refund. If the organisers cancel the event, everyone who paid is refunded in full without asking.

Refunds always go back the way you paid. Card and UPI refunds can take 5 to 10 working days to appear; money paid in person is returned by the organisers.

## Cancel within the refund policy

Each paid event has a refund policy, shown before you pay and on your order page, such as "Full refund if you cancel until 2 days before the event." Your order keeps the policy it was bought under, even if the organisers change it later.

While the policy allows it, your order page has a **Can't make it?** section that says what you'd get back.

<Steps>

1. Open your order page from the link in your receipt email.
2. Under **Can't make it?**, choose **Cancel order**.
3. Read the message and confirm with **Cancel order**.

</Steps>

Cancelling gives up every ticket in the order, and they stop working straight away. The refund follows by itself, and you're emailed when it's made. This can't be undone.

You can't cancel online once someone on the order has checked in, after the policy's deadline, or when the policy is "No refunds on cancellation". You can still ask the organisers (below).

<Callout kind="warning">

On a team event where the leader paid once for the whole team, cancelling the leader's order cancels everyone's place in the team. The order page warns you first.

</Callout>

## Ask the organisers for a refund

Use this when the policy doesn't cover you, when you want more back than it gives, or when you'd like money back but want to keep your tickets.

<Steps>

1. Open your order page.
2. Under **Need a refund?**, fill in **Why you're asking** (up to 1,000 characters).
3. Choose **Ask for a refund**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot name="order-refund-request" alt="The Need a refund? section of an order page with the Why you're asking box and the Ask for a refund button" />

The page then says "You asked for a refund. The organisers will reply by email." You can have one request open per order at a time.

## What the organisers decide

The organisation's owners and admins see your request and your reason. They can:

| Decision | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Approve in full | Everything still refundable on the order is refunded. |
| Approve in part | They choose an amount and refund that. |
| Decline | They give a reason, which you see by email and on your order page. |

When approving, they also decide whether your tickets are cancelled or whether you keep them. You're emailed when the refund is made. Your order page shows "Your refund request was approved", with the amount, or "Your last request wasn't approved" with their reason. After a decision, you can ask again if something changes.

<Callout kind="note">

The organisers decide refund requests themselves. Space Works Events isn't part of the sale and can't refund you on their behalf.

</Callout>

## When an event is cancelled

If the organisers cancel an event, or one date of a series:

- Everyone with a place, a request or a waitlist spot is emailed that it's cancelled, with any reason the organisers gave.
- Every paid order is refunded in full and its tickets cancelled. You get a separate email about your refund. A pass is refunded its share for the cancelled date.
- Orders still waiting for payment are released, and requests are closed. Nothing more is charged.

If someone on an order had already checked in, the organisers deal with that order themselves.

## Good to know

- Free tickets and unpaid requests have no refund. Cancel them from your ticket instead: see [Your ticket](/help/attending/your-ticket#cancel-your-registration).
- If a refund doesn't arrive within 10 working days, contact the organisers.
- Organisers: see [Refund requests](/help/finance/refund-requests) and [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).
