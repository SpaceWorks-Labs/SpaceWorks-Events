Registering takes a minute. You can register as a guest with just your name and email, or while signed in to a Space Works Events account. Free events give you a place straight away (or a spot on the waitlist); paid events continue to the organiser's payment page.

## Register for an event

<Steps>

1. Open the event page. The **Join this event** card shows how many people are going and, if places are limited, how many are left.
2. Choose the register button. Its wording tells you what will happen (see below).
3. In the **Register for …** dialog, choose your tickets if the event sells them.
4. Fill in your **Name** and **Email**. If you're signed in, only your name is asked for.
5. Answer any questions the organisers added. Required questions must be answered; the rest are marked **Optional**.
6. Choose **Register**, or the payment button on a paid event.

</Steps>

## As a guest or with an account

| | As a guest | Signed in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you enter | Your name and email. Your ticket is sent to that email. | Only your name, which you can leave blank to use your account name. Your account's email is used. |
| Finding it later | The confirmation email, or the event page in the same browser. | Also **Account settings** → **Registrations**, on any device. |
| Certificates | Download when you send feedback. | Also kept under **Account settings** → **Registrations** → **Certificates**. |
| Invite-only events | Not possible. | Possible with the invited, verified email address. |

Some ticket types are for members of the organisation only. To buy one, sign in with your member account.

See [Creating an account and signing in](/help/account/sign-up-and-sign-in) if you'd like an account.

## What the button means

| Button | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Register — it's free** | You get a place straight away. |
| **Get tickets** | The event sells tickets. You choose them and pay the organiser. |
| **Request to join** | The organisers review each sign-up. You wait for their approval. |
| **Join the waitlist** | The event is full. You join the waitlist and may get a place later. |
| **Registration closed** | Sign-ups have closed. |

Team events use different wording, such as **Register your team**. See [Registering as a team](/help/attending/teams).

## Paid events

On a paid event, the button reads **Continue to payment** with the total. Choosing it holds your place for 30 minutes while you pay on the organiser's secure Razorpay or Stripe page.

If the event needs approval or is full, nothing is charged when you register. The button reads **Request tickets** or **Join the waitlist**, and if a place is yours, you get an email with a link to pay. See [Paying for a ticket](/help/attending/paying-for-tickets).

## After you register

- **Place confirmed**: you see "Registration confirmed." with **Download and save ticket** and **View ticket**. You're also emailed a link to your ticket with its QR code, the event details and a calendar invitation. Save the ticket: you'll show its QR code at the door.
- **Waitlisted or waiting for approval**: you see "Registration received. We'll let you know when its status changes." and **View status**. You're emailed to confirm, and again when your status changes.

The event page remembers your registration in this browser: the card shows **You're in**, **On the waitlist**, **Waiting for approval** or **Payment pending**, and its button opens your ticket.

## Waitlists and approvals

- **Waitlist**: if someone with a place cancels, the first person on the waitlist usually moves up automatically and is emailed their ticket. On a paid event, they're emailed a link to pay instead. Organisers can also give waitlisted people a place by hand. On events that need approval, nobody moves up automatically.
- **Approval**: the organisers accept or decline each request, and you're emailed either way. If the event is full, they can only accept you once a place frees up.

## Invite-only events

Only people on the organisers' invite list can open an invite-only event. To register:

<Steps>

1. [Sign in](/help/account/sign-up-and-sign-in) with the email address your invitation was sent to. That address must be verified.
2. Open the event link and register as usual.

</Steps>

An invitation covers one place, for you. To bring someone else, ask the organisers to invite them too.

## Common messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "This email is already registered for this event." | Someone already registered with that email. Check your inbox for the earlier confirmation. |
| "You have already RSVP’d for this event." | Your account is already registered. Find it in **Account settings** → **Registrations**. |
| "This event is not open for RSVP." | The event has ended, sign-ups have closed, or the organisers took it offline. |
| "This event is invite-only. …" | Sign in with the email address that was invited. |

## Good to know

- You can register once per event or date. For a recurring series, such as a weekly meetup, register for each date you want, or buy a pass where one is sold.
- Your answers go to the organisers of this event only.
- Changed your mind? See [Your ticket](/help/attending/your-ticket#cancel-your-registration).
