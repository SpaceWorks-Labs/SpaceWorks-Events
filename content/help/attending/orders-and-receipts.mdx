Every paid purchase has an order page. It shows what you bought, whether it's paid, your receipt and a ticket for each person. Keep its link: it's how you pay, name attendees, cancel and ask for refunds.

## Open your order page

You reach your order page:

- straight after paying, when the payment page sends you back
- from **View your order** or **Pay for your tickets** in the emails about your order
- from **View your order** in the receipt email, whose subject starts with "Receipt" and your order reference

<Callout kind="warning">

The order link is private to you. Anyone with it can see the order, change attendee details and cancel it, so don't share or post it. Your ticket links are separate, so you can pass a ticket on without giving away the order.

</Callout>

## What the order page shows

<Screenshot name="order-page" alt="A confirmed order page with the order reference, the lines and total, Download receipt (PDF) and a ticket for each attendee" />

At the top is the event, your order reference and a status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaiting payment** | Your places are held while you pay. Choose **Pay now** to open the payment page. |
| **Requested** | The event needs approval or you're on the waitlist. Nothing has been charged; you'll be emailed a link to pay if a place is yours. |
| **Confirmed** | Paid (or reserved to pay at the desk). Your tickets are issued. |
| **Released** | The time to pay ran out, so the tickets went back on sale. Nothing was charged. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled. Any refund is shown underneath. |

Below that is the summary: each ticket and extra, any **Discount**, the **Total**, who it was **Paid to** (the organisation), and the refund policy your order was bought under. A **Test mode** chip means no real money moved.

## Download your receipt

Once your payment has been received, choose **Download receipt (PDF)** under the order summary. The receipt carries the organisation's receipt details and how you paid. The receipt email also links to it, and you can download it again at any time.

## Find your tickets

Under **Tickets** (or **Passes**), each person on the order has a row with their name and email.

<Steps>

1. Choose **View ticket** next to a confirmed ticket to open it with its QR code.
2. To name someone, choose **Edit details**, fill in **Attendee name** and **Attendee email**, answer any questions, and choose **Save details**.

</Steps>

Everyone with an email address gets their own ticket by email. If a row says "No attendee yet", add their details so they get one; you may get an "Add attendee details" email as a reminder. Once an email address is saved, it can't be changed here.

A pass covers every remaining date of a series. Its ticket page lists the other dates under **Your other dates**.

## Give a ticket to someone else

Some organisers let you transfer tickets until shortly before the event. When they do, a **Transfer** button appears next to the ticket.

<Steps>

1. Choose **Transfer**.
2. Enter **Their name** and **Their email**.
3. Choose **Transfer ticket**.

</Steps>

They get a new ticket by email, and the old ticket stops working. So do the links the previous holder had for it: the "Are you still coming?" link and a project submission link. The new holder is asked about attendance afresh and can request their own project link from their ticket. A ticket that has already been used to check in can't be transferred. The form says until when transfers are possible.

## Emails about your order

| Email | When |
| --- | --- |
| "Complete your payment" | Your places are held and waiting for payment. A reminder follows for a payment link. |
| "Receipt" with your order reference | Your payment arrived and your tickets are confirmed. |
| "Reserved, pay at the desk" | You chose to pay in person. |
| "Your held tickets were released" | The time to pay ran out. Nothing was charged. |
| "Refund" or "Order cancelled" | Money was refunded, or the order was cancelled. |
| "Refund on its way" | Your payment arrived after the event (or the date) was cancelled. No ticket was issued, and the whole amount is being refunded. Card and UPI refunds can take 5 to 10 working days to appear. |

## Good to know

- To cancel or get money back, see [Asking for a refund](/help/attending/asking-for-a-refund).
- If you have an account, your orders and refunds are included when you [download your data](/help/account/your-data).
- Lost the link? Search your inbox (and spam folder) for the emails about your order. Otherwise, ask the organisers: they can see your order and its payment.
