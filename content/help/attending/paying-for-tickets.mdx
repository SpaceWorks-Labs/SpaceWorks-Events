When an event sells tickets, you choose them in the registration dialog and pay on the organiser's own Razorpay or Stripe page. The money goes straight to the organisation running the event. Space Works Events never sees your card, UPI or bank details.

## Buy tickets

<Steps>

1. On the event page, choose **Get tickets**.
2. Under **Tickets**, use **+** and **−** to choose how many of each ticket you want.
3. Add any **Extras**, such as lunch or a T-shirt, the same way.
4. If you have a code from the organisers, enter it under **Access code** or **Promo code** (see below).
5. Fill in your details and answer the organisers' questions.
6. Choose **Continue to payment**. The button shows the total.
7. Pay on the secure payment page. When you're done, you come back to your order page, which confirms your tickets.

</Steps>

The total updates as you choose. Booking fees, if any, are shown before you pay ("Includes … in booking fees"), and the refund policy is printed under the tickets.

## What the ticket list tells you

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Admits 3** | A group ticket: one ticket covers several people. |
| **Pay what you want** or **From …** | You choose the price in **Your price per ticket**, within the limits shown. |
| **+ … booking fee** | A fee added to each ticket. |
| **Members only** | Only for members of the organisation, signed in. |
| **Not on sale yet**, **Sales ended**, **Sold out** | The ticket can't be bought right now. |

Each ticket can have its own limits per order. One order can hold up to 20 tickets and seat up to 100 people. On a series, a pass and single dates are bought in separate orders.

## Tickets for other people

When your order seats more than one person, the form asks **Who else is coming?** You can add each person's name and email now, or later from your order page. Everyone with an email address gets their own ticket. See [Your order and receipt](/help/attending/orders-and-receipts).

## Promo and access codes

- **Promo code**: a discount. Type it and choose **Apply**. The discount and new total appear underneath. If the code doesn't fit what you chose, the form says why, for example "That promo code doesn't apply to these tickets." Choose **Remove** to take it off.
- **Access code**: unlocks tickets that are hidden from everyone else. Type it and choose **Unlock**; the extra tickets appear in the list.

Codes come from the organisers. If one "isn't valid right now" or "has been used up", ask them.

## Your place is held while you pay

When you choose **Continue to payment**, your places are held for 30 minutes so nobody else can take them. Your order and ticket pages show how long is left ("Your tickets are held for 12 more minutes.").

| If you | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Pay in time | Your tickets are confirmed and you're emailed a receipt. |
| Close the payment page | Nothing is charged. Open your order or ticket page and choose **Pay now** while the hold lasts. |
| Run out of time | The places go back on sale and nothing is charged. You're emailed, and can order again if tickets are left. |
| Pay just after the hold ended | If your places were taken in the meantime, the payment is refunded in full and you're emailed. |

Coming back from the payment page before the provider has confirmed, you see "Confirming payment…". The page updates by itself.

<Callout kind="note">

If you register from an event card embedded on another website, the payment page opens in a new tab. Finish paying there; your order page confirms the tickets.

</Callout>

## Pay at the desk

Some tickets let you reserve now and pay in person. When every ticket you chose allows it, pick **Pay at the desk** instead of **Pay online now**. Your places are confirmed straight away, and you pay when you arrive. If you can't make it, cancel from your order so the places go to someone else.

## Approvals and waitlists

If the event needs approval, the button reads **Request tickets** and nothing is charged yet. If it's full, you can **Join the waitlist**. When the organisers approve you, or a place frees up, you're emailed a link to pay. The link holds your places for 24 hours, with one reminder before it runs out.

## Good to know

- The organiser sets the prices you see, and organisers are advised to include any tax in them. Ask the organiser if you need a tax invoice.
- If a ticket page or order says **Test mode** or "(test payment)", no real money moved.
- To get money back, see [Asking for a refund](/help/attending/asking-for-a-refund).
- Organisers: see [Ticket types, group tickets and add-ons](/help/payments/ticket-types).
