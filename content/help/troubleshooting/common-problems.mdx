Find the heading that matches what you're seeing. If nothing here helps, contact the event's organiser, or for account and email problems, the person who runs your server.

## Emails not arriving

Emails are queued and sent in the background, so one can take a few minutes. If sending fails, the app keeps retrying for a few hours.

- **Check your spam or junk folder**, and search for the event's name.
- **Check the address.** A typo when registering sends the ticket elsewhere. Ask the organiser to correct it.
- **Some links expire quickly.** Verification and password-reset links last one hour, and sign-in codes 10 minutes. Ask for a new one: **Resend verification email**, **Forgot password?**, or start the sign-in again.
- **Senders differ.** Account emails come from the platform; tickets, reminders and receipts may come from the organisation's own mail server, under its name.
- **Nobody is getting emails?** Organisers should ask the person who runs your server to check email delivery.

## Can't sign in

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Check your email** | A new browser, or several wrong passwords. Enter the emailed code within 10 minutes. |
| "That code isn't right, or it has expired." | Use the newest code, or start the sign-in again for a fresh one. |
| "Too many unsuccessful sign-ins." | Wait up to an hour, or reset your password. |
| "Verify your email before signing in." | Choose **Resend verification email** and follow the link. |
| "Invalid email or password." | Check for typos or reset your password. Signed up with Google? Use **Continue with Google**. |

An unexpected sign-in code or "New sign-in to your account" email means someone knows your password. Reset it and use **Sign out everywhere**. See [Creating an account and signing in](/help/account/sign-up-and-sign-in).

## Asked for an authenticator app

Anyone with an organisation role or an event co-organiser permission must use an authenticator app.

- **"Set up an authenticator app to continue"?** Set one up under **Two-step verification**, then sign in again.
- **"Confirm it's you" after Google sign-in?** Enter the current code from your authenticator app. You're asked again after 12 hours.
- **Code rejected?** Codes change every 30 seconds. Wait for a fresh one, and make sure your phone sets its clock automatically.

See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).

## Lost your authenticator app

Sign in with your email and password and type one of your recovery codes into **Authenticator or recovery code**. Then turn two-step verification off under **Account settings** → **Security** with another recovery code, and set it up again on your new phone. The **Confirm it's you** page after Google sign-in doesn't take recovery codes. With no recovery codes left, ask the person who runs your server. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).

## Payment problems

- **Paid, but no ticket yet?** Your order page may say "Confirming payment…" while the payment provider reports back. Reload it after a minute.
- **Places released?** Places are held for 30 minutes at checkout, or 24 hours with an emailed payment link. If time runs out, nothing is charged; choose **Order again** if places are left.
- **Want a refund?** Cancel from your order page within the event's refund policy, or ask the organisers. See [Asking for a refund](/help/attending/asking-for-a-refund).
- **Refund not showing yet?** Refunds go back the way you paid. Once sent, how long it takes depends on your bank.
- **Card declined?** The payment page belongs to Razorpay or Stripe. Try another payment method, or contact the organiser.

## Finding your ticket

- Look for the confirmation email. Your ticket link is in it.
- If you registered while signed in, open **Account settings** → **Registrations**.
- If you bought tickets, open your order page from your order emails.
- Still nothing? Ask the organiser. They can find you on the guest list and copy your ticket link.

Waitlisted, pending and unpaid registrations have a ticket page, but it can't be used for entry until you're confirmed.

## Tickets that won't scan

| Message at the door | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Already checked in** | This ticket has been used. Check the name; if it was a mistake, an organiser can choose **Undo check-in**. |
| "This ticket is not for this event." | The ticket belongs to another event or another date of a series. Check the guest opened the right ticket. |
| "No matching ticket found." | The code was mistyped, or the registration was cancelled or removed. Search the guest by name instead. |
| "Guest is on the waitlist…" | Organisers can let them in with **Override waitlist** if there's room. |
| "Registration is pending approval and cannot be checked in." | Approve the registration on the guest list first. |

If the camera won't open, allow camera access for the site in your browser settings, or choose **Manual code** and paste the ticket link. See [The check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk).

## Offline check-ins not syncing

Offline check-ins are sent when the device reconnects with the check-in page open. Ones timed more than a day before the start or over an hour after the end are refused. Don't clear offline data until everything has synced. See [Checking in offline](/help/check-in/offline).

## Page not found for an event

The app shows "not found" rather than revealing events you can't see: usually the event is a draft, was taken offline, or is invite-only and you aren't signed in with the invited, verified email. Short links stop working the same way, and when an organiser makes a new one. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).

## Missing buttons or permissions

What you can do depends on your role. Members can't create or publish events, admins can't change roles or connect payments, and co-organisers only see what they were given on one event. Ask an owner or admin for the access you need. See [Roles and permissions](/help/organisations/roles).

If the app says **Verify your email before creating events.**, open **Account settings**, choose **Resend verification email**, and follow the link in the email.
