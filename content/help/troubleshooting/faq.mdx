Quick answers to the questions people ask most. If yours isn't here, try [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems) or search the guide.

## For guests

### Does it cost anything?

Most events are free. When an event sells tickets, you see the price first and pay on the payment provider's own page (Razorpay or Stripe). The money goes straight to the organisation running the event, so ask the organisers about charges. See [Paying for a ticket](/help/attending/paying-for-tickets).

### Do I need an account to register?

Usually not. For most events you enter your name and email, answer the organiser's questions, and your ticket arrives by email. **Invite-only events** are the exception: sign in with an account using the email address your invitation went to, and verify that address.

An account keeps your tickets, ID cards and certificates together. See [Registering for an event](/help/attending/registering).

### Where is my ticket?

In the confirmation email, and on your **Registrations** page if you registered while signed in. If you bought tickets, your order page lists every ticket on the order. Still stuck? See [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems#finding-your-ticket).

### How do I cancel?

For a free place, open your ticket and choose **Can't make it? Cancel registration**, or use **Cancel registration** on your **Registrations** page. You can't cancel once you've been checked in or the event has ended.

For a paid ticket, open your order page. You can cancel there within the event's refund policy, or ask the organisers for a refund. See [Asking for a refund](/help/attending/asking-for-a-refund).

### When will I get my refund?

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with. Once the organisers' payment provider has sent it, how long it takes to show up depends on your bank. If an event or a date is cancelled, everyone who paid is refunded automatically; a pass gets back its share for that date.

### Can I give my ticket to someone else?

For a paid ticket, the order page shows **Transfer** when the organisers allow it. Otherwise ask the organiser. If you registered without an account, they can change the name and email on your registration so the ticket moves to the other person.

### What do "waitlisted" and "waiting for approval" mean?

**Waitlisted** means the event was full. If a place frees up, you're emailed your ticket, or a link to pay on a paid event. **Waiting for approval** means the organisers review every sign-up, and you'll be emailed when they decide.

### Why did I get a sign-in code by email?

You signed in with your password from a browser the app didn't recognise, or after several wrong passwords. Enter the code to finish signing in. If you didn't try to sign in, someone knows your password: reset it straight away. See [Creating an account and signing in](/help/account/sign-up-and-sign-in).

### Who can see my details?

The event's organisers and the people who run their organisation can see your name, email and answers, and can export the guest list. Anonymous feedback is stored without your identity. The person who runs this server can see account details when helping with support.

### Can I get a copy of my data, or delete my account?

Signed-in users can choose **Download my data** on **Account settings** → **Registrations**. There's no button to delete your account: contact the person who runs this server, or the event's organiser for anything about one event. See [Your data and memberships](/help/account/your-data).

## For organisers

### Can I charge for tickets?

Yes, once the person who runs this server turns paid events on. Your organisation's owner connects your own Razorpay or Stripe account, and the money goes straight to you, with no fee added by the platform. See [Turning on paid events](/help/payments/enable-paid-events).

### Why do I need an authenticator app?

Organisation staff and co-organisers can reach guest lists and contact details, so each sign-in needs an authenticator code. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).

### Who in my team can do what?

That depends on their role. Owners and admins create, publish and run events, and invite people. Only the owner changes roles, connects payments, sets up the organisation's email, manages API access and can delete the organisation. Members can't create or publish events. Co-organisers get exactly the permissions you give them on one event. See [Roles and permissions](/help/organisations/roles).

### Why can't I create an event?

Usually because you haven't verified your email yet (the app says **Verify your email before creating events.**), or your role doesn't allow it. See [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems#missing-buttons-or-permissions).

### Can I edit someone's registration or move their ticket?

Yes. On the guest list, open the guest's **More actions** menu and choose **Edit registration or transfer ticket**. For guests who signed in to register, you can view the registration but not move it. **Copy ticket link** in the same menu copies their ticket address if they've lost the email.

### How do I refund someone?

On the **Guests** tab, open the guest's **More actions** menu, choose **Payment**, then **Refund**. You can refund in part or in full. Owners and admins can do this, and so can co-organisers who manage registrations. Refund requests that buyers send appear in the **Finance** tab. See [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).

### Does check-in work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Before the doors open, choose **Make available offline** on the check-in desk. See [Checking in offline](/help/check-in/offline).

### Can I leave an organisation?

Yes: **Account settings** → **Organisations** → **Leave**. The only owner can't leave; see [Your data and memberships](/help/account/your-data).

### How do I connect other tools?

Owners and admins can post activity to a team chat (see [Chat notifications](/help/organisations/chat-notifications)) and set up webhooks under **Settings** → **Webhooks**. Only the owner manages API access. Owners and admins see the guide's *Connecting other tools* section once signed in.
