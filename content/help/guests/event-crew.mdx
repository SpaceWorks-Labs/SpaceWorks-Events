The crew is the team running your event: volunteers, hosts, stage and logistics helpers, and mentors if you use the Mentor Desk. Keep them in **Guests** → **Crew** so each one gets a free ticket for check-in, you know what to order for their food, and, if you like, the event page can thank them by name.

Crew members don't need an account, and they don't use up guest places. Being on the crew gives nobody access to your dashboard.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission. Turning on the public Crew section also needs permission to edit the event page: owners and admins, or co-organisers with **Edit details**.

</Callout>

Crew are available on one-off events. The dates of a recurring series don't have a crew.

## Adding crew members

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose **Guests** → **Crew**.
2. Choose **Add crew member**.
3. Enter their **Name** and **Email**. Add a **Role** (for example "Volunteer" or "Registration desk"), a **Food preference**, a **Short bio**, a **Photo** and a **LinkedIn URL** if you want.
4. Leave **Show on event page** on to list them in the Crew section, or turn it off to keep them private.
5. Choose **Add and send ticket**.

</Steps>

They get an email saying "You're on the crew for" your event, with their crew ticket (and your designed ticket as a PDF, if the event has one). If you change a crew member's email address later, the ticket is sent to the new address too.

If the person had already registered for the event on their own with a free ticket, their registration becomes their crew ticket. It keeps its ticket link, QR code and any check-in, and the guest place it held is freed for the waitlist. Some registrations can't be turned into a crew ticket:

- Someone who bought a ticket: cancel or refund their order first. Crew tickets are always free and never part of an order.
- Someone in a team: they have to leave their team first.
- Someone with a hackathon project: withdraw or remove the project first.

### Managing the list

Each row shows the member's role, email, food preference and whether they've checked in. On each row you can:

- switch **Show on event page** on or off
- move the member up or down, which sets their order on the event page
- edit their details
- remove them from the crew, which cancels their crew ticket, including any check-in. They're only emailed if the crew ticket was their own registration.

### Mentors

Changing an unlinked crew member's email to a verified account also links their crew place to that account. If their approved volunteer shifts overlap that person's booked office hours, the link is refused. Cancel the shift place or reschedule the booking first, then try again. The same check applies when an invitation or a mentor crew ticket links an account.

If you run the [Mentor Desk](/help/hackathons/mentor-desk) and turn on **Give mentors a crew ticket**, mentors you give Mentor Desk access to from then on join the crew with the role "Mentor", hidden from the event page, and get a free crew ticket. Removing a mentor removes that ticket. A mentor who's already registered keeps their own ticket.

## The crew sign-up link

Instead of adding everyone yourself, share a link so crew can add themselves.

<Steps>

1. In the **Crew sign-up link** card, choose **Create link**.
2. Choose **Copy link** and share it with your team.

</Steps>

People who sign up fill in the same details and get their crew ticket by email. They start hidden from the event page with a **Joined via link** chip, so you can check their details before switching on **Show on event page**.

- **Replace link** makes a new link; the old one stops working straight away.
- **Turn off** removes the link. Crew who already signed up keep their tickets.
- The link stops taking sign-ups once the event has ended or is cancelled.

<Callout kind="warning">

Anyone with the link can join the crew and get a ticket, so share it only with your team. Replace it if it reaches the wrong people.

</Callout>

To keep people's details private, the link never says whether an email address is already on the crew or registered for the event. In that case nothing changes, and the confirmation asks them to contact you. Add them yourself with **Add crew member**.

## Food for crew

The **Food for crew** card groups everyone's food preferences with a count for each, ready for catering. It covers crew only; guests' answers to your registration form aren't included. Only organisers see food preferences.

## Showing the crew on the event page

The event page has an optional **Crew** section. It's off on every event until you turn it on, so nobody is shown publicly by accident. When it's on, it lists crew members with **Show on event page** on, as cards with their name, role, bio, photo and LinkedIn link. Email addresses, food preferences and tickets are never shown.

Turn it on in **Event page** → **Sections**. If some crew are set to show but the section is off, **Guests** → **Crew** also offers **Turn on Crew section**. See [Building your event page](/help/event-page/building-your-page).

## How crew tickets work

- Crew count as going, but never against your place limit or your paid places. An event with 50 places and 8 crew still takes 50 guests. The **Overview** tab shows crew next to the number going.
- Crew check in at the desk like anyone else. The desk shows a **Crew** chip and their food preference. See [The check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk).
- ID badges have **Event crew** and **Confirmed attendees (no crew)** filters, and a crew badge prints *Crew* and the role. See [ID cards and badges](/help/check-in/badges). Designed tickets print *Crew* for the ticket name and leave the price empty.
- In **Guests** → **People**, crew show as **Going** with a **Crew** chip. Edit and remove them in **Crew**, not there. The guest CSV marks them as crew.
- Crew can't cancel their own crew ticket, buy a ticket, join a team, or enter a hackathon project.
- Crew are never asked to [confirm attendance](/help/guests/attendance-confirmation).

## Crew expenses

If crew pay for things on your behalf, an owner or admin can turn on **Let crew claim expenses from their crew ticket page** in the **Finance** tab. Crew then see **Claim expenses** on their ticket page. There's no limit per person; owners and admins approve, reject and pay each claim, and the claims list marks them *(crew)*. See [Expense claims](/help/finance/expense-claims).

## Good to know

- An event can have up to 500 crew members, and each email address can only be on the crew once.
- Cloning an event doesn't copy the crew or the sign-up link. See [Cloning, cancelling and deleting](/help/events/clone-cancel-delete).
- Cancelling the event tells the crew, like everyone with a place.
- What crew members see is in [Joining an event's crew](/help/attending/joining-the-crew).
