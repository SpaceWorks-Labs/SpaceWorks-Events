A day or two before a busy event, it helps to know who is really coming. Attendance confirmation emails every confirmed guest a short "Are you still coming?" with **Yes** and **No** buttons, and collects the answers for you. If you like, a No can free the guest's place for someone else.

Nothing is ever sent automatically. There's no schedule or timer: guests are only asked when you choose to ask them.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation. No co-organiser permission covers emailing guests, so co-organisers don't see the **Attendance confirmation** card or the **Share attendance link** option.

</Callout>

## Who gets asked

- Every guest whose registration is **Going** on a one-off event, at the address they registered with. On team events, each team member is asked individually.
- Guests on the waitlist, waiting for approval, waiting to pay, or declined are never asked.
- [Crew](/help/guests/event-crew) are never asked: they're running the event. A guest who was asked and then joins the crew loses the question, and its link stops working.
- Each guest is only asked once. Anyone already asked is skipped, even if two organisers press send at the same time.

Recurring series dates and passes can't be asked yet.

## Ask your guests

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Emails** tab. The **Attendance confirmation** card is at the top.
2. Decide whether a No should free the guest's place (see below), and set **Free the place when a guest says No** before you send.
3. Choose **Ask guests to confirm attendance**.
4. Check how many confirmed guests will be asked and the event date.
5. Choose the send button, for example **Send to 40 guests**.

</Steps>

Each guest gets one email, and each email counts towards your email sending limit. The email shows the event's date, time and place, with two buttons: **Yes, I'll be there** and **No, I can't make it**. Choosing one opens the guest's private answer page with that answer picked; they then send it and can add a note of up to 500 characters. See [Answering "Are you still coming?"](/help/attending/still-coming) for what guests see.

Most organisers send it one or two days before the event. You can send until the event starts.

### Guests who get a place later

After the first send, the button reads **Ask newly confirmed guests**. Use it for people who got a place since, for example from the waitlist. It only asks guests who haven't been asked before.

### Reminding people who haven't replied

Choose **Remind people who haven't replied** and confirm. Guests without an answer get the question again, with a new link; the link in their earlier email stops working. Each guest can be reminded at most once every 12 hours, and the card tells you when the next reminder is possible.

You can send or remind up to 10 times an hour.

### Sending a guest's link yourself

If you'd rather reach someone by text or WhatsApp, or their email isn't getting through, you can copy their personal link and send it yourself.

<Steps>

1. Open the **Guests** tab and choose **People**.
2. Find the guest, open the row's menu and choose **Share attendance link**.
3. Choose **Create link**, then **Copy link**, and paste it into your message.

</Steps>

No email is sent. The link opens the same answer page as the email. Send it only to that guest, because anyone with the link can answer for them.

- If the guest hasn't been asked yet, they now count as asked and show **No reply**. **Ask guests to confirm attendance** won't email them as well.
- If they were already asked, the button reads **Create new link**. The new link replaces the one in their email, which stops working. An answer they already gave stays, and they can change it with the new link.
- For safety, Space Works Events can't show a link again later. Each time you choose **Share attendance link**, you get a new one, and the previous one stops working.

The option shows for guests who are **Going**, until the event starts.

## Freeing places when a guest says No

**Free the place when a guest says No** is off unless you turn it on.

- **Off:** a No is only recorded. The guest keeps their registration until you or they cancel it.
- **On:** a No gives the place up, in the same way the guest could themselves.

What "giving the place up" means depends on the ticket:

| Ticket | What a No does |
| --- | --- |
| Free | The registration is cancelled, as if the guest cancelled from their ticket. If you don't approve each sign-up, the next person on the waitlist moves up. On team events the usual team rules apply, such as passing leadership on. |
| Paid (or reserved to pay at the desk), on an order of its own, and the person answering bought it | The order is cancelled under the event's refund policy, exactly as the buyer could cancel it from their order page, and they get back what the policy allows. A desk reservation is simply cancelled, with nothing to refund. |
| Paid, but the refund policy no longer allows a cancellation | The place is kept and a refund request is opened for you, once. Approve or decline it under **Finance**. If the guest then says Yes while it's still open, it's withdrawn and you're told they're coming after all. See [Refund requests](/help/finance/refund-requests). |
| Paid or reserved at the desk, on an order with several tickets (or a team ticket that pays for the whole team), or bought by someone else | Nothing is cancelled. You get an email (and a chat message, if your chat is connected), and the guest list shows **Said No · paid**. Cancel or refund it yourself if you need to: see [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds). |

Guests are warned before they send a No that releases their place, and they can't take it back from the answer page: they'd have to register again while places are left. A guest who has already checked in always keeps their place, but their answer is still recorded.

The email follows the switch, and the guest's own ticket, at the moment it's delivered: it only mentions giving up the place when that would really happen, and for a paid ticket it names the refund the policy gives. You're told about a guest's paid No once, however often they change their answer.

A guest's note goes into the refund request when one is opened, so you'll see it in **Finance**; the email and chat message about a kept place never include it. It never promises the place to someone on the waitlist, because events where you approve each sign-up don't move anyone up automatically.

<Callout kind="tip">

Choose the setting before you send. The confirmation step reminds you which way it's set: "A No frees their place" or "A No is recorded only".

</Callout>

## Following the replies

Answers from a link you shared yourself show up in exactly the same places as answers from the email.

- **The card** shows how many guests were asked, when and by whom the last email went, and counts for **Coming**, **Not coming**, **No reply** and **Newly confirmed, not asked**. If places were released, it says how many.
- **Guests** → **People** shows each guest's answer as a chip (**Said No · paid** when a paid place was kept) and any note under their row. A drop-down filters the list by answer, with counts, and saved views keep it. See [The guest list](/help/guests/guest-list).
- **The check-in desk** shows the answer chip for guests who were asked, never their note. See [The check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk).
- **The Overview tab** adds a line such as "Attendance: 12 coming · 3 not coming · 5 no reply" once anyone has been asked.
- **The guest CSV** has **Attendance confirmation** and **Attendance note** columns. See [Exporting guest data](/help/guests/exports).

Guests can change their answer until the event starts. After that, the replies are final.

## Good to know

- You can't ask, remind or share links once the event has started, or if it's cancelled.
- A queued email is dropped rather than sent if the guest has already answered, is no longer going, or the event was cancelled.
- Cloning an event copies the **Free the place when a guest says No** setting, never the questions or answers. See [Cloning, cancelling and deleting](/help/events/clone-cancel-delete).
- Answers are deleted with the registration. Guests' own answers are included when they [download their data](/help/account/your-data).
- For any other message to guests, see [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests).
