You can download your guest list as a CSV file to open in a spreadsheet, print a door list, share numbers with a venue, or import into another tool. Guest and team exports hold every matching row for the whole event, not just the rows on screen, and follow the filters you've picked.

## Which export to use

| Button | Where | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Export CSV** | **Guests** → **People** | One row per registration, with status, check-in time, ticket link, ticket and order, and every answer. |
| **Export emails** | **Guests** → **People** | Just names and email addresses, one row per address. |
| **Export CSV** | **Guests** → **Teams** (team events) | One row per team, with its members. |
| **Export attendance** | **Check-in** tab | The same file as People **Export CSV**, for every registration. |
| **Download orders (CSV)** | **Analytics** tab, Money card (paid events) | One row per order, with its money. |

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission. **Export attendance** is on the Check-in tab, so co-organisers also need the **Check-in** permission to see it; they can get the same file from People instead.

</Callout>

## Downloading guests

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Guests** tab, then **People**.
2. Pick any filters you want, for example **Going**, or type a search. Leave everything on **All** for every registration.
3. Choose **Export CSV** or **Export emails**. The file downloads straight away.

</Steps>

The export holds every registration that matches the filters and search showing, even ones you haven't loaded. If nothing matches, the buttons are greyed out.

## What's in the guest CSV

Columns appear in this order:

- **Name**, **Email** and **Status**.
- **Team** and **Team role** (Leader, Member or Solo), on team events only.
- **Registration Date** and **Checked In At**.
- **Ticket URL**: each guest's private ticket link.
- **Ticket**, **Order**, **Order status** and **Order total**: the ticket type, order number, order state and total with its currency. Empty for registrations without an order.
- One column for each question on your registration form, headed with the question.
- One column for each custom check-in list, headed "Check-in:" and the list name, with the time the guest was checked into it.

A few things to know when you open it:

- **Status** uses the stored names: CONFIRMED (going), WAITLISTED, PENDING_APPROVAL (to approve), REJECTED (declined) and PENDING_PAYMENT (unpaid).
- Dates and times are in UTC in a standard format, for example `2026-10-14T09:30:00.000Z`. Most spreadsheets can convert them to your time zone.
- Yes / no answers show as Yes or No.
- A question where guests can tick several choices shows every choice in one cell, separated by semicolons, in the order the choices appear on your form. Anything typed into **Other** comes last.

<Callout kind="warning">

The **Ticket URL** column contains working ticket links. Anyone with a link can open that guest's ticket and QR code, so store and share the file carefully, and delete the column before sending the file outside your team.

</Callout>

## The email list

**Export emails** gives a two-column file, **Name** and **Email**, with each address once. It's handy for importing into a mailing tool. To email guests from Space Works Events instead, use the **Emails** tab; see [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests).

## The teams CSV

On team events, **Export CSV** on the **Teams** sub-tab downloads every team matching the filter chip and search showing. Each row has the **Team** name, **Status**, **Size**, whether it's **Incomplete** or **Looking for members**, the **Leader** and **Leader email**, the **Members** (each as a name and email), the **Submission status** of its hackathon project (None when there isn't one) and when it was **Created**.

## The orders CSV

On an event that sells tickets, open the **Analytics** tab and choose **Download orders (CSV)** in the Money card. It isn't filtered: you get every order, with its number, status, buyer, tickets and extras, subtotal, discount and promo code, booking fees, total, what was paid and how, refunds, anything owed at the desk, chargebacks, and whether it was a test payment. See [Revenue and reports](/help/payments/revenue).

## Good to know

- File names include the event title and the date, such as `attendees_Spring_Meetup_2026-10-27.csv`.
- Each download is recorded in the audit log. For guest and team exports, it notes which filters were used but never the search text.
- Feedback responses, judging scores and event finance have their own exports. See [Feedback forms](/help/after/feedback), [Awards and results](/help/hackathons/results) and [The Finance tab and budget](/help/finance/overview-and-budget).
- For counts and charts rather than a list, see [Analytics](/help/after/analytics) and [The post-event report](/help/after/event-report).
