The **Emails** tab lets you write to the people registered for your event: joining instructions, a change of room, or a thank-you afterwards. You pick who gets it, start from a template, personalise it with placeholders, choose a design, check the preview, and send.

Emails only go to people who have registered for this event. You can't email arbitrary addresses from here.

<Callout kind="permission">

Organisation owners and admins. Co-organisers can't send emails to guests, whatever their permissions.

</Callout>

## Sending an email

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Emails** tab.
2. Under **Pick a starting point**, choose a template. **More templates** shows the rest.
3. Under **Who gets it**, choose one or more registration statuses, and an attendance option.
4. Under **Write it**, edit the **Subject** and the **Message**.
5. Check the **Preview · as sent**, and choose a **Design**.
6. Choose **Send a test to me** and read the test in your inbox.
7. Choose **Send to** and the number of people, tick the box to confirm you've checked the preview, and choose **Send to** again.

</Steps>

A bar counts down five seconds before the email goes. Choose **Undo** to stop it; nothing is sent. After that, the emails are queued and delivered in the background, a few at a time, so a large audience takes a few minutes. Emails can't be recalled once delivery starts.

## Templates

Templates fill in a subject, a message and a suggested audience. Everything stays editable.

The first cards are **General update**, **Reminder before the event**, **Know before you go**, **Agenda update** and **Thank you and feedback**. **More templates** adds **Schedule or venue change**, **Waitlist update**, **Speaker announcement**, **Event cancelled**, **Pending approval update**, **Live update on the day**, **Sorry we missed you** and **Slides and resources**.

## Choosing who gets it

The status chips match the guest list: **Going**, **Waitlist**, **To approve** and **Declined**, each with its count. Pick any combination.

The attendance choice narrows them further: **Everyone**, **Checked in**, or **Didn't check in**. For example, **Going** with **Didn't check in** reaches people who had a place but didn't come.

The line under the audience says how many people will get the email. Each address gets one email, even if it registered more than once.

## Writing the message

The message toolbar has bold, italic, headings, bulleted and numbered lists, quotes and links, plus two menus:

- **Button** adds a call-to-action button: **View your ticket**, **Open the event page**, **Share your feedback**, or **Custom button…** with your own text and link.
- **Placeholder** inserts details that are filled in for each person.

| Placeholder | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{{name}}` | The guest's name, or "there" if they didn't give one |
| `{{event}}` | The event title |
| `{{eventDate}}` | The start date and time, in the event's time zone |
| `{{location}}` | The venue, or "Online" |
| `{{eventUrl}}` | The public event page |
| `{{ticketLink}}` | The guest's own ticket, for people going; otherwise the event page |
| `{{feedbackUrl}}` | Your feedback form; open the form before sending this |
| `{{scheduleUrl}}` | The public programme |
| `{{organisation}}` | Your organisation's name |

In the subject you can use `{{name}}`, `{{event}}`, `{{eventDate}}` and `{{organisation}}`; they're listed under **Subject placeholders**. A placeholder with a typo stays visible in the preview, so you can spot it before sending.

## Designs and preview

**Design** frames the same message in one of five looks: **Classic**, **Minimal**, **Accent band** (in the event's colour), **Photo header** (using the event's cover photo, so it needs a cover) and **Letter**. The preview is the real email, filled in with sample details, and you can switch between **Phone** and **Desktop** widths.

## Who it comes from

Emails go out under your organisation's name. If your organisation has set a sender name and reply-to address, or connected its own email server, they're used here too, so replies reach you. See [Sending email from your organisation](/help/organisations/email-sending).

## Drafts

Your email is saved as a draft as you type, so you can close the tab and pick it up later on any device. The line under the message shows whether it's saved; choose **Save draft** to save straight away, or **Discard draft…** to start again.

Drafts are private: other organisers never see yours. Your draft is cleared once you send the email. Loading a template or reusing an old email asks before replacing your draft.

## Reusing an email

**Sent before** lists recent emails for this event, with their audience, how many people they went to and how delivery went (delivered, waiting, failed, or skipped because the person no longer matched). Choose **Reuse** to load one, with its design and audience, into the editor.

## Test emails

**Send a test to me** sends the current email to your own address, which must be verified. Tests don't affect your draft.

## Good to know

- Only guests who are going get a working ticket link. Everyone else's `{{ticketLink}}` and **View your ticket** button opens the event page.
- If a guest's status changes before their email is delivered so they no longer match the audience, they're skipped.
- If you send several emails in a short time, you're asked to wait before sending another.
- Some emails go out without you doing anything, such as tickets, approval decisions and a reminder the day before. See [Automatic emails](/help/guests/automatic-emails).
- If guests say an email never arrived, see [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems).
