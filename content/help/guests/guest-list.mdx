The guest list shows everyone registered for your event, their status, payment and check-in. It's where you approve requests, give waitlisted guests a place, add people yourself and fix mistakes.

## Opening the guest list

Open the event and choose the **Guests** tab, then **People**. Each row shows the guest's name and email, when they registered, their status and their check-in time.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission. Undoing a check-in also needs the **Check-in** permission.

</Callout>

<Screenshot name="guests-people" alt="The Guests tab People list with status filters, search and guest rows" />

The list loads 50 guests at a time; choose **Load more guests** for the next batch. The filter counts always cover the whole event.

## Guest statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Going** | Has a place and a ticket. |
| **Waitlist** | Signed up when the event was full and is waiting for a place. |
| **To approve** | Asked to join an event where you approve each sign-up. |
| **Declined** | You turned down their request or removed them from the waitlist. |
| **Unpaid** | Has places held while they pay for a ticket. |

Narrow the list with the filter chips (**All**, **Going**, **Waitlist**, **To approve**, **Declined**, **Checked in**, **Not checked in**), choose **Everyone**, **Guests only** (registered without an account) or **Signed-in users**, and search by name or email. Team events add **In a team** and **Solo**. Once you've asked guests whether they're still coming, an attendance drop-down filters by their answer, and each row shows the answer and any note. See [Filters and saved views](/help/guests/saved-views) and [Attendance confirmation](/help/guests/attendance-confirmation). Your event's crew show as **Going** with a **Crew** chip and are managed in **Crew**; see [Event crew](/help/guests/event-crew).

## Approving, declining and giving places

The buttons on each row change with the guest's status:

- **To approve**: **Approve** gives them a place; **Decline** turns them down.
- **Waitlist**: **Give a spot** gives them a place if one is free. To turn them down, open the row's menu and choose **Decline**.
- **Declined**: **Re-accept** gives them a place if there's room, or puts them on the waitlist if the event is full.

Each change emails the guest: a ticket, or a note when they're declined. On a paid ticket, getting a place emails a payment link instead; see [Automatic emails](/help/guests/automatic-emails).

If **Approve** or **Give a spot** says the event is at capacity, raise the place limit or free a place first; see [Capacity, waitlists and approvals](/help/registration/capacity-and-waitlist). On team events, people in a team move with their whole team, so their row shows **Manage in Teams**; see [Managing teams](/help/guests/managing-teams).

## Paid guests

When an event sells tickets, guests with a paid order show a payment chip: the amount paid or still awaited, the amount to pay at the desk, or labels such as **Complimentary**, **Refunded** or **Disputed**. Choose **Payment** from the row's menu to see the order and, depending on its state:

- Record money taken in person with **Mark as paid**, or waive it with **Make complimentary**.
- Email the buyer a payment link with **Send payment link**, which holds their places for 24 hours.
- **Refund** in full or in part, **Cancel tickets**, or **Correct record** for a payment taken in person.

Every payment action applies to the whole order. See [Orders and receipts](/help/payments/orders-and-receipts) and [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).

## Adding a guest yourself

<Steps>

1. On the **People** sub-tab, choose **Add guest**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Email**, and answer any registration questions.
3. If the event sells tickets, choose the **Ticket**. For a paid one, choose **Email a payment link**, **They've paid me already** (with how they paid) or **Complimentary**.
4. Choose **Add attendee**.

</Steps>

They're emailed their ticket, or a payment link, straight away. On a free ticket the usual rules apply: the waitlist if the event is full, or **To approve** if you approve each sign-up. You can add guests even after sign-ups close, but each email address only once per event.

## Editing a registration or transferring a ticket

Choose **Edit registration or transfer ticket** from the row's menu to correct answers, or change the name and email to hand the ticket to someone else, then choose **Save transfer**. The ticket link, status and check-in stay the same, and the new address is emailed the ticket.

For people who registered with an account, the menu says **View registration**: you can update their answers but not move their ticket.

## Removing a guest

Choose **Remove RSVP** from the row's menu and confirm. A message offers **Undo** for about ten seconds, which brings the registration back exactly as it was. Otherwise the guest is emailed a few minutes later that their registration was removed, and a freed place goes to the next person on the waitlist, unless you approve each sign-up.

<Callout kind="warning">

Paid tickets can't be removed this way. Use **Payment** → **Cancel tickets** instead, so the refund and receipt stay on record.

</Callout>

## Other actions

- **Answers** shows what the guest wrote on your registration form; the number next to it is how many questions they answered.
- **Copy ticket link** copies the guest's private ticket link. Treat it like a ticket: anyone with the link can use it.
- **Undo check-in** marks a checked-in guest as not checked in, after you confirm.
- On team events, **Add to a team** or **Move to another team** puts a guest into a team with room.

## Good to know

- Guests who cancel their own registration leave the list and get an email confirming it.
- Chips under a guest show the custom check-in lists they've been checked into, such as meals or swag. See [Custom check-in lists](/help/check-in/check-in-lists).
- To download the list, see [Exporting guest data](/help/guests/exports).
