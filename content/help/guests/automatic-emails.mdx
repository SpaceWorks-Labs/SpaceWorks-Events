Space Works Events sends some emails for you, without anyone pressing send. This page lists them, who gets them and when, so you know what your guests have already been told.

You can't change their wording or turn them off. For anything else, write to guests yourself from the **Emails** tab; see [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests).

## Who they come from

Emails about your events go out under your organisation's name, using its sender name, reply-to address or own email server if an owner has set them up. Account emails, such as email verification and password resets, always come from Space Works Events itself. See [Sending email from your organisation](/help/organisations/email-sending).

## Registration emails

These go to the guest at the address they registered with.

| When | Subject | What's in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| They register and get a place | RSVP Confirmed: *event* | Their ticket link, the start, end and location, and calendar links. A designed ticket is attached as a PDF. |
| They register while the event is full | RSVP Waitlisted: *event* | That they're on the waitlist. |
| They register where you approve each sign-up | RSVP Pending Approval: *event* | That their request is waiting for you. |
| They get a place later | RSVP Confirmed: *event* | The same ticket email as above. |
| You decline them | RSVP Update: Declined - *event* | That their request was declined. |
| They cancel their own registration | Cancelled: *event* | That their ticket no longer works. |
| You remove them and don't undo it | Registration removed: *event* | That the organiser removed their registration. Sent a few minutes later. |

"Get a place later" covers **Approve**, **Give a spot** and **Re-accept**, a place freeing up for the next person on the waitlist, and a move into a team that's going. Guests you add with **Add guest** get the email they would have got by registering, and transferring a ticket sends it to the new address.

On team events, each member gets their own copy, naming their team.

## Reminders and changes

| When | Subject | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About a day before the start | Reminder: *event* starts soon | Each guest who's going, with their ticket link. Someone who registered recently gets theirs once 12 hours have passed since they signed up, if the event hasn't started by then. |
| You change the time or place of a live event | Updated: *event* | Everyone with a place, a waitlist spot or a request, with the new time and place. Wording edits send nothing. |
| You cancel the event | Cancelled: *event* | The same people. Anyone who paid also gets a refund email. |

Reminders also go out for each date of a recurring series.

## Payment emails

On events that sell tickets, the buyer gets:

| When | Subject |
| --- | --- |
| Their places are held while they pay: at checkout, after approval, from the waitlist, or with a link you sent | Complete your payment: *event* |
| A few hours before a day-long hold ends, once | Reminder: your tickets are held until *time*: *event* |
| They reserved to pay at the desk | Reserved, pay at the desk: *event* |
| The payment goes through | Receipt *order number*: *event*, with a link to the PDF receipt |
| The hold ran out unpaid | Your held tickets were released: *event* |
| Some tickets in their order have no attendee yet | Add attendee details: *event* |
| You refund or cancel the order | Refund *order number*, or Order cancelled *order number* |

Each attendee with an email address also gets their own ticket email. See [Orders and receipts](/help/payments/orders-and-receipts) and [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).

## Invitations

| When | Subject | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You add an address on **Guests** → **Invites** | You're invited: *event* | The invited address, with a link to the event page. See [Invite-only events](/help/registration/invitations). |
| You invite a co-organiser | Co-organiser invitation: *event* | The person you invited, with a link to accept. See [Event co-organisers](/help/organisations/co-organisers). |

## Team emails

| When | Subject | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Someone creates a team | Your team *team* · *event* | The leader, with their private team page link and the join link to share. |
| Leadership passes to someone else | You now lead *team* · *event* | The new leader, with a new team page link. |
| The leader resets the team page link | New team page link for *team* · *event* | The leader. Old links stop working. |
| The leader adds a member by name and email | You're on team *team* · *event* | The new member, with their ticket link. |

## Hackathon emails

| When | Subject | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A project is submitted | Submission received · *event* | The person who submitted it. |
| A solo entrant asks for a submission link | Your submission link · *event* | That entrant's registered address. |
| You publish the results for the first time | Results are out · *event* | Every registration that's going, once per address. |

## Emails to organisers

The organisation's owners and admins get an email when:

- People ask to join an event where you approve each sign-up (New requests: *event*, at most one every half hour).
- Someone submits an expense claim or asks for a refund. See [Expense claims and reimbursements](/help/finance/expense-claims) and [Refund requests](/help/finance/refund-requests).
- A refund is refused by the payment provider, or a buyer opens a chargeback (owners only).

To get these in your team chat too, see [Chat notifications](/help/organisations/chat-notifications).

## Good to know

- Emails are queued and sent in the background, usually within a minute or two. If delivery fails, it's tried again.
- Each email is checked again just before sending. If the guest's situation has changed by then, the out-of-date email is dropped.
- People also get account emails, such as sign-in codes, new sign-in alerts and password changes. See [Profile, appearance and security](/help/account/profile-and-security).
- If nothing arrives at all, see [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems).
