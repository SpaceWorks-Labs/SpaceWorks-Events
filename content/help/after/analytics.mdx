The **Analytics** tab shows how your event is doing in plain numbers: how many people signed up, how many have a place, how many turned up, and how many gave feedback. Before the event it shows live numbers, so you can see whether to promote it more or open more places. Afterwards, it shows the final picture.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation, and co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission. Downloading the PDF report is for owners and admins only.

</Callout>

## Opening analytics

<Steps>

1. Open the event from your dashboard.
2. Choose the **Analytics** tab.
3. Read the line at the top: it says whether you're looking at live numbers or the final numbers for an event that has ended.

</Steps>

Until someone signs up, the tab just says "No responses yet". Everything on it is a count: it never lists who registered.

## Key numbers

Four numbers sit across the top:

| Number | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Going** | People with a confirmed place, and how many places are left or that the event is full. On team events, places are counted in teams and solo registrants. |
| **Waitlist** | People waiting for a place to open up. |
| **Waiting for approval** | Sign-ups that need your decision. Choose **Review** to go to the **Guests** tab. |
| **Turnout** | The share of people going who were checked in. It shows once check-in starts. |

## Money

If the event sells tickets, a **Money** card shows what was **Collected**, **Refunded** and the **Net**, with the number of **Paid orders**. When they apply, it also shows promo discounts, booking fees, extras, complimentary orders, money still owed at the desk, paid sign-ups still waiting for approval or a place, and any chargebacks to answer in your payment provider's dashboard. Below that, takings are split **By ticket** and by **How people paid**, with a chart of money collected per day. Choose **Download orders (CSV)** for the full list.

If every payment so far was a test, the card says so: no real money moved. For more on the numbers, see [Revenue and reports](/help/payments/revenue).

## From sign-up to thank-you

This chart follows people through four steps:

1. **Responded**: everyone who registered or asked to join.
2. **Going**: everyone with a confirmed place.
3. **Checked in**: everyone checked in at the event.
4. **Gave feedback**: every response to the feedback form.

Each bar is measured against everyone who responded, so you can compare the steps at a glance. Each step also shows its share of the step before, such as "75% of going". A sentence above the chart sums it up, for example "Of 80 responses, 62 are going and 40 checked in."

**Checked in** and **Gave feedback** stay empty until check-in or feedback starts, or until the event ends.

<Callout kind="note">

A later step can be bigger than the one before it. Turnout can include guests let in with an override at the door, and anonymous feedback can come from people who didn't check in, or from the same person twice. When that happens, the chart says so instead of showing a percentage over 100.

</Callout>

## The other cards

- **Where every response stands** splits every response into **Going**, **Waitlist**, **Waiting for approval** and **Declined**, so you can see how many decisions are still open.
- **When people signed up** is a bar chart of sign-ups per day in the event's time zone, with the **Total sign-ups**, **Average per day**, the **Last 7 days** (while the event is still to come) and the busiest day. For long sign-up periods, each bar covers several days. Use it to see which announcements brought people in.
- **When people arrived** charts check-ins over time, with the first check-in and the busiest stretch.
- **How people were checked in** splits arrivals between the **Check-in desk**, the **Door station** and **Offline, synced later**. Each guest counts once, by the device that last checked them in.
- **Waitlist** shows how many joined the waitlist, how many got a place and how many are still waiting.
- **Short link visits** counts opens of your event's short link per day over the last 14 days. Only a number is kept, never who opened it, and link previews in chat apps aren't counted.
- **Answers to your registration questions** charts the choice questions on your sign-up form, from the people going. Written answers are counted, never shown.

Cards that have nothing to show yet, such as arrivals before check-in starts, stay hidden.

## After the event

Once the event ends, the tab switches to final numbers, and owners and admins see **Download report (PDF)**. The report has everything on this tab and more, ready to share with sponsors or your team. See [The post-event report](/help/after/event-report).

## Good to know

- To see who registered, use the **Guests** tab. See [The guest list](/help/guests/guest-list).
- To download the guest list with check-in times, see [Exporting guest data](/help/guests/exports).
- Feedback numbers come from your feedback form. See [Feedback forms](/help/after/feedback).
