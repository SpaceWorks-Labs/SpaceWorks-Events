After your event, Space Works Events can put the whole story into a tidy PDF: who signed up, who came and when, what the tickets brought in, what was on the programme, and what people thought. It's made for sharing with sponsors, funders, venue partners or your own team, so it holds counts and totals only and never names a guest.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation. The report is available once the event has ended.

</Callout>

## Downloading the report

<Steps>

1. Open the event from your dashboard. The event must have finished.
2. Choose **Download PDF report** at the top of the event's overview, or open the **Analytics** tab and choose **Download report (PDF)**.
3. Save the PDF, or open it straight away.

</Steps>

Before the event ends, neither button appears. Each download is recorded in the organisation's audit log. Reports are limited to 20 downloads every 10 minutes per person; wait a few minutes if a download is refused.

### Recurring series

Each date in a series has its own report. Open the series from your dashboard, and under **Reports**, choose **Download PDF report** next to the date you want. Only dates that have finished are listed. See [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).

## What's in the report

The report opens with the event itself: its date and time, place, visibility and capacity, and a sentence summing up how many people signed up, had a place, were checked in and gave feedback. Two rows of headline numbers follow:

- **Attendance**: signed up, going, checked in, and feedback responses.
- **The event**: sessions, speakers, tracks, sponsors, resources, emails sent, invitations, questions on the sign-up and feedback forms, and co-organisers.

Then come the sections:

| Section | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Registrations** | How many signed up, got a place and came; capacity; the first and busiest sign-up days; teams and solo registrants on team events; invitations; the waitlist; short link visits; and a chart of sign-ups over time. |
| **At the door** | Who was checked in and who didn't come, the first check-in, the busiest time, a chart of arrivals, and how people were checked in: at the check-in desk, offline then synced, or at the door station. |
| **Money** | What tickets brought in after refunds, with discounts, fees, extras, takings by ticket and how people paid. When the event has a budget, approved expense claims or prize money paid out, a **Spending** part shows them, and what is left of the budget. |
| **Programme** | The published sessions, speakers and sponsors, as they appeared on the event page. |
| **Feedback** | How many people answered the feedback form and the response rate, whether certificates were offered, and a count of answers for each question. |
| **Registration answers** | A count of answers for each sign-up question, from the people going. |
| **Emails and team** | The emails you sent to guests from the app and how many people each went to, and how many co-organisers helped. |

**Money**, **Programme**, **Feedback** and **Registration answers** only appear when the event took money or had spending or a budget, had sessions, speakers or sponsors, a feedback form, or sign-up questions. If every payment was a test, the money section says so. If nobody was checked in with the app, **At the door** says so.

It closes with **About this report**, which says when it was made. All times are in the event's time zone, and amounts are shown with their currency code.

## What's left out

The report is meant to be shared, so it leaves out anything personal:

- No guest is named.
- No email addresses, phone numbers or ticket links.
- Typed answers, in the sign-up form or the feedback form, are counted but never quoted. Choice answers, such as "Excellent" or "Vegetarian", are shown as counts.
- Confirmation and ticket emails aren't listed under emails.

Speakers and sponsors do appear by name, because they're already public on your event page.

<Callout kind="tip">

Need the detail behind the numbers? Download the guest list with check-in times from the **Guests** tab (see [Exporting guest data](/help/guests/exports)), and feedback responses with **Download CSV** on the **Feedback** tab (see [Feedback forms](/help/after/feedback)). Unlike the report, those files contain personal data, so share them carefully.

</Callout>

## Good to know

- The numbers reflect the app's records at the moment you download. If you check in a late arrival or more feedback comes in, download the report again.
- The same numbers are on the **Analytics** tab while you're working. See [Analytics](/help/after/analytics).
- Check-ins you recorded some other way, such as a paper list, aren't counted.
- A series date's report covers only that date. Passes that cover several dates are counted in the series' own figures, not in each date's money section.
