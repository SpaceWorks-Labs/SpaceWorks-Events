Space Works Events can make an ID card or name badge for every guest. You design the badge once and it's saved with the event, so every desk uses the same design. Print badges as guests arrive, or download them all in advance as one print-ready PDF.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, and co-organisers with the **Check-in** permission. Volunteers at the [door station](/help/check-in/door-station) can print badges with your saved design, but can't change it.

</Callout>

## The Badges page

The **Badges** page is the place to set up the design and prepare badges before the day. To open it, choose **Open check-in** on the event's **Check-in** tab, then **Badges** at the top of the desk.

<Steps>

1. Under **Design**, check the preview and choose **Edit design** to open the badge designer (see below).
2. Under **Download all**, choose which **Attendees** to include: **Confirmed**, **Checked in**, **Confirmed, not checked in yet**, or **Confirmed, waitlisted and pending**.
3. Pick a **Layout**: **One per page** (each page is one badge, for card and label printers) or **A4 sheets** (as many badges as fit on A4, with crop marks).
4. Choose **Download all (PDF)**. One PDF holds up to 1,000 badges; large events can take a minute.

</Steps>

The **Attendees** list beside it lets you search by name or email and download one guest's badge as a **PDF** or **PNG**, or **Print** it.

## Printing at the check-in desk

<Steps>

1. Open the check-in desk on the device connected to your printer.
2. Turn on **ID card printing**, next to **Override waitlist**.
3. Check someone in. A sheet opens with their badge.
4. Choose **Print badge** or press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>. Choose **Skip** if they don't need one.

</Steps>

After printing, the sheet offers **Print again** in case the badge didn't come out, and **Done**. To reprint, choose **Print ID card** on the guest's **Checked in** panel. On a signed-in desk, **Design badge** and **Edit design** open the designer.

**ID card printing** is off by default and only applies to the desk you're on, so turn it on at each desk that has a printer.

<Callout kind="tip">

In your browser's print settings, print at 100% (actual size), with headers and footers turned off. If nothing happens, allow pop-ups for this site: badges print from a separate window.

</Callout>

## Designing the badge

The **Badge designer** has settings on the left, a live preview in the middle, and the selected item's properties on the right. Changes save by themselves. Without a connection the status shows **Saved on this device only**, and the design saves when you're back online.

<Screenshot name="badge-designer" alt="The badge designer with style, paper and text settings, a live badge preview and the selected item panel" />

**Style.** Pick **Classic** (a thin frame, with a rule under the heading), **Bold** (the heading on a colour band, the name in capitals) or **Minimal** (no lines, just the name). The guest's name is always the largest text. Changing style resets item positions.

**Colour.** In **Colour** mode, choose colours for the **Card**, the frame or band, and the **Text**; if a text colour wouldn't be readable, the badge picks one that is and tells you. **Black and white** prints black on white only, for laser and thermal printers.

**Paper.** Choose your **Badge stock**: **A6**, **4 × 6 in**, **3 × 4 in**, **4 × 3 in**, **ID card (CR80)**, or **Custom size** with a width and height between 40 and 300 mm. Pick **Portrait** or **Landscape**.

**Text.** The **Heading** starts as the event title. The **Badge label** (such as "ATTENDEE" or "SPEAKER") and the **Footer** each have a switch to hide them. On team events, type `{{team}}` in any of them to print the guest's team name.

**Logos.** Switch on the **Space Works symbol**, your **Organisation logo** (from organisation settings), the **Event logo** (from the event website) and **Sponsor logos** (published sponsors with a logo), with up to six sponsors on each badge.

**Show on the badge.** Switch **Attendee photo** (their profile photo, or their initials, in a **Circle** or **Rounded square**) and **Attendee email** on or off. **Shrink long names to fit** keeps long names on one line, down to 60% of the normal size.

**Registration answers.** Print answers from your sign-up form, such as company or role, under the name. Add them, put them in order with **Up** and **Down**, and give each a shorter printed label if you like. On team events you can also add **Team**.

### Arranging items

Click an item on the preview to select it, then drag it, or nudge it with the arrow keys (hold <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> for bigger steps). The right-hand panel sets its size and exact position in millimetres, aligns it, or resets it with **Reset this item**. **Snap to** sets the grid to 1, 2, 5 or 10 mm, and **Undo** and **Redo** are at the top. Switch **Preview with** to **Long name** to check that long names fit.

Every badge carries the guest's own check-in QR code, so it can be scanned at the door. Keep it at least 20 mm across so it scans reliably once printed.

## Testing and reusing the design

- **Print test card** prints a badge with sample details. Do this before the doors open.
- **Export design** saves the design as a file, and **Import design** loads one, so you can reuse a design for another event.

## Good to know

- Badges also print when you're [checking in offline](/help/check-in/offline).
- Registration answers only print if you choose them. Nothing else from the sign-up form appears on the badge.
- Badges are separate from the tickets guests download. See [The ticket designer](/help/check-in/ticket-designer).
