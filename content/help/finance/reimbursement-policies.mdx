Reimbursement policies pay back people's costs, usually travel, by stage of your event: everyone confirmed, your crew, the finalists of a judging round, or the teams mentored in a mentoring round. Each policy has its own cap per person, categories, claim window and optional budget, and people only see the policies they qualify for. They work on free and paid events alike.

<Callout kind="permission">

Creating, editing, closing and deleting policies, and approving claims under them: organisation owners and admins only.

</Callout>

## Add a policy

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Finance** tab.
2. In **Reimbursement policies**, choose **Add a policy**.
3. Give it a **Name** people will recognise, such as "Finalist travel".
4. Choose **Who can claim**:
   - **Confirmed participants**: everyone with a confirmed place who is not crew.
   - **Crew**: your event crew. Tick roles under **Which crew** (for example Volunteer) to limit it to them; leave them all unticked for everyone on the crew.
   - **Projects in a judging round**: people whose team's project (or their own, when solo) was advanced into the round you pick. Tick **Only once this round's finalists are published** to keep it closed until you announce them.
   - **Teams mentored in a round**: members of teams that had a Mentor Desk check-in in the round you pick (an "away" check-in doesn't count).
5. Enter **Most each person gets back**.
6. Tick what it covers under **What it covers**, for example Travel and Accommodation.
7. Optionally set **Claims open** and **Claims close** (in the event's time zone), a **Budget for this policy**, **Only people checked in at the event** and **Email people when they can claim**.
8. Choose **Add policy**.

</Steps>

The round pickers list judging rounds that take advanced projects and mentoring rounds that weren't skipped. Add the round first if it isn't there yet.

## What people see

On their ticket page, under **Claim expenses**, each person sees the policies open to them right now and how much is left for them: the smaller of what's left of their cap and of the policy's budget. When they send a claim, they choose which policy it is under, and the categories narrow to what that policy covers.

A claim is refused when the policy is closed or outside its window, the category isn't covered, the person doesn't qualify, or the amount would go over their cap or the policy's budget. Waiting claims count at the amount asked and approved ones at the amount approved, so two people can't both use the last of a budget.

## Approving claims under a policy

Claims under a policy appear in **Expense claims** with the policy's name. Use **Policy** above the list to show one policy's claims, or those without a policy.

When you approve, Space Works checks again that the person still qualifies, that the approval keeps them within the cap they claimed under, and that the policy stays within its budget. If something changed since they filed, for example their check-in was undone or their project was disqualified, the claim shows **Eligibility changed since submission** with the reason, and approving is refused. You can reject it instead.

Each claim keeps the policy terms it was filed under, so lowering the cap doesn't stop you approving claims filed under the higher one. When you approve, a person's total under the policy can reach the highest cap any of their approved claims was filed under, or the current cap if that is higher, whichever order you approve them in. Lowering the budget does stop approvals that would go over it.

## Close, edit or delete a policy

- **Close to new claims** stops new claims. Claims already filed can still be approved and paid. **Open again** reverses it.
- **Edit…** changes the name, cap, categories, window, budget and switches at any time. Who the policy is for is fixed once anyone has claimed under it: close it and add a new one instead.
- **Delete** appears only while nobody has claimed under the policy.

A judging round a policy uses can't be deleted until you delete that policy.

## The email

With **Email people when they can claim** on, each person gets one email, "You can claim expenses", with a link to their ticket, when the policy opens to them: as soon as it is added, when its window opens, or later when they qualify, such as when you publish finalists. Nobody gets it twice for the same policy, and it isn't sent if the policy has closed or they no longer qualify by the time it goes out.

## Good to know

- Policies sit alongside the general **Let confirmed participants claim expenses** and **Let crew claim expenses** switches under **Budget and participant claims**. Those keep working as before, and the general cap only counts claims without a policy.
- Every policy change is recorded in the audit log.
- People claim from their ticket page or the mobile app. See [Claiming expenses](/help/attending/claiming-expenses) for what they see, and [Expense claims and reimbursements](/help/finance/expense-claims) for approving and paying.
