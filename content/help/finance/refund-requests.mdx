Your refund policy decides what a buyer gets back automatically when they cancel. Sometimes people need something the policy doesn't give: a refund after the cut-off, more money back than the policy allows, or money back while keeping their tickets. A refund request lets them ask, with a reason, and lets you decide.

<Callout kind="permission">

Deciding refund requests: owners and admins of the organisation. Buyers ask from their order page; they don't need an account.

</Callout>

## How buyers ask

On the page for a paid, confirmed order, the buyer sees **Need a refund?** with a **Why you're asking** box and **Ask for a refund**. They can ask whether or not the policy would give them anything back.

Each order can have one open request at a time. While it's open, the order page tells the buyer you'll reply by email. Owners and admins get an email when a request arrives.

What buyers see is covered in [Asking for a refund](/help/attending/asking-for-a-refund).

## Deciding a request

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Finance** tab.
2. Under **Refund requests**, find the request. It shows the buyer, the order reference and total, when they asked, and their reason.
3. Choose **Refund…** or **Decline…**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot name="finance-refund-request" alt="The Refund requests list on the Finance tab with a waiting request, its reason, and the Refund and Decline buttons" />

### Refunding

In **Refund this order**:

- **Amount**: leave it blank to refund everything still refundable on the order, or enter a smaller amount for a partial refund.
- **Also cancel the tickets**: tick it if the buyer is giving up their place. Leave it unticked to refund money while they keep their tickets.
- **Note**: optional, for your own records.

Choose **Refund**. The refund runs like any other refund from the order: the money goes back the way the buyer paid, through your payment account, and team ticket rules apply. The request is marked **Refunded**, with the amount and whether the tickets were cancelled.

<Callout kind="warning">

A refund can't be undone. Check the amount before you confirm. If the refund can't go through (for example, the order has nothing left to refund), you'll see why and the request stays open.

</Callout>

### Declining

In **Decline this request**, fill in **Why (the buyer sees this)**. A reason is required. Choose **Decline**.

The buyer is emailed that their request wasn't approved, with your reason, and their order page shows it too. They can ask again from the order page if something changes.

## Request statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | Not decided yet. |
| **Refunded** | You approved it and the refund was sent. |
| **Declined** | You declined it, with a reason the buyer sees. |

A request is only decided once. If two admins act on the same request at the same moment, only the first decision counts, so a buyer is never refunded twice.

## Good to know

- Refund requests are for paid orders only. Free registrations have nothing to refund.
- Refunds you give here count against **Income** on the Finance tab and appear in its **Download CSV**. See [The Finance tab and budget](/help/finance/overview-and-budget).
- You don't have to wait for a request: you can refund any order yourself. See [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).
- If you've connected a chat channel, new refund requests can be posted under **Expense claims**. See [Chat notifications](/help/organisations/chat-notifications).
