Expense claims are how people get money back for things they paid for themselves: a volunteer's printing, a speaker's train fare, a participant's travel grant. Someone files a claim with receipts, an owner or admin approves or rejects it, and once it's paid back you record the payment. Every step is kept on record.

<Callout kind="permission">

Filing a claim: anyone who can open the event dashboard (owners, admins, members and co-organisers), and confirmed participants from their ticket page when you allow it.

Approving, rejecting and recording payments: owners and admins only.

</Callout>

## Filing a claim

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Finance** tab.
2. In **Claim expenses**, choose **What for**: Travel, Accommodation, Food, Venue, Equipment, Printing, Marketing, Supplies or Other.
3. Fill in the **Details**: where you went or what you bought, and why.
4. Enter the **Amount** in the event's currency.
5. If you're claiming for someone else, such as a speaker or volunteer, fill in **Paying back someone else?** with their name and **Their email**. Leave these blank to be paid back yourself.
6. Add up to 3 **Receipt photos** (JPEG, PNG or WebP, under 5 MB in all).
7. Choose **Send claim**.

</Steps>

Receipts are private: only the organisation's owners and admins, and the person who filed the claim, can open them.

Owners and admins get an email when a claim arrives. The person being paid back gets an email when it's approved, rejected and paid.

### Following your claims

Members and co-organisers see their claims under **Your claims** on the **Finance** tab, with a status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | Not decided yet. You can still **Withdraw** it. |
| **Approved** | Approved, for the amount asked or less. Waiting to be paid. |
| **Paid** | The money has been paid back. |
| **Rejected** | Not approved. The reason is shown under the claim. |
| **Withdrawn** | Withdrawn before a decision. |

## Deciding a claim

Owners and admins see every claim in the **Expense claims** list, newest first, with who it's for, the details and links to each receipt.

<Steps>

1. Open the receipts and check the claim.
2. Choose **Approve…** to approve it. The amount starts at what was asked; lower it to approve part of the claim. Add a **Note** if you like, then choose **Approve**.
3. Or choose **Reject…**, fill in **Why (the claimant sees this)**, and choose **Reject claim**.

</Steps>

You can't approve more than was asked. Once a claim is decided, the decision stands; it can't be changed back to waiting.

## Recording the payment

Pay the person back however you normally would, then record it.

<Steps>

1. Choose **Record payment…** on the approved claim.
2. Choose **How it was paid**: **Cash**, **UPI**, **Bank transfer**, **Card terminal** or **Other**.
3. Add a **Reference** if you have one, set **Paid on** (today or earlier), and add a **Note** if you like.
4. Choose **Record payment**.

</Steps>

The claim becomes **Paid**, and the payment appears under **Claim payments**. The amount paid is always the approved amount.

If an organiser changes the ticket holder while you record or reverse a payment, the claim and payment records stay with the original payee. Their receipts and amounts remain in Finance and the money export; the replacement holder cannot see those claims on their ticket.

### Reversing a payment

Payments can't be edited or deleted. If one was recorded by mistake, or the money came back:

<Steps>

1. Find it under **Claim payments** and choose **Reverse…**.
2. Fill in **Why**, then choose **Reverse payment**.

</Steps>

A reversal is recorded beside the original, which stays on record marked **Reversed**. The claim goes back to **Approved**, so you can record the payment again with the right details. Each payment can be reversed once.

## Letting participants claim

For travel grants or similar, you can let confirmed participants claim from their ticket page.

<Steps>

1. On the **Finance** tab, find **Budget and participant claims**.
2. Tick **Let confirmed participants claim expenses from their ticket page**.
3. Optionally set **Most each participant can claim**. Leave it blank for no limit.
4. Choose **Save**.

</Steps>

Participants then see **Claim expenses** on their ticket while their registration is confirmed. They don't name someone else to pay; the claim is always for themselves. Their claims appear in your list marked "(participant)".

The limit covers all of a participant's claims together. Waiting claims count at the amount asked, decided ones at the amount approved, and rejected or withdrawn ones don't count. A claim that would go over the limit is refused with how much is left.

What participants see is covered in [Claiming expenses](/help/attending/claiming-expenses).

## Good to know

- If you've connected a chat channel, new claims can be posted under **Expense claims** and payments under **Payouts**. See [Chat notifications](/help/organisations/chat-notifications).
- Paid claims and approved claims count against the event's budget. See [The Finance tab and budget](/help/finance/overview-and-budget#setting-a-budget).
- Every claim payment and reversal is in the Finance tab's **Download CSV**.
