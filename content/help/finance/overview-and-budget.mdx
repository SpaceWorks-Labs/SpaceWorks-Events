The **Finance** tab on the event dashboard keeps an event's money in one place: what came in from tickets and entry fees, what went out in expense claims and prize money, and how that compares with your budget. It's also where claims and refund requests are decided.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins see the whole tab: the money summary, the budget, every claim, refund requests, payments and the export.

Everyone else who can open the event dashboard (organisation members and co-organisers) sees only **Claim expenses** and **Your claims**, to file and follow their own claims. See [Expense claims and reimbursements](/help/finance/expense-claims).

</Callout>

## Reading the money summary

Open the event and choose the **Finance** tab. **Money in and out** shows four figures:

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Income** | Money collected for tickets and entry fees, less refunds. The line underneath splits it into tickets, entry fees and refunds. |
| **Expenses paid** | Expense claims you've paid back, less any reversed payments. Underneath: claims approved but not yet paid, and claims still waiting for a decision. |
| **Prize money paid** | Prize payouts to hackathon winners, less reversals. See [Prize payouts](/help/hackathons/prize-payouts). |
| **Net** | **Income** less **Expenses paid** and **Prize money paid**. It's highlighted when it goes below zero. |

Money is shown in the event's currency (or your organisation's default currency, before anything was priced).

Payments taken online while your payment account uses test keys aren't real money, so they're left out of **Income** and **Net**. A note under the summary says how many there were and their total after refunds, and the export marks their rows "(test)". Money you record as collected in person always counts.

Below the summary are the working lists:

- **Expense claims**: every claim, newest first, with its status and receipts. See [Expense claims and reimbursements](/help/finance/expense-claims).
- **Refund requests**: buyers asking for money back outside your refund policy. See [Refund requests](/help/finance/refund-requests).
- **Refundable orders**: cancelled or expired orders with money still on them, to refund what's left. See [Refunding a cancelled order](/help/payments/refunds#refunding-a-cancelled-order).
- **Claim payments**: every claim payment you've recorded, and any reversals.
- **Claim expenses**: your own claim form, the same one everyone else sees.
- **Budget and participant claims**: the settings below.

## Setting a budget

A budget is optional. It's a simple ceiling for what the event may spend on claims and prizes.

<Steps>

1. On the **Finance** tab, find **Budget and participant claims** at the bottom.
2. Enter the **Budget** in the event's currency. Leave it blank for none.
3. Choose **Save**.

</Steps>

Once set, a note under the summary compares the budget with what's spent or approved: paid claims, approved claims not yet paid, and prize money paid. It shows how much is left, or turns into **Over budget** with the amount you're over by. Claims still waiting for a decision don't count until you approve them.

<Callout kind="note">

The budget only warns you. It never stops you approving a claim or recording a payout.

</Callout>

The same card holds **Let confirmed participants claim expenses from their ticket page**, for travel grants and similar. See [Expense claims and reimbursements](/help/finance/expense-claims#letting-participants-claim).

## Downloading the money export

Choose **Download CSV** next to **Money in and out**. The file lists every money movement for the event, oldest first:

- payments in for tickets and entry fees
- refunds
- claim payments
- prize payouts
- reversals of claim payments and prize payouts

Amounts are signed from the organisation's side, so money in is positive and money out is negative. Every download is recorded in the organisation's audit log.

Use it for your accounts, or to reconcile against your payment provider and bank statements.

## Good to know

- Who may decide money is fixed: only owners and admins approve claims, record payments, decide refund requests and change the budget. There's no co-organiser permission for it.
- Every approval, rejection, payment, reversal and settings change is recorded in the audit log.
- The post-event report's money section includes spending: claims and prizes paid, approved but unpaid, the budget, and the net after spending. See [The post-event report](/help/after/event-report).
- Ticket sales in more detail, including fees and payouts from your provider, are in [Revenue and reports](/help/payments/revenue).
- The expense claims list shows the latest 200 claims. The CSV always has every payment.
