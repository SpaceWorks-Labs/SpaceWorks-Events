Once an event sells tickets, Space Works Events keeps a running account of the money: what came in, what went back, what's still owed, and where it came from. You'll find it per event, per series, and across your whole organisation.

<Callout kind="permission">

An event's or series' money: owners, admins, and co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission. The organisation-wide **Money** panel, the money summary on the **Finance** tab, and the PDF event report: owners and admins.

</Callout>

## An event's Money card

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Analytics** tab.
2. Read the **Money** card at the top, below the key numbers.
3. Choose **Download orders (CSV)** for the full list of orders.

</Steps>

The card appears once the event has taken or is owed money, or has complimentary or waiting paid orders.

<Screenshot name="analytics-money-card" alt="The Money card on the Analytics tab with collected, refunded and net totals, takings by ticket, how people paid and money in per day" />

| Figure | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Collected** | Everything buyers paid, online and in person. |
| **Refunded** | Money sent back, including refunds still on their way. |
| **Net** | Collected less refunds. |
| **Paid orders** | Orders where money was collected. |
| **Owed at the desk** | Reserved to pay in person but not yet collected, with the number of orders. |
| **Being paid now** | Orders whose buyers are at checkout, with their places held. |
| **Promo discounts** | How much promo codes took off. |
| **Booking fees** | The booking fees buyers paid on top of ticket prices. |
| **Extras** | Takings from extras, such as T-shirts or lunch. |
| **Complimentary** | Orders you waived, and what they would have cost. |
| **Requests waiting** | Paid sign-ups waiting for approval or a waitlist place. |
| **Late or duplicate, refunded** | Payments that arrived after the place had gone, or twice, and were refunded automatically. |

Figures that don't apply, such as extras on an event without any, are left out. Below them:

- **By ticket** shows each ticket's takings and how many were sold.
- **How people paid** splits the money by Razorpay, Stripe, and each in-person method such as cash or UPI.
- **Money in** charts what was collected each day over the last 14 days.

If a buyer has opened a chargeback, a red note shows how many are open and their total, to answer in your payment provider's dashboard, and any that were lost.

## A series

On a recurring series, the series page has a **Money across the series** card with the same figures for every date and pass, and its own **Download orders (CSV)**.

## Across your organisation

Owners and admins see a **Money** panel on the organisation's dashboard. It covers orders placed in the last six months, after refunds:

- **Collected**, **Refunded**, **Net** and **Paid orders**, shown separately for each currency you've sold in;
- how much is still to collect at the desk, and any open chargebacks;
- your top five events and series by net takings, each linking to its dashboard for the full breakdown.

## What isn't included

<Callout kind="note">

The amounts are what buyers paid. Your provider's own processing fees, taxes on them, and payouts to your bank aren't shown. Check those in your Razorpay or Stripe dashboard.

</Callout>

Test payments count in the totals like real ones. When every payment so far was a test, the card's title says "(test payments)" and notes that no real money moved, and the organisation panel marks its figures "(test)". To keep your real figures clean, try paid tickets out on a separate event rather than the one you're selling.

## Other places money appears

- The event's **Finance** tab sets ticket and entry-fee income against expense claims, prize money and your budget. See [The Finance tab and budget](/help/finance/overview-and-budget).
- The PDF event report, available after the event, has a **Money** section with the same figures. See [The post-event report](/help/after/event-report).
- The orders CSV has one row per order, with a **Test** column. See [Orders and receipts](/help/payments/orders-and-receipts#downloading-the-orders-list).

## Good to know

- Each event or series sells in one currency. Organisation totals are split by currency rather than converted.
- The daily chart counts money on the day it was paid. Refunds lower the totals but don't change the chart.
- Your analytics overview is in [Analytics](/help/after/analytics).
