On a team event, such as a hackathon or a game jam, you can charge for places. Either every member pays for themselves, or the team leader pays once for the whole team. Solo registrants buy their own ticket. Places are always paid for up front, before a team counts as registered.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners, admins, and co-organisers with the **Edit details** permission set up tickets and choose who pays. Refunds and payments on orders need the **Registrations** permission, or owner or admin. Team registration itself is covered in [Setting up team registration](/help/registration/team-registration).

</Callout>

## Choosing who pays

<Steps>

1. Turn on team registration for the event, as described in [Setting up team registration](/help/registration/team-registration).
2. On the event's **Settings** tab, add at least one ticket in the **Tickets** card. See [Ticket types, group tickets and add-ons](/help/payments/ticket-types).
3. In the **Who pays for teams** card that appears, choose **Every member** or **The leader, once per team**.
4. Choose **Save**.

</Steps>

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Every member** (the default) | The leader buys a ticket when creating the team, and each member buys theirs when they join with the team's join link, which shows the tickets. Solo registrants buy their own. |
| **The leader, once per team** | The leader's ticket covers the whole team. Members join free from the join link once it's paid. |

<Callout kind="warning">

**Who pays for teams** can't change once any ticket has been sold. Decide before you open registration.

</Callout>

Each ticket on a team event seats exactly one person, so group tickets aren't available. An event that sells group tickets can't be switched to team registration until they're taken off sale.

## Paid up front, never waiting

- When a leader pays to create a team, the team is held while they pay. Nobody can join it until the payment goes through. If the time to pay runs out, the team is removed and its name is free again.
- When a member pays to join (with **Every member**), their place in the team is kept while they pay, and they join once the payment lands.
- A paid place never goes on the waitlist. When the event is full, new paid teams and solo places are refused rather than waitlisted, and members can't pay to join a team that's on the waitlist. Free tickets keep the usual waitlist.
- With **Every member**, a leader can't add members by email, because members pay for themselves.
- If a place is lost while someone is paying, for example the team is rejected or removed in the meantime, their payment is refunded in full.

## Approval-required team events

On an event that approves each sign-up, team places are paid online first and then wait for your approval. Paying at the desk isn't available for them.

- **Approving** or promoting a team changes no money.
- **Rejecting** a team refunds every paid order of its members in full.
- **Declining** a paid solo registration refunds it in full.

See [Managing teams](/help/guests/managing-teams).

## Cancellations and refunds

Paid registrations are cancelled as an order, from the buyer's order page within your refund policy, or by you from **Payment** on the guest's row in the **Guests** tab. See [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).

- A cancelled member leaves their team, as if they had cancelled.
- With **The leader, once per team**, cancelling the leader's order cancels the whole team, and the members are emailed. The buyer sees this warning on their order page before they confirm.
- A team ticket can't be cancelled once anyone in the team has checked in.

To protect people who have paid:

- Leaders can't remove paid members or disband a team that has paid members.
- Organisers can't delete a team with paid members. Cancel or refund their orders instead.
- Members who paid and want to leave are pointed to their order page.

## Good to know

- Payments and cancellations on team events trigger the usual registration webhooks, if your organisation uses them.
- Hackathon project entry fees are separate from team places: see [Project entry fees](/help/hackathons/entry-fees).
- For what guests see when they register as a team, see [Registering as a team](/help/attending/teams).
