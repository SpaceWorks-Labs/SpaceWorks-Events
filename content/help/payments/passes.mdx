A pass is one purchase that gives a guest a place on every remaining date of a recurring series: a season ticket for a monthly meetup, or a whole course of weekly classes. Guests who only want one date can still buy a ticket for that date alone.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners, admins, and co-organisers with the **Edit details** permission on the series. Paid passes also need paid events turned on and a connected payment account: see [Turning on paid events](/help/payments/enable-paid-events).

</Callout>

## Adding a pass

Passes belong to a series, not to a single event. Set them up on the series page.

<Steps>

1. Open the series from your organisation's dashboard.
2. In the **Tickets** card, choose **Add ticket**.
3. Choose **Pass for every remaining date**.
4. Enter a **Ticket name**, such as "Season pass", a price, and any other settings you need.
5. Set the **Limit** to the number of passes you want to sell, or leave it blank.
6. Choose **Add ticket**.

</Steps>

The choice between **One date** and **Pass for every remaining date** is only offered when you first add the ticket; it can't be changed afterwards. Passes show a **Pass** chip in the ticket list.

Everything else works as it does for event tickets: fixed or pay-what-you-want prices, booking fees, sales windows, per-order limits, access codes and members-only passes. See [Ticket types, group tickets and add-ons](/help/payments/ticket-types). The series' extras, promo codes, refund policy and transfer setting cover its passes and single-date tickets alike.

## How a pass works

- A pass covers every date of the series that hasn't ended and hasn't been cancelled when it's bought. On the registration form, buyers see "Pass for all" and the number of remaining dates.
- A pass takes a place on each of those dates, so each date's capacity still applies. The pass's **Limit** counts passes, not places.
- Each pass holder gets a ticket for every covered date. Their ticket page lists their other dates, and they check in at each date as usual.
- One order is either passes or single-date tickets, never both. Buyers who want both place two orders.

<Callout kind="tip">

Start selling passes early. The fewer dates are left, the less a pass covers, but its price stays the same unless you change it. You can lower the price, or take the pass off sale, part way through the season.

</Callout>

## Cancelling a date

Owners and admins can cancel one date of a series from **Upcoming dates** on the series page, with **Cancel date**. When they do:

- tickets for that date alone are refunded in full;
- each pass is refunded that date's share of what's left on it, and keeps its other dates;
- everyone on that date is emailed.

For example, a pass that cost 1,000 and covers five dates gets back 200 when one of them is cancelled, as long as nothing has been refunded on it yet. Cancelling the same date again never refunds a pass twice. If the pass holder already checked in to that date, the pass is left as it is for you to settle by hand. See [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).

## Refunding a pass

A pass is one order, so it's refunded like any other order: in full or in part from the guest list, or by the buyer under your refund policy. Cancelling the order gives up every date it covers. See [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).

## Revenue

The series page shows a **Money across the series** card with what was collected, refunded and the net, including passes, with **Download orders (CSV)**. See [Revenue and reports](/help/payments/revenue).

## Good to know

- Passes are for series only. On a standalone event, the option doesn't appear.
- If you allow transfers, a series' transfer cut-off applies before each date's start.
- Taking a pass off sale stops new sales; existing pass holders keep all their dates.
- For how series and their dates work, see [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
