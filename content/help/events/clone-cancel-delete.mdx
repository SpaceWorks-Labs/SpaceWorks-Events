When an event goes well, you'll want to run it again. When plans change, you may need to call it off. This page covers copying an event as a starting point, cancelling one (which refunds and tells everyone), and deleting an event for good.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation. All three live in the event's **Settings** tab.

</Callout>

## Clone an event

Cloning makes a new draft event with the same core information, so you don't start from scratch.

<Steps>

1. Open the event you want to copy and choose the **Settings** tab.
2. In **Run it again**, choose **Clone event**.
3. The app opens the new event's dashboard. Its title ends in *(copy)*.
4. Go to **Event page** → **Details**, update the title, dates and anything else that's different, and save.

</Steps>

The copy is always a **Draft**, so nothing goes public until you publish it. You need a verified email address to clone.

### What's copied

- Title (with *(copy)* added) and cover image
- Start, end and time zone
- Location, map link and whether it's online
- Capacity, who can find it and whether you approve sign-ups
- Team registration settings
- The **Hackathon emails** switches (results emails and submission receipts), and **Free the place when a guest says No** for attendance confirmation

### What isn't copied

- Guests, invites, orders, check-ins, feedback and expense claims
- The event page content: the formatted introduction, tagline, logo, accent colour, sections, highlights, speakers, sponsors, resources, FAQs and the programme
- Tickets, prices, extras, promo codes, the refund policy and the ticket design
- Registration form questions, emails, the feedback form and certificate
- Hackathon submissions and judging settings
- Co-organisers, tags, the short link, the sign-up closing rule and the budget

<Callout kind="tip">

The copy keeps the original dates, which are often in the past, so set new dates first. You'll also need to fill in **About the event** again before you can save the Details form.

</Callout>

## Cancel an event

Cancelling calls the event off for good: registration closes, everyone who paid gets their money back, and everyone with a place is told.

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Settings** tab.
2. In **Cancel event**, choose **Cancel event…**.
3. Check the message, then choose **Cancel event and refund**.

</Steps>

When you confirm:

- The event is marked **Cancelled** and nobody can register.
- Everyone with a place, on the waitlist, waiting for approval or still paying gets an email saying the event has been cancelled.
- Every paid order is refunded in full, back the way the buyer paid, and its tickets are cancelled. Buyers get a separate email about their refund; card and UPI refunds can take 5 to 10 working days to appear.
- Orders still being paid for are released, and orders waiting for your approval are cancelled.
- If your organisation has [chat notifications](/help/organisations/chat-notifications) connected, the chat is told too.

A message tells you how many orders were refunded and how many guests were emailed. If any refund couldn't go through, it says how many need settling by hand; you'll find them in the guest list. See [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).

<Callout kind="warning">

Cancelling can't be undone. If you're only moving the date or venue, change it in **Event page** → **Details** instead: guests with a place are emailed the new time and place automatically, and keep their places. See [Event details](/help/events/details#when-guests-are-told).

</Callout>

## Delete an event

<Callout kind="warning">

Deleting is permanent. The event and all of its registrations, check-ins, feedback and page content are removed, and it can't be undone.

</Callout>

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Settings** tab.
2. In **Delete event**, choose **Delete event…**.
3. Type the event's address (the last part of its link, shown in **Event address**) to unlock the button.
4. Choose **Delete event**.

</Steps>

The app takes you back to your organisation's dashboard. The event's link and short link stop working.

If the event is live and hasn't ended yet, everyone with a place is emailed that it has been cancelled before it's deleted. Draft, offline and finished events are deleted without emailing anyone.

You can't delete an event that people have paid for. Cancel it instead, so they're refunded; the cancelled event can then stay on your dashboard as a record.

## Which should I use?

- **Running it again next month or next year?** Clone it, or for a regular meetup, use a [recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
- **New date or venue?** Edit the details; guests are told automatically.
- **Not going ahead?** Cancel it. Everyone is told and paid orders are refunded.
- **Not ready to show it yet, or need it hidden for a while?** Take it offline in **Publishing**. Guests keep their places and aren't emailed. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).
- **Created by mistake and nobody has paid?** Delete it.
- **Finished normally?** Leave it. Past events stay on your dashboard under **Past**, with their analytics and report.
