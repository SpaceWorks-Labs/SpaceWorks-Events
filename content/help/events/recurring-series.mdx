A recurring series is for an event that repeats on a schedule, such as a weekly meetup, a monthly book club or fortnightly office hours. You set it up once, and the app creates each date for you. Every date has its own page and its own guest list, so people sign up for the dates they can make.

For a one-off event, or one that repeats only now and then, use a normal event and [clone it](/help/events/clone-cancel-delete) instead. Series are for individual sign-ups only; team registration, hackathon submissions and judging need a normal event.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins create, edit and delete series, and change or cancel their dates. You also need a verified email address to create one.

</Callout>

## Create a series

<Steps>

1. Open your organisation's dashboard and choose **New series**.
2. Enter a **Title** and, if you like, a **Description**.
3. Set the **First occurrence start** and **First occurrence end**. Every date lasts as long as this first one.
4. Enter the **Recurrence (RRULE)**: the repeat pattern (see below).
5. Choose the **Timezone**, and a **Capacity per occurrence** if you want to limit places on each date.
6. Choose a **Status** (**Draft** or **Published**) and an **Audience**.
7. Choose **Create**.

</Steps>

The app opens the series page, with the dates it created listed under **Upcoming dates**.

## Writing the repeat pattern

The **Recurrence (RRULE)** field uses a standard calendar format. Type the rule without `RRULE:` at the start. Some common patterns:

| Pattern | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Every week, 12 times | `FREQ=WEEKLY;COUNT=12` |
| Every two weeks | `FREQ=WEEKLY;INTERVAL=2` |
| Every month on the same date | `FREQ=MONTHLY` |
| Every day, 5 times | `FREQ=DAILY;COUNT=5` |

Without a day in the rule, the series repeats on the same day and time as the first occurrence, which is usually what you want. Use `COUNT=` to stop after a number of dates, or `UNTIL=` with a date to stop on a day.

The app creates dates from now onwards, up to 48 of them within the next year. Dates before today are skipped. Each date keeps the same local clock time in the series' time zone, even across a summer-time change; a date whose time doesn't exist that day, because the clocks skip forward through it, is left out.

## Status and audience

- **Status**: **Draft** keeps the series to your team. **Published** opens every date for sign-ups.
- **Audience**: **Public** lists the series on your organisation page and Discover; **Hidden link** only works for people with the link; **Approval required** means you approve each sign-up; **Invite only** limits it to people on the series' invite list.

These apply to every date in the series.

## Manage a series

Open a series from **Series** on your organisation's dashboard. The series page has:

- **Series details**: change the title, description, time zone, capacity per occurrence, status and audience, then choose **Save**.
- **Invites**: the email list for invite-only series. Everyone on it can sign up for any date.
- **Schedule**: a simple running order shared by the dates.
- **Upcoming dates**: each date with how many places are taken, and **View** to open its public page. Share these links for people to sign up.
- **Tickets and passes**: tickets, extras, promo codes, transfers and the refund policy for the whole series. A pass covers several dates in one purchase. See [Passes](/help/payments/passes).
- **Money across the series**: once anything has been sold.
- **Reports**: attendance across the dates, and a PDF report for each date once it has finished.
- **Attendees (next date)**: the guest list for the next date, where you can approve, decline and manage guests.

<Callout kind="note">

The repeat pattern can't be changed after the series is created; **Series details** shows it for reference. To change it, create a new series. Changing the time zone later doesn't move the dates that already exist.

</Callout>

### Change one date's capacity

Choose **Capacity** on a date, enter the **Places on this date** and choose **Save**. Leave it blank to use the series capacity again. A date with its own limit shows "this date's own limit".

### Cancel one date

<Steps>

1. Under **Upcoming dates**, choose **Cancel date** on the date.
2. Optionally add a **Reason, for the email**.
3. Choose **Cancel this date and refund**.

</Steps>

Everyone on that date is emailed. Tickets for that date alone are refunded in full; each pass is refunded that date's share and keeps its other dates. This can't be undone. See [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).

If processing stops before all refunds are recorded, choose **Finish refunds** beside the cancelled date and confirm. Cancelled dates stay reachable under **Upcoming dates** or **Past cancelled dates**, even after their end time. Orders already processed are skipped and cancellation emails are not sent again. You cannot cancel a date for the first time after it has ended.

## Delete a series

<Callout kind="warning">

Deleting a series removes it and every date it created, with all their registrations. It can't be undone.

</Callout>

<Steps>

1. Open the series page.
2. Under **Series details**, choose **Delete series**.
3. Choose **Delete series** again to confirm.

</Steps>

If the series is published, everyone booked on an upcoming date is emailed that it has been cancelled. You can't delete a series while it holds collected money, including payments for earlier attended dates, or has unpaid orders or pending refunds to resolve. Cancelling the series closes all upcoming dates together and queues their cancellation emails; it does not refund earlier attended dates. Keep the series cancelled as a record if you are retaining that revenue.

To delete it, resolve unpaid orders, refund every collected payment, retry failed refunds and wait for pending refunds to finish. Use each date's guest list for attended orders and **Refundable orders** on the series page for cancelled orders. After deletion, fully refunded orders and refunds remain in organisation totals and buyer receipts, but organiser finance and order exports become unavailable. Receipts lose the series title, date and time zone. Download your orders CSV first, or keep the series to preserve its purchase context. See [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds#good-to-know).

## Good to know

- Capacity applies to each date separately. You can't set the series capacity lower than the number of people already confirmed on any date that uses it.
- Guests register for one date at a time and get a ticket for that date, unless they buy a pass.
