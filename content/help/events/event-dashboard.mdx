Each event has its own dashboard where you do everything for that event, from building the page to running the door and seeing how it went. Open it by choosing an event on your organisation's dashboard.

What you see depends on your role. Owners and admins see everything. Members and co-organisers see the parts their permissions allow, with a short note where they don't have permission. A co-organiser who is only a judge sees the judging workspace and nothing else. See [Roles and permissions](/help/organisations/roles) and [Event co-organisers](/help/organisations/co-organisers).

## The header

The top of the dashboard shows the event's date, title, time and place, and three labels:

- **Status**: **Draft**, **Published** (live), **Hidden** (offline) or **Cancelled**.
- **Audience**: **Public**, **Hidden link**, **Approval required** or **Invite only**.
- **When**: how soon the event is, such as *Tomorrow*.

Beside them are the main actions:

- **View page** opens the public event page. Owners, admins and co-organisers who can edit the page or the programme can preview it while it's still a draft.
- The link button copies the event's link, once the event is live.
- **Open check-in** opens the check-in desk, for people with check-in access.
- **Download PDF report** appears for owners and admins once the event has ended. See [The post-event report](/help/after/event-report).

Owners, admins and co-organisers with **Registrations** also see live numbers under the title: **Going**, **Waitlist**, **To approve** and **Checked in**. On team events, **Places taken** counts teams and solo registrants instead of **Going**.

## The tabs

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | See what's left to do, recent sign-ups, approvals waiting, sharing links and the event at a glance. |
| **Guests** | Work the guest list in **People**, manage **Teams** on team events, and send **Invites**. See [The guest list](/help/guests/guest-list). |
| **Event page** | Edit the **Details** (title, dates, location, cover and publishing), the **Page & programme**, and the **Registration form**. |
| **Emails** | Write to your guests and see what's been sent. See [Emailing your guests](/help/guests/emailing-guests). |
| **Check-in** | Run the door: the check-in desk, the door station, check-in lists and badges. See [The check-in desk](/help/check-in/check-in-desk). |
| **Judging** | Only on hackathons, once submissions are on. Submissions, judges, scoring, results, awards and, for owners and admins, **Prizes** for prize payouts. See [Running a hackathon](/help/hackathons/overview) and [Prize payouts](/help/hackathons/prize-payouts). |
| **Feedback** | Build the feedback form, read responses and design certificates. See [Feedback forms](/help/after/feedback). |
| **Analytics** | Your registration funnel and turnout, and the money on paid events. See [Analytics](/help/after/analytics). |
| **Finance** | Money in and out against a budget, expense claims and refund requests. See [The Finance tab and budget](/help/finance/overview-and-budget). |
| **Settings** | Tickets and prices, co-organisers, the event address, cloning, embedding, cancelling and deleting. |

A number on the **Guests** tab shows how many sign-ups are waiting for your approval.

The address bar remembers which tab is open, so you can bookmark a tab or send a teammate a link straight to it.

## The Overview tab

### Readiness checklist

Before the event, a checklist shows what's done and what's left, with a button beside each open item:

- **Not published yet**: **Publish event** makes it live in one click.
- **No cover image**: **Add one** opens the Details form. A page accent colour also ticks this off.
- **No programme yet**: **Add sessions** opens **Page & programme**.
- **Registration form**: shows how many questions you ask. Name and email are always collected.
- **No check-in station PIN** (in-person events): **Set up** opens the **Check-in** tab. See [The door station](/help/check-in/door-station).
- **Waiting for approval**: **Review** opens the guests who need a decision.
- **No email sent yet**: **Write one** opens **Emails**.

You only see the items you can act on. While the event is running, the heading changes to *Happening now*. Once it has ended, the checklist is replaced by *This event has ended*, with **See how it went** linking to **Analytics**.

### The other cards

- **Sign-ups**: sign-ups per day over the last 14 days, and how this week compares with the week before.
- **Waiting for you**: people asking to join, when the event needs approval.
- **Share**: the event link, the short link and **Embed on a website**. See [Sharing your event](/help/events/sharing).
- **At a glance**: when, where, who can join, capacity and status, with **Edit details**.
- **Running this event**: the organisation admins and co-organisers, with **Manage co-organisers**.

## The Finance tab

Owners and admins see the event's money in and out, the budget, expense claims and refund requests. Everyone else who can open the dashboard sees **Claim expenses** and their own claims. See [Expense claims and reimbursements](/help/finance/expense-claims).

## The Settings tab

<Callout kind="permission">

Ticket cards: owners, admins and co-organisers with **Edit details**. Everything else: owners and admins.

</Callout>

- **Tickets**, **Extras**, **Promo codes**, **Ticket transfers** and **Refund policy**: what people pay and their options. See [Ticket types, group tickets and add-ons](/help/payments/ticket-types) and [Refunds and cancellations](/help/payments/refunds).
- **Who pays for teams**: on team events with tickets. See [Paid team places](/help/payments/paid-team-places).
- **Ticket design**: how tickets look. See [The ticket designer](/help/check-in/ticket-designer).
- **Project entry fee**: on hackathons with submissions on. See [Project entry fees](/help/hackathons/entry-fees).
- **Co-organisers**: invite people to help with this event.
- **Project submissions**: on events without judging, turn on hackathon submissions, which adds the **Judging** tab.
- **Event address**: change the end of the event's link. See [Sharing your event](/help/events/sharing#change-the-event-address).
- **Run it again**, **Cancel event** and **Delete event**: see [Cloning, cancelling and deleting](/help/events/clone-cancel-delete).
- **Put it on your website**: copy a register button or the embeddable event page. See [Sharing your event](/help/events/sharing#put-it-on-your-website).

## Good to know

- Organisation staff and co-organisers need an authenticator app and pass a check each session before the dashboard opens. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).
- Recurring series have their own page instead of this dashboard. See [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
