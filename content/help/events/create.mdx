Every event starts in the **New event** dialog: three short steps for the basics, the time and the place. Space Works Events saves the event as a draft, so nobody can see it or sign up until you're ready to publish.

For a meetup that repeats on a schedule, create a [recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series) instead.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation. You also need a verified email address.

</Callout>

## Create an event

<Steps>

1. Open your organisation's dashboard and choose **New event**. On a phone, tap the round **+** button at the bottom of the screen.
2. **Basics**: enter a **Title** and write **About the event**. Add **Tags** if you like, then choose **Next**.
3. **Schedule**: pick the **Start** and **End**, and the **Timezone** they're in. Choose **Next**.
4. **Location & sign-ups**: enter the **Location**, or turn on **Online event** and paste the meeting link. Decide whether people register in teams.
5. Choose **Create**.

</Steps>

The app opens the new event's dashboard. You can go **Back** between steps without losing what you typed, but closing the dialog with **Cancel** discards it.

## Step 1: Basics

- **Title**: required, up to 200 characters. The app also turns it into the event's web address, which you can change later in the **Settings** tab.
- **About the event**: required. Use the toolbar for headings, lists and links. This becomes the introduction on your event page.
- **Tags**: optional words that help people find the event in search, such as *workshop* or *meetup*. Press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> or type a comma after each one. You can add up to 12.

## Step 2: Schedule

The start is filled in with a whole hour at least half an hour from now, and the end an hour after it. When you change the start, the end moves to one hour after it, so set the start first.

- The start must be now or in the future, and the end must be after the start.
- **Timezone** is the clock your start and end times are on. Guests see times in the event's time zone, so choose the zone where the event happens, especially for in-person events.

If the app says a time doesn't exist in your time zone, it falls in the hour the clocks skip forward. Pick a different time.

## Step 3: Location and sign-ups

- **Location**: the venue or street address, at least 3 characters.
- **Online event**: turn it on for a virtual event. The field changes to **Online meeting link**, which must be a full web address starting with `https://` or `http://`. The event page shows "Online event"; guests get the link on their ticket.
- **Map link**: optional, for in-person events. Paste a Google Maps link so guests can open directions.
- **Team registration**: turn it on for hackathons, game jams and competitions where people sign up in teams. Set the **Smallest team** and **Largest team**, and answer **Can people sign up without a team?**. See [Setting up team registration](/help/registration/team-registration).

## How a new event starts

A new event is a **Draft**: only your team can see it and nobody can sign up. It starts with these settings, which you can change in **Event page** → **Details** before you publish:

- **Who can find it**: everyone (listed publicly once it's live).
- **Spots**: unlimited.
- **Sign-ups**: open until the event ends.
- **Show how many are going**: on.
- **Tickets**: none, so registration is free. Owners, admins and co-organisers with **Edit details** can add tickets in the **Settings** tab. See [Ticket types, group tickets and add-ons](/help/payments/ticket-types).

## What to do next

The event's **Overview** tab shows a checklist of what's left. Most organisers:

1. Add a cover image and check the details. See [Event details](/help/events/details) and [The cover photo editor](/help/events/cover-photo).
2. Build the page and programme, if you want more than the introduction. See [Building your event page](/help/event-page/building-your-page).
3. Add questions to the registration form, if you need more than name and email. See [The registration form](/help/registration/form).
4. Decide who can find the event and how sign-ups work, then publish. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).
5. Share the link. See [Sharing your event](/help/events/sharing).

## Good to know

- If you see "Verify your email before creating events", verify your address first. See [Creating an account and signing in](/help/account/sign-up-and-sign-in).
- To run an event again, clone it instead of starting from scratch. See [Cloning, cancelling and deleting](/help/events/clone-cancel-delete).
- Organisation staff need an authenticator app and pass a check each session before the dashboard opens. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).
