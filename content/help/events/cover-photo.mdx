The cover photo editor opens whenever you add a cover image to an event. Use it to frame your photo for the wide banner on the event page, straighten it, and tweak the colours before anyone sees it. Your original photo is never changed.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins, from **Event page** → **Details**. You also need a verified email address to upload a cover.

</Callout>

## Add or change a cover

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose **Event page** → **Details**.
2. In the **Cover image** card, choose **Choose image**, drag a photo onto the box, or copy a photo and paste it anywhere on the page.
3. The **Edit cover photo** editor opens. Position and adjust the photo (see below).
4. Choose **Apply**.
5. Choose **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom of the page.

</Steps>

The cover isn't live until you save the Details form. Use a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo up to 5 MB.

To change a cover later, choose **Edit** (or click the cover itself) to re-crop it, **Replace** to pick a different one, or **Remove** to take it off.

## Position the photo

The photo always fills the frame, so there are never empty edges.

- **Move**: drag the photo. With the keyboard, use the arrow keys; hold <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> to move further.
- **Zoom**: scroll, pinch, use the slider under the photo, or the **Zoom out** and **Zoom in** buttons. You can zoom up to four times. On the keyboard, press <Kbd>+</Kbd> or <Kbd>-</Kbd>.

### Shape

Pick the **Shape** at the top of the controls:

- **Event page banner** (16:7): the default, and the right choice for most covers.
- **Wide** (16:9).
- **Square** (1:1).

## Crop tools

On the **Crop** tab:

- **Rotate left** and **Rotate right** turn the photo a quarter turn.
- **Flip** mirrors it left to right.
- **Straighten** tilts it up to 15° either way, to level a wonky horizon.
- **Show other crops** draws outlines showing what other places, such as event cards and social shares, will keep of your photo. Keep faces and text inside them.
- **Reset crop** undoes all of the above.

## Adjust the colours

On the **Adjust** tab:

- **Looks**: one-tap styles: **Original**, **Vivid**, **Warm**, **Cool**, **Mono** and **Soft**.
- **Brightness**, **Contrast** and **Saturation** sliders for finer changes.
- **Reset adjustments** puts the colours back.

## Check where it appears

**Where it appears** shows your photo as it will look in the **Event page header**, on an **Event card**, and as a **Social share** preview. They update as you edit.

Along the bottom, the editor shows the final size in pixels, the file type and the file size.

## Finish or cancel

- **Apply** keeps your edits and puts the new cover into the form.
- **Cancel**, or the close button, leaves the editor. If you've made changes, the app asks whether to **Discard edits** or **Keep editing**.

If the edited photo comes out larger than 5 MB, the editor says so when you choose **Apply**. Try a simpler photo or a different shape.

## Good to know

- The app checks and resizes every cover when you save, and removes hidden details such as camera and location data from the file.
- If your browser can't open the current cover for editing, the app asks you to upload the original photo again.
- A cover isn't required. Without one, the event page starts with the title, and link previews use a generated card instead. You can still give the page some colour with an [accent colour](/help/events/details#accent-colour).
- Uploads are limited for a short while if you upload many images quickly. Wait a moment and try again.
