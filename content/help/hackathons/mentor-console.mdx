As a mentor you see the event's mentor console and nothing else: the help queue, the teams you look after, your office hours, round visits and your profile.

<Callout kind="permission">

Mentors: people invited to the event with the **Mentor** permission, after setting up an authenticator app.

</Callout>

## Your profile and shifts

Open **Profile** to set your skills, bio and availability. You can only claim help and visit teams while you're inside one of your availability windows. Your affiliation is private to you and the organisers.

## Helping teams

<Steps>

1. Open **My queue**. Requests you can take have a **Claim** button; greyed-out ones need another mentor (a skill you don't have, or a team's own mentor gets a few minutes first).
2. Choose **Claim**, then head over to the team.
3. Use **More time** if you need longer, message the team, and choose **Resolved** when you're done, or **Release** to hand it back.

</Steps>

You hold one request at a time. If you leave a claim, it lapses and returns to the queue.

You can also **Raise a request for a team** you're with.

## Your teams and conflicts

**My teams** lists the teams the organisers assigned to you. If you have a conflict of interest with a team (a friend, a colleague), declare it there: you won't get its requests, assignments or visits. Only the organisers can clear a conflict they recorded.

## Office hours

Under **My office hours**, add slots inside your availability. Teams book them from their team page and you both get a calendar invite. Cancelling a slot needs a reason and tells the booked teams. If you also volunteer, teams can't book you during your approved shifts, and a booked slot can't be moved onto one.

## Round visits

When the organisers open a mentoring round you're part of, **Round visits** lists the teams.

<Steps>

1. Choose **Start visit** on a team. Nobody else can visit it while you're there.
2. Fill in the check-in form, or tick **The team was away**.
3. Optionally **Nominate this team**, then choose **Submit check-in**.

</Steps>

Each team gets one check-in per round, and you don't visit the same team twice across rounds unless the organisers allow it and you give a reason. Nominations are a private signal for the organisers and never change judging scores.
