When an event runs a Mentor Desk, you can ask its mentors for help during the event and, as a team, book time with them.

## Asking for help

<Steps>

1. Open your **team page** (the private link your team leader got by email), or sign in and choose **Get help** on your ticket.
2. Under **Get help from a mentor**, choose what you need help with, describe the problem and say where you are.
3. Choose **Ask for help**.

</Steps>

Your request shows **Waiting** until a mentor picks it up, then **Mentor on the way**. You can add a message, or **Cancel request** if you sorted it out. Once it's resolved, rate how it went from 1 to 5.

<Callout kind="note">

Only your team, the mentors and the organisers see what you wrote. A ticket link alone can't ask for help: sign in with the account you registered with, or use your team page.

</Callout>

A team can have only a few waiting requests in each category, and a request closes on its own if nobody picks it up in time or when the event ends.

## Booking office hours

Teams can book time with a mentor under **Mentors and office hours**.

<Steps>

1. Pick an open slot and choose **Book**.
2. Everyone in your team and the mentor get an email with a calendar invite.

</Steps>

You can't book two slots at the same time, and you can cancel a booking until the slot starts. After that, ask the organisers.

## Good to know

- The mentors listed on the event page chose to be listed.
- Asking for help is free: it never adds anything to your ticket.
