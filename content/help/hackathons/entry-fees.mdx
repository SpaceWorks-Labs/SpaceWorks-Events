A project entry fee is charged once per entrant, when they submit their project. On a team event the team pays once, through whoever submits; on an individual event each entrant pays for their own project. It's separate from ticket prices, so you can run a free event with a paid competition, or charge for both.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation, and co-organisers with the **Edit details** permission (the same people who manage ticket prices).

</Callout>

## Before you start

Entry fees are paid through the same checkout as paid tickets, straight into your organisation's own payment account. You need:

- paid events turned on for your installation, and
- a Razorpay or Stripe account connected by your organisation's owner in **Settings** → **Payments**.

Until both are in place, the card explains what's missing. See [Turning on paid events](/help/payments/enable-paid-events) and [Connecting Razorpay or Stripe](/help/payments/connect-a-provider).

## Setting the fee

<Steps>

1. Turn on project submissions first. See [Running a hackathon](/help/hackathons/overview#turning-on-submissions).
2. Open the event's **Settings** tab and find the **Project entry fee** card, next to your ticket settings.
3. Enter the **Fee** in the event's currency. Leave it blank for free entry.
4. Leave **Refund the fee in full when a project is disqualified** ticked, or untick it if fees are kept on disqualification.
5. Choose **Save**.

</Steps>

Say on your event page what the fee is for and whether it's refundable, so entrants aren't surprised at the deadline.

## What entrants see

When a fee is set and the entrant hasn't paid it, their project form says what submitting costs, and the submit button reads **Pay** followed by the fee **and submit**.

<Steps>

1. The entrant fills in their project and chooses to pay and submit.
2. The project is saved as a draft and the payment page opens.
3. Once the payment lands, the project is submitted and whoever submitted gets the usual "project received" email.

</Steps>

The payment page holds the order for 30 minutes. If the entrant comes back to it within that time, the same order is reused. A payment that lands later is still accepted, because no places are being held.

The entrants' side is covered in [Submitting a hackathon project](/help/attending/submitting-a-project) and [Paying for a ticket](/help/attending/paying-for-tickets).

## Withdrawing, disqualifying and refunds

- **Withdrawn and submitted again.** A project that's already paid for stays paid. The entrant isn't charged twice.
- **Refunded in full.** If you refund the fee in full, submitting again asks for it again.
- **Paid twice.** If a second payment lands after the fee was already paid on another order, the extra payment is refunded in full.
- **Disqualified.** When **Refund the fee in full when a project is disqualified** is ticked, disqualifying a paid project refunds its fee in full. Reinstating the project charges nothing.
- **Event cancelled.** Cancelling the event refunds entry fees along with ticket payments.

<Callout kind="warning">

A disqualification refund goes out straight away, and reinstating the project doesn't take the money back. Check the setting on the **Project entry fee** card before you disqualify.

</Callout>

## Where the money shows up

Entry fees are orders with no tickets attached. They appear:

- in the orders export and your revenue reports, like ticket orders. See [Revenue and reports](/help/payments/revenue).
- on the event's **Finance** tab, as **entry fees** under **Income**. See [The Finance tab and budget](/help/finance/overview-and-budget).

## Good to know

- Adding or changing the fee doesn't charge entrants who have already paid, or projects that are already submitted.
- Entrants need an email address on their registration to pay the fee.
- Entry fees and prize money are separate. Prizes for winners are recorded on **Judging** → **Prizes**. See [Prize payouts](/help/hackathons/prize-payouts).
