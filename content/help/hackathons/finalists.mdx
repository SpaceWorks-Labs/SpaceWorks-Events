Finalists are the projects that move on from one judging round to the next. You choose them from the previous round's ranking, then announce them on the results page, and by email if you want, before the winners are known.

<Callout kind="permission">

Advancing and publishing finalists: owners and admins of the organisation.

</Callout>

## Before you start

You need a round that takes **Finalists advanced from the previous round**, and the round before it must be closed and finalised. See [Judging in rounds](/help/hackathons/rounds).

## Advancing finalists

<Steps>

1. Open **Judging** → **Rounds** and select the round that takes finalists.
2. Choose **Advance finalists**.
3. Choose **Top of the ranking** and enter the **Number of finalists**, or choose **Pick by hand**.
4. Choose **Preview**. Nothing changes yet: you see which projects would go through and their rank in the previous round.
5. For **Pick by hand**, tick or untick projects in the list the preview loaded, then preview again.
6. Choose **Advance finalists**.

</Steps>

**Top of the ranking** takes the highest-ranked projects that have a score. If the next project below the cut has the same score as your last finalist, the preview warns you, so you can pick by hand and decide what to do about the tie.

<Callout kind="note">

Disqualified and withdrawn projects never advance. If a finalist is disqualified later, it simply drops out of the round.

</Callout>

If someone changes scores or projects between your preview and your choice, the app asks you to preview again rather than advance a list you haven't seen.

You can advance again to change the list as often as you like until the round opens. Once it opens, its finalists are fixed and judges in that round see only them.

## Announcing finalists

When you're happy with the list, choose **Publish finalists** on the round. This is separate from publishing results:

- The event's results page shows a **Finalists** block with each finalist's project title, team or entrant name and links. It never shows scores or ranks. The page appears as soon as finalists are published, even before the results.
- If **Email entrants when results are published** is on in **Judging** → **Setup**, each finalist's confirmed team members (or the solo entrant) get one email saying they're through. Someone behind more than one finalist project gets a single email listing them all. With that switch off, nobody is emailed.

Nobody gets the same round's announcement twice. To change the finalists after publishing, choose **Unpublish finalists**, advance again, then publish. Emails that hadn't gone out yet are withdrawn when you unpublish, and only those people are emailed when you publish again.

## Winners come from the final round

Once a final round of finalists has been judged, the **Results** ranking, the award picker and the export all use that round, and only its finalists can win an award. Advancing finalists into the final round takes any award winner who isn't among them off their award, and the preview warns you first. Publishing results waits until every round has been judged and the final round is finalised, or you delete the rounds you won't use. See [Awards and results](/help/hackathons/results).
