The Mentor Desk helps teams get help from mentors during your event. Teams ask for help and a mentor on shift picks the request up; you can also assign mentors to teams, let teams book office hours, and run mentoring rounds where mentors check in on every team.

<Callout kind="permission">

Turning the Mentor Desk on and running it: owners and admins of the organisation. Mentors are invited as co-organisers with the **Mentor** permission and only ever see their own mentor console.

</Callout>

## Turning it on

<Steps>

1. Open the event and choose the **Settings** tab.
2. In **Mentor Desk**, turn on **Turn on the Mentor Desk** and choose **Save**.
3. Optionally turn on **Email the team when a mentor picks up their request** (once per request) and **Give mentors a crew ticket**.

</Steps>

A **Mentors** tab appears on the event. Turning the desk off later hides it again and stops all mentor access; nothing is deleted.

<Callout kind="note">

While the Mentor Desk is on, a judge who mentored a team, or has a conflict with it, never judges that team's project, in any round.

</Callout>

## Inviting mentors

<Steps>

1. In **Settings** → **Co-organisers**, invite each mentor by email and give them only the **Mentor** permission.
2. When they accept, they appear under **Mentors** → **Mentors**.
3. Open **Edit profile** to set their skills, a short bio, a private affiliation, the most teams they look after at once and their availability.

</Steps>

Mentors must set up an authenticator app, like all organisers. Outside their availability windows they are *off shift*: they can still edit their profile, but they can't claim help or visit teams. With no windows at all they are off duty.

Turn on **Show on the event page** to list a mentor in the event page's **Mentors** section, with their name, bio and skills. Their affiliation and email are never shown.

With **Give mentors a crew ticket** on, mentors who accept their invite join the crew with a free crew ticket (role "Mentor", not listed publicly). Removing a mentor removes that ticket. Someone already registered for the event keeps their own ticket.

The permission change, mentor profile and new crew ticket are saved together. If linking the account would make an approved volunteer shift overlap a booked office hour, the save or invite acceptance is refused and nothing changes. Cancel the shift place or reschedule the booking, then save or accept the same invite again. A refused invite remains unused.

## Help categories

Under **Mentors** → **Categories**, add what teams can ask for, such as "Cloud" or "Design". For each category you choose:

- **Priority**: higher-priority requests come first in the queue.
- **Skill tags**: only mentors with one of these skills may claim the request (a team's assigned mentor always may).
- **Open per team**: how many waiting requests a team may have in the category (1 to 10).
- **Claim minutes**: how long a mentor holds a request before it returns to the queue (1 to 60; mentors can ask for more time).
- **Expiry minutes**: when an unanswered request closes (15 minutes to 7 days, never after the event ends).

## The live queue

**Mentors** → **Queue** shows every waiting and claimed request and refreshes every 15 seconds. You can **Assign** a request to a mentor on shift, **Release** or **Resolve** a claim, and **Reopen** a resolved request with a reason. A claim a mentor leaves goes back to the queue within a minute.

## Assigning mentors to teams

Under **Mentors** → **Assignments**, choose the scope (the whole event or one mentoring round) and a mode: **Skill match**, **Round robin** or **Random**. **Preview** changes nothing. **Random** shows a seed: the same seed always gives the same result, and **Re-roll** draws again. **Apply** records the run and lists any teams left without a mentor. You can also **Assign by hand**.

Assignments never give a mentor their own team, a team they have a conflict with, or more teams than their limit. Record conflicts under **Mentors** → **Mentors** → **Conflicts of interest**.

## Office hours and rounds

Linking a mentor to a crew place checks their existing commitments too. If an approved volunteer shift overlaps a booked office hour, cancel the shift place or reschedule the booking before linking them. Back-to-back commitments are allowed.

Under **Office hours**, open slots for any mentor inside their availability. Teams book them from their team page and everyone gets a calendar invite. A mentor who also volunteers can't be booked during their approved volunteer shift, and a booked slot can't be moved onto one.

Under **Rounds**, add mentoring rounds: a window, a short check-in form, optional rules that raise help from the answers, and the round's own **Mentor pool** (none chosen means every mentor). Open one round at a time. Mentors can nominate teams after a real visit; nominations appear as a private shortlist on the round and never change judging scores.

## Good to know

- **CSV** exports help requests (without their text or contact details) and assignments.
- Help request text is private to the team, the mentors and the organisers.
- Removing a mentor ends their assignments, hands their claims back to the queue and cancels their future office hours, emailing the booked teams.
