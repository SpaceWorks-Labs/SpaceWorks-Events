If your awards come with prize money, the **Prizes** section of the **Judging** tab keeps track of it: how much each award pays per winner, what you've paid so far, and how. You pay winners yourself, in cash, by UPI, bank transfer or however suits you, and then record the payout here. The app never sends money; it keeps an honest record.

<Callout kind="permission">

Owners and admins of the organisation only.

</Callout>

## Setting the prize for an award

Create your awards and pick winners first. See [Awards and results](/help/hackathons/results).

<Steps>

1. Open the event's **Judging** tab and choose **Prizes**.
2. Each award has its own card. Enter the **Prize per winner**, in the event's currency. Leave it blank for no prize money.
3. Choose **Save prize**.

</Steps>

The amount is per winner, so an award with a 1st and a 2nd place pays each of them the full amount. If places should get different amounts, make a separate award for each place, such as "Overall winner" and "Runner-up".

The top of the section shows the total of all prizes and how much has been paid.

## Recording a payout

Each award card lists its winners with their place, the team or entrant, and what has been paid so far.

<Steps>

1. Choose **Record payout** next to the winner.
2. Check **Paid to**. It starts as the team or entrant name; change it to the person who actually received the money if you like.
3. Check the **Amount**. It starts at what's still owed.
4. Choose **How it was paid**: **Cash**, **UPI**, **Bank transfer**, **Card terminal** or **Other**.
5. Add a **Reference**, such as a transfer or UPI reference, cheque number or receipt, if you have one.
6. Set **Paid on** to the day you paid. It can't be in the future.
7. Add a **Note** if you like, then choose **Record payout**.

</Steps>

You can split a prize over several payouts, for example when a team asks for the money to be shared between members. When the award has a prize set, the total paid to a winner can never be more than it: the app refuses a payout that would go over.

## Fixing a mistake

Payouts can't be edited or deleted. Every payout stays on record, so the history always adds up.

<Steps>

1. Find the payout in the **Payouts** list at the bottom of the section.
2. Choose **Reverse…**.
3. Enter **Why**, then choose **Reverse payout**.

</Steps>

A reversal is recorded beside the original for the same amount, cancelling it out. The original is marked **Reversed** and the new row **Reversal**. Each payout can be reversed once. If the payout itself was right but the details were wrong, reverse it and record it again with the correct details.

## Where payouts show up

- The event's **Finance** tab shows **Prize money paid**, net of reversals, and counts it against the budget. See [The Finance tab and budget](/help/finance/overview-and-budget).
- The Finance tab's **Download CSV** lists every prize payout and reversal with the other money movements.
- If you've connected a chat channel, payouts and reversals can be posted under **Payouts**. See [Chat notifications](/help/organisations/chat-notifications).

## Good to know

- Prize money is never shown on the public results page.
- Taking a winner off an award, or disqualifying them, doesn't remove payouts already recorded. Reverse them if the money comes back.
- Collecting money from entrants is a separate feature. See [Project entry fees](/help/hackathons/entry-fees).
