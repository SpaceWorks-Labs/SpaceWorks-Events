Drafting, releasing and cancelling decisions, and answering or extending offers: owners and admins of the organisation, and co-organisers with **Manage registrations**.

## Drafting and releasing decisions

<Steps>

1. Open **Applications** → **Review and decide**. Filter by stage and status.
2. Open an application to read its answers and its reviews. **Reviews** shows how many count against the stage's target.
3. Tick one or more applications, choose a **Decision** (**Advance to next stage**, **Offer a place**, **Waitlist** or **Decline**) and choose **Draft decision**.
4. When the batch is ready, choose **Release** at the top of the list.

</Steps>

Release checks every decision again and changes nothing unless they all pass:

- **Reviews.** An application needs the stage's number of reviews, on its current answers, from reviewers who are still on the stage's panel and have no conflict. To decide without them (a known speaker, say), give an **Override reason** when drafting.
- **Changes.** If an application changed after you drafted its decision (the applicant withdrew, or edited their answers), draft it again.
- **Offers.** Offers that are open or accepted can't go above **Most offers out at once**, or the event capacity.

**Cancel draft** throws the whole batch away. Nothing is sent.

Applicants can change their answers until the first decision about them is released. Changed answers need fresh reviews; the old reviews are kept but no longer count. The same goes for answers you correct in **Edit registration** on the **Guests** tab, or that a buyer changes on their order page: after the first decision, the answers can't change, though a name can still be corrected.

## Offers

An offer gives the applicant a few days to answer (see **Days to accept an offer** in **Overview**). They are emailed a link to their ticket page, reminded a day before the offer ends, and can:

- **Accept**: a free place is confirmed at once, with their ticket;
- **Accept and pay**: a paid place is held for a day while they pay. It is confirmed only when the payment arrives;
- **Decline**.

On team events, the team leader answers for the team. When the leader pays, the whole team is confirmed.

If someone asks you to answer for them, open their application and choose **Accept for them** (for a paid place, this sends them the payment link), **Decline for them**, or **Extend** to give them more days.

## When an offer ends

An unanswered offer expires at its answer-by time, and the applicant is told. If a paid applicant started paying but didn't finish, their offer stands again until its answer-by time. If their price used a promo code that someone else has used up in the meantime, they can't pay at that price until a use frees up.

## The waitlist

Waitlisted applicants are offered places first in, first offered, whenever a place frees up: an offer declined, withdrawn or expired, or more offers allowed. An application that can't take a place right now (its ticket type is full, or its team has too few members) is passed over and keeps its place in the queue; **Offers and waitlist** shows why.

## Good to know

- Moving an application to the next stage emails the applicant only if you turned that email on.
- The guest list's **Approve**, **Decline** and **Promote** don't work for applications.
- Once you cancel the event, no more places are offered and open offers can't be accepted. Offer and reminder emails still waiting to go out are not sent. Applicants can still decline or withdraw.
- Every release, override and assignment is recorded in the organisation's audit log.
