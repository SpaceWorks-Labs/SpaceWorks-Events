If an organiser asked you to review applications, this is your part. You see only the applications assigned to you, and only what the stage allows.

<Callout kind="permission">

Co-organisers with the **Review applications** permission, and owners and admins of the organisation. You'll be asked for your authenticator code, like every organiser.

</Callout>

## Reviewing an application

<Steps>

1. Open the event from your dashboard. Your review list is shown (organisers find it under **Applications** → **My reviews**).
2. Choose **Open** next to an application marked **To review**.
3. Read the guidance and the answers. On a **blind** stage you won't see names, email addresses, phone numbers or team names.
4. Score each criterion from 1 (weakest) to 5 (strongest), add notes for the organisers if you like, and choose **Submit review**.

</Steps>

A review can't be changed once submitted. If the applicant changes their answers afterwards, the application comes back to your list so you can review the new answers.

## Conflicts of interest

If you know the applicant, or anything else makes a fair review difficult, choose **I have a conflict of interest** instead of scoring. The application goes to someone else on the panel, and you won't be given it again. You're never assigned your own application, or anyone's if you applied to the event yourself.

## Good to know

- You can review only while the stage is **Open** and the application is still in review there.
- Your notes are for the organisers. Applicants never see reviews or scores.
