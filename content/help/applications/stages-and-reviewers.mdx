Stages let you shortlist in steps: a quick first look at everyone, then a closer look at a shortlist, perhaps an interview round after that. Each stage has its own reviewers, its own criteria and its own number of reviews per application.

<Callout kind="permission">

Stages, panels and assignments: owners and admins of the organisation, and co-organisers with **Manage registrations**.

</Callout>

## How stages work

Every application starts in the first stage. Later stages hold only the applications you advance into them, so the work narrows as you go. You can offer a place, waitlist or decline an application at any stage; moving it on is just one more choice.

Each stage has:

- a **Status**: reviewers can review only while a stage is **Open**;
- **Reviews per application**: how many reviews an application needs before you can release a decision about it without giving a reason;
- **Blind review**: reviewers don't see names, email addresses, phone numbers or team names, only the other answers;
- **Fresh eyes** (stage 2 onwards): nobody reviews an application here that they reviewed in an earlier stage. Automatic and manual assignment skip them, and they can't review it. You can change it only while the stage is a draft with nobody assigned;
- **Criteria**: up to 10, each scored from 1 to 5. With none, reviewers give one overall score;
- **Guidance for reviewers**, shown above every application they open.

## Adding a stage

<Steps>

1. Open **Applications** → **Stages**.
2. Choose **Add stage**. It is added at the end, as a draft.
3. Name it (for example "Shortlist"), set its reviews, criteria and blind review, and choose **Save stage**.
4. Set its **Status** to **Open** when reviewers should start.

</Steps>

Only the last stage can be deleted, and only while no application has reached it.

## Choosing a stage's reviewers

First invite reviewers: in **Settings**, invite co-organisers with the **Review applications** permission. That permission opens their review workspace and nothing else.

A stage with no panel is reviewed by everyone with **Review applications**. To use a different group for a stage (say, senior reviewers for the final stage), tick them under **Reviewer panel** and choose **Save panel**. Owners and admins can be on a panel too.

## Assigning reviewers

<Steps>

1. Open **Applications** → **Reviewers** and choose the stage.
2. Set **Reviewers per application**.
3. Choose **Balanced** (even workloads, the same plan every time) or **Random** (even workloads, shuffled).
4. Choose whether to **Keep** existing assignments and add more, or **Start over** (assignments already reviewed always stay).
5. Untick anyone who shouldn't get work this time, then choose **Preview**.
6. Check the workloads and any applications that can't reach the target. With **Random**, choose **Re-roll** for a different shuffle.
7. Choose **Apply**.

</Steps>

A random assignment is drawn from a stored seed, so the same seed always gives the same plan, and every applied run is recorded. If anything changed between the preview and **Apply** (a new application, a reviewer removed), Apply is refused: preview again.

## Conflicts of interest

A reviewer is never assigned an application they have a conflict with:

- their own application, or one from their team;
- any application at all, if they applied to the event themselves;
- one they stepped back from.

A reviewer who opens an application and chooses **I have a conflict of interest** steps back from it. The application goes to the reviewer on the panel with the least work.

You can also add or remove a reviewer for one application by opening it in **Review and decide**.
