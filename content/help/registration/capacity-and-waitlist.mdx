These settings control how places are given out: cap the number of places, let a waitlist form when you're full, approve each person yourself, and choose when sign-ups close.

## Where these settings live

They're all in the **Publishing** card on the **Event page** tab, under **Details**. Change what you need, then choose **Save changes** in the bar that appears at the bottom of the screen.

<Callout kind="permission">

Organisation owners and admins. Co-organisers with the **Registrations** permission can then approve, decline and give places from the **Guests** tab.

</Callout>

## Limiting places

<Steps>

1. In **Publishing**, under **Sign-ups**, turn on **Limit the number of spots**.
2. Enter the number of places.
3. Choose **Save changes**.

</Steps>

With the switch off, places are unlimited. On a team event the limit counts teams and people signing up on their own, not people; see [Setting up team registration](/help/registration/team-registration). You can raise the limit at any time, but not lower it below the number of people who already have a place.

## Ticket types and held places

If you sell tickets, each ticket type can have its own **Limit** too. The event's limit stays the overall cap, and a group ticket takes one place for each person it admits. See [Ticket types, group tickets and add-ons](/help/payments/ticket-types).

While someone pays, their places are **held** and count as taken: for 30 minutes at checkout, or for 24 hours when a place is offered by payment link (after an approval, from the waitlist, or when you add a guest and email them a link). If the time runs out unpaid, nothing is charged and the places go back on sale, or to the next person waiting.

## The waitlist

When every place is taken, the register button on the event page changes to **Join the waitlist**. New sign-ups are emailed that they're on the waitlist and appear under **Waitlist** on the **Guests** tab.

People waiting for a **paid** ticket pay nothing while they wait. When a place opens up for them, they're emailed a payment link that holds it for 24 hours.

Some paid places never wait, and are refused when the event is full: team places and paid solo places on team events (paid up front), passes for a series, tickets reserved to pay at the desk, and guests you add by hand as already paid or complimentary.

People leave the waitlist in three ways:

- **Automatically.** When a place frees up (someone cancels, a hold runs out, you remove a guest or raise the limit), the person who has waited longest gets it within a minute or so: their ticket, or a payment link for a paid one.
- **When you give them a place.** Choose **Give a spot** on their row in the **Guests** tab. This only works while a place is free.
- **When you decline them.** Choose **Decline** from the row's menu. Nothing was charged, so there's nothing to refund.

## Approving each sign-up

Turn on **Approve each sign-up** (under **Who can find it?**) to choose who comes. Everyone who signs up then waits under **To approve** on the **Guests** tab, even when places are left. They're emailed that their request is pending, and the organisation's owners and admins get an email about new requests (at most one every half hour).

<Steps>

1. Open the **Guests** tab and choose the **To approve** filter.
2. Choose **Answers** on a row to read what they told you.
3. Choose **Approve** to give them a place, or **Decline** to turn them down.

</Steps>

Each decision is emailed to the guest. **Re-accept** on a declined guest gives them a place if there's room, or puts them on the waitlist if not.

On paid tickets, people ask first and pay after you approve: **Approve** emails them a payment link that holds their places for 24 hours. Team places are the exception: teams pay first, then wait for approval, and declining a paid team refunds it in full. See [Paid team places](/help/payments/paid-team-places).

With a place limit set, you can only approve while places are free, and nobody is moved off the waitlist automatically: you decide every place. Approval doesn't apply to invite-only events; see [Invite-only events](/help/registration/invitations).

## Closing registration

Under **Close sign-ups**, pick one rule:

| Option | Sign-ups close |
| --- | --- |
| **At the end** | When the event ends. |
| **Before start** | A set time before the start, moving with the start time if you change it. |
| **Set time** | At a date and time you choose, in the event's time zone, on or before the start. |

The line under the options tells you exactly when sign-ups close. Whatever you choose, they always close when the event ends. You can still add guests by hand afterwards; see [The guest list](/help/guests/guest-list#adding-a-guest-yourself).

## Good to know

- **Show how many are going**, at the bottom of **Sign-ups**, shows the number of sign-ups on the event page. Turn it off if a low number early on might put people off.
- Taking an event **Offline** or back to **Draft** stops new sign-ups straight away, but guests keep their places. See [Publishing and visibility](/help/events/publishing).
- Approvals and **Give a spot** only work while the event is live and hasn't ended.
- A guest you add by hand on a free ticket follows the same rules: the waitlist if the event is full, or **To approve** if you approve each sign-up.
- Recurring series set their limit and approval rules on the series, and each date has its own places. See [Recurring series](/help/events/recurring-series).
