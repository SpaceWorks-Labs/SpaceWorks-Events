Your account keeps track of the events you've registered for, the ID cards you hold and the organisations you help run. From **Account settings** you can see all of it, download a copy of your personal data, and step away from organisations you no longer help with.

## Your registrations

The **Registrations** tab lists the events you registered for while signed in, in three sections:

- **Upcoming**: events that haven't ended yet, soonest first.
- **Past**: events that have ended, most recent first.
- **Certificates**: certificates from events where you gave feedback. Choose **View certificate** to download one.

Each registration shows its status (**Confirmed**, **Waitlisted**, **Waiting for approval**, **Payment pending**, **Declined** or **Checked in**) and, depending on the event, buttons such as:

| Button | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **View ticket** | Your ticket with its QR code. See [Your ticket](/help/attending/your-ticket). |
| **Manage team** | Your team page, if you lead a team for an upcoming event. See [Registering as a team](/help/attending/teams). |
| **Project** or **Team project** | Your hackathon project, to draft, submit or edit it. See [Submitting a hackathon project](/help/attending/submitting-a-project). |
| **Event page** | The public event page, while you can still open it. |
| **Cancel registration** | Gives up your place, until you're checked in or the event ends. A paid ticket is cancelled from its order page instead, under the event's refund policy. |

An **Event cancelled** label means the organisers called the event off. Long lists are split into pages of 50.

Registrations you made without signing in aren't listed here, even if you used the same email address. Use the links in their emails instead. For a paid ticket, your order page has your receipt and refund options; see [Your order and receipt](/help/attending/orders-and-receipts).

## Your IDs

The **Your IDs** tab shows the member ID cards organisations have issued you: cards you asked for while signed in, and cards issued to your verified email address. Tilt a card to catch the light, flip it to see the back, or download it to print. A card that isn't approved yet, or has been withdrawn, says so, with a link to the programme. See [Your member ID](/help/attending/member-id).

## Download your data

At the bottom of the **Registrations** tab, **Download my data** gives you a copy of what the app holds about you, as a JSON file you can open in any text editor.

<Steps>

1. Open **Account settings** → **Registrations**.
2. Scroll to **Download my data** and choose **Download my data**.
3. The file downloads straight away, and the app shows "Your download has started."

</Steps>

The file contains:

- your profile: name, email, whether it's verified, when you joined, whether two-step verification is on, and which sign-in methods are linked
- your organisation memberships and roles
- your registrations, with their status, check-in time, team, and your answers to registration questions
- hackathon projects from your registrations, without scores or judges' comments
- feedback you gave that is linked to your registration (for certificates)
- your event co-organiser roles and permissions
- orders you placed, with what you bought, payments and refunds

Passwords, security and recovery codes, and ticket or other private links are never included. Anonymous feedback isn't linked to you, so it isn't in the file either.

<Callout kind="note">

You can download your data up to five times an hour. If you see "You've downloaded your data several times recently", try again in an hour.

</Callout>

## Your organisations

The **Organisations** tab has three cards:

- **Your organisations**: each organisation you belong to, its web address, when you joined, your role, and how many upcoming events it has. **Open dashboard** takes you to it, and **Create organisation** starts a new one.
- **Event co-organiser roles**: events and series where an organisation gave you co-organiser permissions, with the permissions you hold. **Open event** takes you to that event's dashboard.
- **Pending invitations**: co-organiser invitations sent to your email address that you haven't accepted yet. Accept them from the link in the invitation email. You need a verified email to see this list.

## Leave an organisation

<Steps>

1. Open **Account settings** → **Organisations**.
2. Next to the organisation, choose **Leave**.
3. Read the confirmation and choose **Leave organisation**.

</Steps>

You lose access to the organisation's dashboard, events and members straight away. To come back, an owner or admin has to invite you again. Leaving only removes your own membership: the organisation, its events and everyone else are untouched. Any event co-organiser roles you hold are kept, so you can still reach those events, and you still need your authenticator app for them.

<Callout kind="warning">

If you're the organisation's **only owner**, **Leave** is greyed out with "You're the only owner, so you can't leave yet." An organisation can't be left without an owner, and ownership can't be handed to someone else in the app yet. Your options are to stay, or to delete the organisation from [Organisation settings](/help/organisations/settings).

</Callout>

## Deleting your account

You can't delete your account from the app. To have it deleted, contact the person who runs this server. For anything about one event, contact its organiser. Downloading your data first is a good idea.
