Your account settings hold your name and photo, how the app looks for you, and the controls that keep your account safe. Open your account menu (your name or picture in the sidebar) and choose **Account settings**.

The page has five tabs: **Profile**, **Registrations**, **Your IDs**, **Organisations** and **Security**. The header above them shows whether your email is verified, whether two-step verification is on, and when you joined. [Your data and memberships](/help/account/your-data) covers Registrations, Your IDs and Organisations.

## Your profile

The **Profile** card sets how you appear across the app, for example to the other members of your organisation.

<Steps>

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. To use your own photo, choose **Choose profile image** and pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 5 MB. Use **Replace image** or **Remove** to change it later.
3. Edit your **Display name** (up to 120 characters).
4. Choose **Save changes**.

</Steps>

Without an uploaded photo, the app uses the picture from your Google account if you signed in with Google, then your Gravatar if you have one, and otherwise your initials.

## Email address

The **Email** card shows the address your tickets and account emails go to, and whether it's **Verified**. If it says **Not verified**, choose **Resend verification email** and follow the link. You need a verified email to create events and to see invitations sent to your address.

You can't change your email address yourself yet.

## Appearance

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** in the **Appearance** card. **System** follows your device's setting. The choice is saved in this browser only, so set it again on other devices.

## Password

Open the **Security** tab.

- **Change your password**: in **Password**, enter your **Current password**, a **New password** of at least 12 characters, and **Confirm new password**, then choose **Update password**.
- **Add a password**: if you've only ever signed in with Google, the card is called **Add a password**. Enter and confirm a new password and choose **Add a password**, so you can sign in with email and password too.

The new password can't be one that has appeared in a known data breach. Either way, you're signed out on every device and sign in again with the new password, and you get an email confirming the change.

<Callout kind="note">

Password changes need a recent sign-in. If the app says "Sign in again before changing your password" (or, for Google accounts, "Sign in with Google again before adding a password"), sign out, sign back in, and make the change within ten minutes.

</Callout>

Forgotten your current password? Use [Forgotten your password?](/help/account/forgotten-password) instead.

## Two-step verification

The **Two-step verification** card shows **On** or **Off** and lets you set up or turn off an authenticator app. Organisation staff must keep it on. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).

## Sign-in methods

**Sign-in methods** lists the ways into your account: **Email and password** (shown as **Set** or **Not set**) and any linked account such as **Google** (shown as **Connected**). A linked account's own security settings apply when you use it.

## Recent security activity

This card lists your ten most recent security events with the time of each:

- Two-step verification turned on or off
- Password added, changed, or reset by email link
- Signed out everywhere
- Signed in from a new device
- Authenticator code confirmed, after signing in with Google

If you see something you didn't do, change your password and choose **Revoke all sessions** straight away, then turn on two-step verification if it's off.

## Sign out everywhere

Lost a phone, or signed in on a shared computer? **Sign out everywhere** ends every session at once.

<Steps>

1. On the **Security** tab, choose **Revoke all sessions**.
2. Confirm with **Revoke sessions**.

</Steps>

Every browser and device, including the one you're using, is signed out immediately. Like other security changes, this needs a sign-in within the last ten minutes.

## Good to know

- Emails about your account, such as sign-in codes, password changes and new sign-in alerts, always come from the platform itself, never from an organisation you belong to.
- There's no button to delete your account. See [Your data and memberships](/help/account/your-data#deleting-your-account).
