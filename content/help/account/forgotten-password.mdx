If you can't remember your password, the app can email you a link to set a new one. The link works once and only for an hour.

## Reset your password

<Steps>

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password?** above the password field.
2. Enter the **Email** you sign in with and choose **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and follow its link. It opens **Set a new password** for your address.
4. Enter a **New password** of at least 12 characters and choose **Save new password**.
5. The app confirms "Password reset successfully!" and takes you back to sign in. Sign in with the new password.

</Steps>

After you ask for a link, the page always says "If an account exists with that email, we've sent a password reset link." It says this whether or not the address has an account, so the form can't be used to find out who has one.

The new password follows the same rules as when you signed up: 12 to 128 characters, and not one that has appeared in a known data breach. If it has, you see "This password has appeared in a data breach, so it isn't safe to use. Choose a different one." Pick another and save again; the link still works until it expires.

## What a reset changes

- **You're signed out everywhere.** Every other browser and device is signed out, so anyone who had your old password loses access straight away.
- **You get an email about it.** A "Your password was changed" email confirms the change. If you didn't make it, reset your password again at once and tell the person who runs your server.
- **Your email counts as verified.** Following the link proves you own the inbox, so an unverified account becomes verified.
- **Two-step verification stays on.** If you use an authenticator app, you still need a code, or a recovery code, when you next sign in. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).
- **Older links stop working.** Each new request replaces the previous link, so always use the most recent email.

The change appears under **Account settings** → **Security** → **Recent security activity** as "Password reset by email link".

## If the email doesn't arrive

- Emails are sent from a queue, so wait a few minutes, then check your spam or junk folder.
- Make sure you typed the address you **sign in with**. A reset can't be sent to a different address.
- If you signed up with **Continue with Google** and never set a password, there's nothing to reset and no email is sent. Sign in with Google instead. Once signed in, you can add a password from **Account settings** → **Security** (see [Profile, appearance and security](/help/account/profile-and-security#password)).
- Asking many times in a row is limited. If you've tried several times, wait a while and use the latest email that arrives.

If nothing arrives at all, the server may have an email delivery problem. See [Common problems](/help/troubleshooting/common-problems) or ask the person who runs your server.

## If the link doesn't work

"Invalid or expired reset link" means the link is more than an hour old, has already been used, or was replaced by a newer request. Go back to **Forgot password?** and ask for a fresh one.

<Callout kind="tip">

Already signed in and just want a different password? You don't need a reset link. Use **Account settings** → **Security** → **Password** instead.

</Callout>

## Good to know

- A reset doesn't turn off two-step verification. If you've lost your authenticator app as well, see [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification#if-you-lose-your-phone).
- The server's super admin can't reset their password from this page. Their password is set on the server itself.
